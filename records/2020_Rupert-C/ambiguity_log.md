## Record reader

- **Table significance as cell shading.** Tables S2–S5 mark between-group significance only by cell colour, with a key ("Significant vs. Control", "vs. Ctrl and p9 hCF", …) and no P. I wrote one pair per shaded relation with `p: "P < 0.05 (Methods)"`, anchored to the condition's table cell (`kind: table cell`). The colour can't be extracted as text, so the gate can't check which cells are shaded.
- **Tables or figures as units.** Each table is the numeric source of a figure (S2 → Fig. 3a–d, S3 → 3e–h, S4 → 6a–d, S5 → 6f–i). I made each table the unit (`ST2`…`ST5`) and named the figure panels in `where`, rather than having two units for one experiment.
- **Implicit non-significance.** Unshaded or unstarred table cells are not recorded as n.s. pairs, because the paper does not print them. Where the text states "did not differ significantly" (Table S2 stress at 10% and 15%; Table S4 p9 vs control; MCR; vup in Table S3; Figs. 5b, 7a, 7b), I wrote `p: "not significant"`. That wording is mine, so the parser reads it as n.s.
- **Upstroke velocity "did not differ between groups" (Fig. 3f).** I wrote one omnibus item per frequency (7 items), because the statement covers every frequency.
- **hCF passage and fraction.** No field holds passage. I put it as a qualifier, `stromal_cell: {hCFs, passage: p4|p9}` in tissues and `cell_types: {hCFs, passage}` in 2D culture. The fraction goes in `composition` ("5% hCFs"); controls get `stromal_cell: none` and `composition: none`. The hCF donor (63-year-old) is in `donor_age` only for the 2D fibroblast units. In tissue units `donor_age` is the hiPSC donor, which is not stated.
- **Passage p4 in the doping series** comes from the Fig. 2 legend ("+5% p4 hCFs"). Fig. 3–5 legends don't repeat it.
- **Culture stimulation** (4 ms biphasic, 1 Hz, 5 V/cm for the whole culture) is in `other_conditions`, not `maturation_strategy` (the paper does not call it maturation) and not `pacing` (pacing holds the stimulation during measurement).
- **Measurement temperature** is printed twice: 30–34 °C (Methods) and 37 °C (Results). I recorded it with a `suspected_error`. In the optical-mapping temperature (37.0 ± 0.2 °C) the decimal point extracts as a colon, so it carries its own anchor on the extracted form. - **Fig. S3 legend** compares control with "p4 and p9 hCF tissues", groups the figure does not have (it shows Control/5/10/15%). I wrote it as a main effect, hCF groups against control, with a `suspected_error`.
- **Regression slopes (Figs. S3, S5)** are tested but no values are printed. They are recorded as pairs or a main effect with `stated_direction`. The vup-slope pair p9 vs p4 has no stated direction.

## Vocabulary agent

- **'upstroke velocity' is matched by code to the wrong measure.** In this paper it is the contraction upstroke ("the steepest slope of contraction rise", µN/s), but the existing synonym sends it to `measure:upstroke_velocity` (the action potential's dV/dt, voltage_rate). Matches cannot be overridden in `terms.yaml`; the right term is `measure:max_force_rise_rate`. Either the reader writes "contraction upstroke velocity" (or "maximal rate of force rise"), or a person adds a way to override a wrong match. Slope and relation mappings that name it say "contraction upstroke velocity".
- **'day three' (day_of_measurement) flagged *not a context*.** The spec says a number in words is a quantity, but code does not parse "day three" ("one week" parses); no flag kind fits a parse gap. The reader should write "day 3".
- **Stress normalised to control vs the twitch_stress key.** "contractile stress normalized to control" maps to `twitch_stress` with `expressed_as: normalised`, but the existing twitch_stress value key does not require `measure.expressed_as`; relative and absolute stresses could meet. The key should be revised at the next checkpoint (I did not rewrite another paper's key).
- **EC50 is not half-maximal here**: "the extracellular Ca2+ concentration necessary to reach 1.5 times baseline force". Mapped to `measure:ec50` with that response qualifier, which keys on `measure.response` and so stays apart from half-maximal EC50s.
- **Alternans**: three new measures (`alternans_amplitude` with `signal`, `alternans_onset_frequency` with `threshold`); "percentage of tissues exhibiting alternans" went to `arrhythmia_incidence` (outcome alternans), not `constructs_with_feature`, whose scope is structural.
- **Plotted relations without a statistic** ("upstroke velocity against contractile stress", "alternans onset frequency against maximum capture rate") got a new term `measure:between_measure_relation` beside the slope and correlation terms; edits cannot make it their parent.
- **Several measures** (flagged): "tissue compaction and syncytial beating", "cardiomyocyte compaction and local fiber alignment", "GFP+ hCF distribution and vimentin".
- **Judgement calls without synonyms**: preparation "strips" → intact tissue (live halves on a force transducer); pacing "stimulation" → electrical pacing (mode not stated for optical mapping); measurement_medium "perfusate" → extracellular solution (an unnamed bicarbonate saline); "spontaneous rate" → contraction frequency (the phrase is not in the paper's text, so no synonym could be anchored).
- **Cell line**: "Human Episomal Induced Pluripotent Stem Cells" (Thermo Fisher) added as a synonym of CVCL_RM92 (CBiPSC6.2, which lists TMOi001-A), following the earlier mapping of "Gibco human episomal iPSCs". The catalogue number is not printed, so this identification rests on the vendor.
- **αSMA/vimentin**: αSMA intensity normalised by vimentin, then as fold against glass, control or p4 hCFs; recorded as protein "αSMA/vimentin", `expressed_as: fold`, with `normalised_to` left to the values.

## Formula agent

- **"n-fold" read two ways (observation for code).** The modulus folds (3.7, 5.2, 9.1-fold vs control) are written as the stated ratio E_hCF/E_C. Code finds them inconsistent with Table S2 (4.66, 6.21, 10.07); each matches the increase (ratio − 1), which I gave as a labelled alternative. The audit reports only the main formula's verdict, so the alternative's fit is not visible. Elsewhere in the paper (1.6-fold, ST5) "fold" is the plain ratio and is consistent.
- **CSA decrease "25% to 63%"** names no groups. I assigned the low end to 5% hCFs and the high end to 15% hCFs (smallest and largest drops in Table S2). 63% is consistent (62.4%); 25% is inconsistent with 5% hCFs (27.5%). No other pair of table means gives 25%.
- **CSA as an ellipse with h = w/2.** I proposed the derived entry `ellipse_area_half_width` (A = πw²/8, from `elliptical_cross_section_area` with h = w/2). Widths are not printed, so code cannot check any area, stress or modulus derivation. CSA is measured at relaxed length, but active force is measured at 130% Lo. Stress therefore divides force by an area taken at another length; the paper does not say whether that is intended.
- **Modulus as a best-fit slope.** I mapped it to `linear_elastic_stress_strain` (E = σp/ε), but the paper fits a line over 5–30% strain with an unstated intercept. The library has no "slope of a fit" form. I reported the 5–30% range as the paper's statement on *small strain*; no entry gives a numeric limit for code to test it against.
- **Ramp stresses (Tables S3, S5)** are taken to use the same tissues' CSA as Tables S2/S4. That is not stated.
- **Not written:** fraction of beats captured (Fig. 4a) and % of tissues with alternans (Fig. 5b). Both are count ratios with no printed values or counts. The S1A NHDF comparisons print no fold. Alternans onset is a threshold, not a formula. MCR, vup, T50 and T90 are measured directly. The Fig. 3e–h values normalised to 5% hCF at 1 Hz are not in the record. F1 folds and 2^−ΔΔCt folds have inputs only in unread bars, so code cannot check them; qPCR efficiencies are not stated.
- **F6e** normalises stress "to control", but the paper does not say which control value (per-experiment mean or overall).
