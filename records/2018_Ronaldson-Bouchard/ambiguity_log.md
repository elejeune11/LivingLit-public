## Vocabulary agent

- **First paper, empty vocabulary.** Upper levels built from this paper: platform (2D culture / 3D engineered tissue / native heart tissue), preparation (intact tissue, dissociated cells, fixed, RNA extract), substrate (hydrogel > fibrin hydrogel), maturation strategy (electrical stimulation > constant / intensity training), pacing (electrical pacing > current clamp stimulation).
