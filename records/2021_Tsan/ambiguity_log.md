## Record reader

- **Simulations (Supplemental Notes, Supplemental Fig. 3, Fig. 2a).** The finite-element results (11.2% FS and 11.4 µN at the experimental buffer, 12.1 to 11.1 µN across buffers, 5.48 µN at 5% FS) are model outputs, not measurements of cells. Every cell-culture field would resolve to the paper's culture context, which would be wrong for a model. I left them out as items. Claim C07 ("high-fidelity") depends on them, so its presence is judged *overstated*. The schema has no group kind for a computational model.
- **Single-cell yield (TXT1).** The text calls it "our prior work", so the single-cell group is `kind: other study` citing [11]. In Fig. 2g, TXT2 and Fig. 1f, single micropatterned cells are compared with no such wording, so I kept them as engineered groups. Refs 11 and 37 are the same paper (Ufford et al. 2021), listed twice. I cite it as c11.
- **Contrast field for Supplemental Fig. 1 (isolated vs in-contact cells).** No field holds "cell contact". I put it in `platform` ("isolated hPSC-CM" vs "hPSC-CMs in contact"). The vocabulary agent may flag it.
- **2DMB vs monolayer group contexts.** Monolayer medium, calcium, coating, formation day and load are not stated for most comparisons ("standard hPSC-CMs from the same batches"). I wrote `not stated` rather than copy the 2DMB values, so code will label most 2DMB–monolayer pairs as confounds. `mechanical_load: auxotonic` is given to 2DMBs only, because the paper contrasts their auxotonic contraction with tethered monolayers. Substrate 8 kPa PDMS is given to monolayers only where a legend or the text says so (Figs. 1, 2e, 4a/c).
- **Purity.** "All selected 2DMBs were 100% pure cardiomyocytes" is a selection criterion confirmed by imaging, not the purity of a culture. I wrote `purity: not stated` except for the RNA-seq units (">95%").
- **Day of formation.** The Fig. 1b legend says "Day-25 hPSC-CMs were replated". The Results and Methods say day 24. I used "day 24" and flag the difference here, not on an item.
- **Fig. 3i bracket.** The lower "p<0.0001" bracket is drawn over the Monolayer Day 10 and 2DMB Day 3 columns. The legend (unpaired, 2DMB vs standard) and the text (day 3) say 2DMB day 3 vs monolayer day 3, so I wrote that pair.
- **Fig. 3g/3h and 3a.** The legends say "two-sided P value shown", but 3g and 3h print no P, and 3a prints only asterisks with no threshold. For 3g I used `stated_direction` (down) from the text. The 3h mean comparison ("similar") has `stated_direction: none`. The SD comparison (73% less, P < 0.0001) is its own item.
- **Text "a ± b" with spread named only for error bars.** Where the legend says the error bars are SD (or SEM in Fig. 3e), I applied it to the matching text value with `spread_source: legend`. For Fig. 1f (box plot) and Fig. 2e, the ± is left `not stated`. In Fig. 2e the legend's SD sentence sits after panel g.
- **Fig. 6h.** "20 ± 0.2% increase, P < 0.0001" may be OxPhosT3 against its own baseline, while the figure's p<0.0001 is between groups. I kept both on one item (text P first, figure P in `p_values`) and logged a suspected unit slip in "± 0.2%".
- **Fig. 6b n.** The legend says N = 6, "triplicates for two lines", but the source data hold 12 values per condition. This is logged as a suspected error. The test is not named.
- **Fig. 5e asterisks.** One asterisk per gene, while the legend covers "2DMB or 3DhCO vs. monolayers". I wrote a pair item for each comparison, both with `*P < 0.01`. The text's DESeq2 fold changes and adjusted P for the same genes (2DMB vs standard) are separate items, because the test differs. "sixfold" is kept as printed.
- **3DhCO and adult heart (Mills et al.).** Both are `kind: other study` citing [27]. The adult heart is native tissue (`platform: native tissue`), but I chose `other study` so it can carry the citation.
- **Heatmaps (Fig. 5a, 5c, 5d) and GO bars (5b).** These are one measurement item per gene or category, covering all groups, with no printed values (they are in the source data). Some heatmap columns are labelled "M2DCT" in the source data and Fig. 1g. I treated that as the 2DMB.

Fig. 8j percents (27/51/63% up, 20/20/17% down, 44% down) are image text, stored as `between` values on the items.
- **Fig. 7d/e controls.** The 1.45 mM group is the labelled control, so pairs put it as the reference. The 0.4 mM group also differs in medium (RPMI/B27 vs RPMI:DMEM), and code will see this as aliased.
- **F6a (mass spectrometry)** re-analyses cells from ref. 14. Differentiation, hiPSC medium, Wnt days and purification are written `by_reference` to [14]. The F8 parental line ("isogenic line ... 14") is also `by_reference`.
- **Suspected errors outside items.** Ref. 2 (Burridge, Nat. Methods 11) is printed with year 2020. ## Formula agent

- **~900-fold analysis time (S4_time) left out.** The inputs are "~45 min per beat per cell" and "~3 seconds per beat per 2DMB". The unit parser reads neither unit, so code would compute 45/3 = 15. The audit then crashes in `chains.evaluate_derivation`: `float(m)` fails on the "~900" that `judge_rebuilt` is handed after an inconsistent verdict. Observations for code: (1) compound units like "min per beat per cell" need parsing; (2) the constant-factor test should strip `~`. By hand, 45 min / 3 s = 900.
- **Fig. 8j percentages (F8c–i).** These are computed from released group means as (KO − control)/control. Code finds 24/49/62% up against the printed 27/51/63%, and 16/17/19% down against 20/20/17%. Only the deceleration time (44%) matches. The legend calls the velocities "normalized" without saying what they are normalized to. The released cells are in µm/s, so the printed summary may come from differently normalized values, or from per-pair or median summaries. The paper does not say.
- **DESeq2 fold changes (F5e_*_deseq).** These are mapped to `ratio_from_log2_values` with the released replicate log2 values (Fig. 5e). That gives a ratio of geometric means: 9.7 (printed 12), 150 (printed 118) and 5.5 (printed 4.3). "sixfold" cannot be parsed. DESeq2's own GLM estimate is the labelled alternative (by reference, c44). Read the inconsistency as "not reproducible from released replicates", not as an arithmetic error.
- **Fractional shortening = Δlength/length** is stated only in the Fig. 2d legend. Per-2DMB lengths are not released, so all FS derivations are *cannot check*. I wrote derivations with outputs for the items that print a value (F2e, F6e, F8b) and formula-only derivations for the Fig. 4 and Supplemental Fig. 5 items.
- **CV inputs.** CV = SD/mean is built from the text's "mean ± x". For Fig. 2e that ± is not named (note on the item), and I read it as the SD. The released Fig. 2e points give 0.209 and 1.567, against the printed 0.21 and 1.58.
- **Traction force per width (TXT2).** The force comes from a finite-element look-up table (no library entry; not printed per 2DMB). The width is the pattern width, 45 µm (Methods), with 44 µm (Supplemental Notes) as an alternative. The Supplemental Fig. 3 example (5% FS → 5.48 µN, i.e. 122–125 µN/mm) is a model output, so it is not used as an input.
- **Power analyses (TXT3/TXT4).** No formula is stated, so these are no_formula. A two-sample normal formula, 2(z₀.₉₇₅ + z₀.₉)²(CV/0.2)², gives 23 for 2DMB FS (CV 0.21), matching the printed 23. The same formula does not reproduce 205, 148 or 12, so I made no proposal.
- **Fig. 3h SD reduction (73%)** is consistent from the printed SDs. The released monolayer amplitudes give SD 1.39, not 1.06 (also logged by the data reader).
- **Not written:** Fig. 3i (the ratio Cx43 area / SiR-actin area appears only as an axis label, not in findable text), Fig. 7d/e intensities (mask co-localization, not a formula), and the APA, RMP and dV/dt measurements. APD90 and calcium-transient amplitude are no_formula (definition not stated).
- **Proposals:** `part_of_whole` (f = a/(a+b), for cTnI/(cTnI+ssTnI) and TNNI3/(TNNI3+TNNI1)), `complement_fraction` (c = 1 − f, for adult TNNT2 variant = 1 − PSI), and `relative_change_from_ratio` (d = r − 1, for the "20% increase" from per-2DMB fold changes). All three are derived from accepted definitions, so no assumption reports are needed. No derivation uses a model.

## Vocabulary agent (key checkpoint)

Re-map checkpoint after 10 papers (03-vocabulary, Earlier papers; 05-across-sources, Standing keys), after the independent check of release v5-rebuild-10. 11 standing_key edits revise the keys of the measures this paper first added.

- Value keys now require treatment, genotype and disease_model for every measure whose value a drug, variant or disease can change. Left unchanged: method, device, design and yield measures (analysis time, required sample size, micropattern yield, usable constructs, post spring constant, applied load, strip bending force, between-sample variability, identified protein count, PCA variance, medium composition, signal-to-noise, embryoid body diameter, cardiomyocyte yield, beating onset).
- Direction keys of force and beat-rate measures now require pacing (paced against spontaneous), so a spontaneous and a paced isoprenaline response do not meet.
- Guess: kinetic times (twitch duration, relaxation and calcium decay times) are also rate dependent, but their direction keys were not given pacing; the request named rate and force only.
- Concern for a person: genotype and disease_model are 'not stated' for most healthy lines, and an unstated must-match field blocks a value comparison. A dry run of the revised keys leaves 2 cross-paper value agree/differ verdicts of 104 (44 with treatment alone). The relaxation report will name genotype; whether an unstated genotype of a healthy line should count as wild type is a design decision.
