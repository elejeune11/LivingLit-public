## Record reader

- **Image-only figures and tables.** Every figure in main.pdf and mmc1.pdf is a raster image, and so are the S6a/S6b/S7b/S12c tables. I read their printed numbers (heat-map RQ values, µ/SD boxes, adjusted P tables) from renders and recorded them as printed values. Their item anchors quote the nearest legend sentence; S6 table anchors are `kind: unit`. The figures reader should check the transcriptions of S6a (112 P values) and S6biii (56 P values).
- **Adjusted P written as `P = 0.5641` / `P < 0.0001`.** The tables print `0.5641`, `<0.0001` and `>0.9999` with no "P". I added "P =" / "P <" so code can parse them, and I kept the printed digits. S6biii also prints a summary (`ns`, `****`), which I put in each item's note.
- **Dot and star positions read from images.** Fig. 2H dots (black, blue or red; 1–3 dots), Fig. 5D black stars above d60 (the legend defines black stars only as CMM vs d30), and the Fig. 7E/7F comparison lines (which groups each line joins) are my reading of the image. Fig. 7D has one `***` on the d30→endo+d30 arrow, and I recorded it for both OCR and ECAR.
- **Text says "significantly" but the figure has no mark** (Fig. 4B OCR; S12c time to peak and MDR). I used `significant, threshold not stated`. Where the text says "no statistical difference" (S12c MDP and APA), I wrote `n.s.`.
- **Tests not named in a legend** got the Methods default "Students t-test was performed unless specified", and thresholds from the Methods carry "(Methods)". Legend thresholds whose dots or asterisks were lost in extraction ("p < 0.001(.)") are written as `***p < 0.001 (legend; symbol lost in extraction)`.
- **CMM is a composite.** CMM = anisotropic nanowrinkles + polyacrylamide at 23 kPa + GRGDS/GFPGER/nephronectin. I split it over `platform` (anisotropic nanowrinkled surface), `substrate` (polyacrylamide, stiffness 23 kPa) and `replating_coating`. So d30 vs CMM differs in three fields: code will call it a confound, which is what the design is. I left `maturation_strategy` as *not stated* rather than repeat the platform's words. Where the paper says only "matrix" (Fig. 2H/2I, "d30+matrix"), the coating is written `matrix`, not the three ligands.
- **Substrate of the non-CMM surfaces in Fig. 2H/2I/S6** (d30+matrix, PLGA, PU) is not stated. ANW ("CMM without matrix") is given polyacrylamide, the same as CMM.
- **cm_day_at_formation.** The general Methods say cells were plated at d13–d14. The Fig. 2I Methods say d11 for that experiment. S6b is "related to Figure 2" but not stated, so I left it as *not stated*.
- **Seeding density.** The iPSC seeding density (125,000 cells per cm2) and the imaging replating density (200,000 per cm2) are different culture steps. Only the imaging units carry a value; everything else is *not stated*.
- **H2O2 challenge.** In Fig. 4G it is `treatment: H2O2` (1 mM per the text; the Fig. 4F legend says 100 µM, logged as a suspected error on the context value). In Fig. 7G the treatment sets are written as `H2O2` and `ET-1 + H2O2`, so each set is complete, but the ET-1 dose qualifiers are not repeated there.
- **ET-1 groups** carry `disease_model: pathological hypertrophy` (Fig. 7A: "acute pathological hypertrophy model") as well as `treatment: ET-1`. Treatment and disease model are linked fields at a checkpoint; I wrote both because the paper names the model.
- **Adult heart group in Fig. 1M** is `other study`. Its citation key points to the four published datasets (Choy, Dias, He, Lewandowski) as one citation entry, because a group takes one citation.
- **Same data printed twice in different units.** Fig. 3S/T (1 Hz) and Fig. S10k/l (1 Hz) are the same TFM data, but they sit in different units, so they cannot be suffix pairs in one item. Fig. 7L control values equal Fig. S12e exactly (logged as a suspected error). S7b line 1 equals S6bii CMM. There is no record-level way to link items across units.
- **Model and analysis outputs left out:** the PCA (Fig. 1L, S2), hive plots (2A–B, 3A), IPA upstream regulators and pathways (2C, 7B–C), the Steiner-tree networks (2D–G, S5), the hierarchical clusters (1N–O, S4, mmc2.xlsx), the HCM and AngII dataset comparisons (S13–S14), the integrin expression of published datasets (1A), and the line plots of S5a. Text-stated RNA-seq directions are recorded as stated-direction items (F1jk, F3ab).
- **Not recorded as items:** qualitative imaging (TEM, Airyscan, RYR2 localisation), the dofetilide 10 nM intolerance, EADs, and PIV vector orientations.
- **Contractility (Fig. 3T, S10l)** has no unit printed. I recorded unit `not stated`.

## Vocabulary agent

Vocabulary version 67, instructions `terms_v4`. 49 edits, 106 mappings, 3 flags, 4 split requests.

**Flags back to the record reader**
- `wnt_activation_days` "d0-d2" and `wnt_inhibition_days` "d4-d5": these are one context each, but code cannot read the paper's "dN-dM" notation as a quantity. "d13-d14" parses only because a digit follows a digit. No flag kind fits a quantity printed in an unreadable form, so I used *not a context* and asked the reader to write "days 0-2" and "days 4-5". The spec could add a flag kind (or a parser rule) for this.
- `replating_coating` "matrix" (Fig. 2H "Ligands is flat surface coated with matrix"): flagged *placeholder*. The word only refers back to the CMM ligands. Filling in GRGDS/GFPGER/nephronectin would be inference.

**Split requests**: "PLGA" and "PU" from two `platform` values go to `substrate`. "at 1 Hz" in measure "APD90 at 1 Hz" goes to `pacing`, which is *not stated* for unit F7hk.

**Judgement calls**
- "IWR" is mapped to `differentiation_factors:iwr_1` because the Key Resources table names IWR-1 (Selleck S7086). I added no synonym "IWR", because the bare name could stand for IWR-1-endo/exo in other papers.
- "lipid-enhanced Cellastium S" (sic; the product is Cellastim S) is mapped to CHEBI:760525 (recombinant rice-derived human albumin) with `no_ontology_term`, because ChEBI has no lipid-enriched form. "B27 with insulin" is B27 with qualifier insulin "with insulin", not a separate insulin addition.
- "capillary force lithography PU fibers": the legend says fibers and the text says "CFL-based PUA substrates". Whether they are grooves or fibres is not stated, so I mapped to the broader `platform:culture_2d` with a format qualifier, not to `grooved_surface` or `electrospun_fibrous_scaffold`.
- New `platform:nanowrinkled_surface` under `culture_2d`, as a sibling of `grooved_surface`. Nanowrinkles are moulded nanotopography, not scored or embossed micro-grooves. Its synonym "ANW" is this paper's abbreviation.
- "contractility" (TFM, Fig. 3T/S10L) is printed with no quantity or unit (values about 1e-7). I added `measure:contractility_not_stated` with dimension "not stated", following the existing "…, not stated" terms. I did not guess force or stress.
- "MYL2 band spacing" is mapped to `sarcomere_length` with a new qualifier `marker: MYL2` (peak-to-peak MYL2 band distance along sarcomeric strips). The key of `sarcomere_length` does not require `measure.marker`, so it compares with Z-line sarcomere length. I think that is intended.
- Respiration of permeabilised cells: new `measure:substrate_driven_respiration` under `oxygen_consumption_rate`, with qualifiers `substrate` and `respiratory_state`. It covers complex I/II and the Fig. 7E FAO state 3/4 values. Fig. 4E "palmitoyl-CoA oxidation (FAO)" has no state, so it goes to the existing `fatty_acid_oxidation_rate`. As a result the same paper's FAO values fall under two measures.
- "% of control" (APD50/APD90, beat frequency, Fig. 6N/S12H) is mapped as `expressed_as: normalised`, `normalised_to: control`, not "% of baseline". The control is a separate DMSO group, not the same cells before the drug.
- "adult-like gene expression (%)" has a new `measure:transcriptomic_maturation_index` (projection on the PC maturation vector). `expressed_as` is left to the printed unit.
- "gene set enrichment (z-score)" maps to `gene_set_distribution_shift` (t-test of genes inside vs outside each set).
- "Grx1-roGFP2 ratio … at 60 s": new `measure:glutathione_redox_state`. "at 60 s" is already the unit's `time_point`, so there is no split.
- "detached cells" (TrypLE-detached for flow cytometry) maps to `preparation:dissociated_cells`. That term's definition says "from a tissue", and these cells came off a 2D substrate.
- mtDNA minor/major arc: I added qualifier `region` on `mitochondrial_dna_content`. Its existing standing key does not require `measure.region` (or `measure.gene`), so minor- and major-arc values would be compared. The key should be tightened at the next re-map checkpoint.

**Values the reader may have recorded wrongly (not changed)**
- `measurement_medium` "KCl buffer" is the reader's own name. The paper says "Buffer (pH 7.2) containing 137 mM KCl", so no synonym could be anchored.
- `preparation` "RNA isolated" is not printed as such ("RNA was isolated"), and "detached cells" is not either ("Cells were detached"). Both were mapped without synonyms.

## Formula agent

Library version 33, instructions `formulas_v3`. 82 derivations (74 with a library formula, 8 `no_formula`), 7 bounds, 2 assumption reports, no proposals.

**Judgement calls**
- **RQ (Fig. 2I) as a ratio, not 2^-ΔΔCt.** The methods name "relative quantification (RQ)" and "normalized to endogenous GAPDH", with d30 as the control condition. They do not name the comparative Ct method. I mapped each RQ to `ratio_def` (GAPDH-normalised expression of the group over d30) and put 2^-ΔΔCt as a labelled alternative. The d30 cells (1.00) map to `normalized_to_own_control`.
- **S6b fold of d30.** No words say "fold of d30": the d30 value is printed as 1 and the table unit is "fold change". The mtDNA/nDNA step is by citation (Phillips 2014), so it is a `by_reference` input. The d30 value of 1 is printed with ±0.212 / ±0.19, which fits normalising to the d30 mean rather than each sample to itself.
- **"Coupled (oligomycin-sensitive) respiration" (Fig. 4B)** is mapped to `mito_stress_test_partition` (atp = basal − oligomycin rate), with basal taken from item F4b_basal. The oligomycin rate is not printed, so the chain cannot be rebuilt, and none of the model's three assumptions is reported.
- **Grx1-roGFP2 ratio:** the methods give 405/488, the legend and Results 400/485. I wrote the methods' pixel-by-pixel ratio. The record notes F4g/F7g printed values as "ΔF/F" in the figure, which is a label slip (the values are ratios).
- **RCR (Fig. 7F)** has no printed values, so the derivations have no output. For cmm_et1, no item plots its state-4 rate, so that input is not stated.
- **"Twice" (S10 duration) and "~40 %" (S12h)** are outputs whose printed words code may not parse as numbers. Both are "cannot check" anyway, because their inputs are bar heights that were not read.

**Not written**
- APD as "% of Control" (Fig. 6N) is printed only on the figure axis, so no text anchor exists. Its values are not printed or read.
- ΔF/F amplitudes (F6g, S12b, F7h, F7j): no F0 or F is printed, and the record has no printed output. S12b has bar readings of the output itself only. % increase after caffeine (F6i) also has no printed values.
- Immunoblot "relative quantification to GAPDH" (F3j–m, F4i, F5i, F6e): no printed values.
- F1m adult-like % (a projection on the PCA maturation vector): this is an analysis model, not closed-form.
- F1h ligand percentages sum to 100.00. A sum check would need a printed total, so only `fraction_bounds` is applied.

**Observations for code**
- The audit applies `constant_scaling_t` to F4b_coupled (a difference, basal − o). For a difference the relevant entry is `constant_shift_t` when o is constant; with o per sample neither applies.
- S7b line 1 (2.9 ± 0.36 minor, 3.2 ± 0.49 major) equals S6b CMM exactly (already logged by the reader). A derivation cannot link the two units.
- The F5d_text ">2-fold versus d60" is flagged by the reader as a suspected error (Fig. 5D bars about 1.3-fold). There are no readings of Fig. 5D, so code cannot check it.
