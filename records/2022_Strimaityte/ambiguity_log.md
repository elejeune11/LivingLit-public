## Vocabulary agent

- **iCMF as a synonym of `platform:muscle_bundle_2d`.** An iCMF (6−10 hiPSC-CMs end to end on a 10 × 700 μm stripe of a 10 kPa hydrogel) fits that term's definition (about 6–12 cells on one narrow rectangular island, contracting uniaxially). I added a synonym rather than a new term. If the re-map finds the two formats differ in island size in a way a comparison cares about, a child term under `micropatterned_cell_patch` would be the alternative.

## Formula agent

- **No input values anywhere.** Fig. 4 (violins), Fig. 5d/f (interval bars), Fig. 6 and Fig. S6 (bars) were not read and print no numbers, so all 72 derivations are *cannot check*. They record the method, the parameter kinds and the 2^-ΔΔCt assumptions (both *not stated*).

I read the reference as the period 1/f and proposed `relative_deviation_from_period` (d = x·f − 1, derived from `relative_change` + `frequency_def`). - **Code's F5e verdict.** "over 100" (rc 5 Hz) is judged *inconsistent* against a read mean of 127.4 %. 127 is over 100. This looks like how code reads an "over" bound, not a paper error. It is for code or the record reader to check, not something from the formula job.
