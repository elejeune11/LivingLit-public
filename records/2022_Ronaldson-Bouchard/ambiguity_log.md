## Record reader

- **A multi-organ paper read against a cardiomyocyte field set.** Heart, liver, bone, skin, endothelium and monocyte tissues all get units. The CM-only fields (differentiation, Wnt days, insulin day, purity, replating coating, cm_day_at_formation, cm_medium, supplements) are `not applicable` for the non-heart tissues. For heart tissues they are *by reference* to [38]. The chosen sentence is "Heart muscle was formed and matured as described previously11,38". The record holds no tissue-specific medium (HCM, osteogenic, epidermalization, EGM-2) for non-heart tissues, because `cm_medium` is scoped to cardiomyocytes. A generic "tissue medium" field would hold them.
- **Chip configuration is `platform`.** "Multi-organ tissue chip", "Mixed tissue chip" and "Isolated" are platform values. The 4-tissue/1-tissue configuration is a qualifier. The real difference between Multi-organ and Mixed is the endothelial barrier. I did not also write it as `endothelial_cell`, because that would make the field aliased with platform. Methods say the Isolated condition was run "with or without endothelial barrier", and no legend says which Isolated arm a panel shows.
- **Chambers and compartments are not fields.** Fig. 6, Supplementary Figs. 7–9, Fig. 4c and Fig. 7h have groups that differ only by the chamber or compartment sampled. Each of these groups carries an `other_conditions` entry and shares the unit's context. Their pairs (Fig. 4c, Fig. 7h) differ only in day or treatment. A `location` / `compartment` field would hold the chamber.
- **Tissue as a group factor.** In Fig. 3f,g, 3i,j and 3l the groups are tissues (skin, bone, heart, liver), so cell_types, substrate, stromal cell, seeding density and so on differ within one unit. Code will see these as confounds. Per the Methods, every tissue in Fig. 3e–l came from the WTC-11 line, so cell_line is WTC-11 for all of them there. The liver, bone and skin lines are otherwise `not stated`.
- **Pairs read from brackets.** Main Figs. 3, 4 and 7 and Extended Data Fig. 3 are vector drawings, so I took the bracket ends from the drawing objects. Fig. 3f, for example, is heart vs skin 0.0160, heart vs bone 0.0141 and heart vs liver 0.0202. Extended Data Figs. 4, 5, 6 and 8 are raster, and their pairs were read from the page image at page resolution. Each such item says so in a note. The two day-14 labels in ED Fig. 5d ('<0.0001', '0.0450') could not be assigned and are left out.
- **ED Fig. 3c (force vs calcium).** Four '<0.0001' labels are printed. In the vector drawing, each bracket line is centred over one bar rather than spanning two, so the compared pairs cannot be told. I recorded one item over the series (`test_of: omnibus`, legend test "unpaired t-test") and noted this. It is not a true omnibus.
- **ED Fig. 5d within-condition tests.** Classical vs intermediate vs non-classical within one condition compares subsets of the same samples, so those tests are not contrasts. They are mentioned in notes only. Cross-condition pairs for the same subset are items.
- **Asterisks in Fig. 4g** (** and ***) have no key. I wrote "**P < 0.05 (Methods)" and "***P < 0.05 (Methods)" from the Methods' paper-wide threshold. The extra stars imply stricter thresholds that are not stated.
- **Printed P format.** Figure labels are bare numbers ("0.0076"). Table pval cells are bare ("2.70e-03"). I wrote them as "P = 0.0076 (figure label)" and "P = 2.70e-03 (table pval)" so code can parse them. One table cell prints "1. 1e-03" or "1. 1e-05" with a space; I wrote it as 1.1e-03 / 1.1e-05.
- **Image tables transcribed.** Supplementary Figs. 10–12 (NES, FC, pval per miRNA), the Supplementary Fig. 2c IPA table, the NES values in Supplementary Figs. 2d,e and 3, and the EC50 values in Supplementary Fig. 4c are images. I transcribed them by eye from the page render, and the anchors are legend quotes. The figures-and-data reader should spot-check them against Source Data where it overlaps: the 7L sheet holds NES for S10–S11 and FC for S12c. I dropped the last S2c row (VSIG4), which is cut off in the image.
- **miRNA groups.** "Doxorubicin vs control" within one configuration is the pair. The table FC is a `between` value, and NES is written as the statistic. The authors inverted the NES sign (Methods), which is noted only here. Clinical studies [16] and [19] are `other study` groups. Counts of consistent miRNAs (10/17, 5/17, 12 vs 9, 9/17, 4/17) are `between` the configuration and the paediatric clinical group.
- **Fig. 7d.** The legend calls it "cardiac contractility", the axis says "Beat frequency (bpm)", and the text says "cardiac excitability". I used the axis measure and logged a cross-reference.
- **Fig. 7l PCA.** The test is not stated. A *significant* P is read by the authors as similarity to the clinical cluster. I recorded the pair as condition vs clinical with that note.
- **Fig. 6 reservoir.** The Source Data (MOESM6, 6A) list 32481.33, 28610.05 and 23489.02 ng ml−1 at 1 h. The plotted bar sits within a 0–4,000 axis. This is logged as a suspected error, not corrected. The PK model curves (Fig. 6, Supplementary Fig. 9, Extended Data Fig. 10) are model outputs and are left out. Supplementary Table 3 (model parameters with their sources) and Supplementary Table 4 (model terms) are also left out.
- **Tables as units.** Supplementary Tables 1 and 2 hold specifications (cell numbers, dimensions, media components), not measurements. They are used only as context anchors (`kind: unit`) for seeding densities, and are not units. Supplementary Table 2 lists B27 components (including insulin and T3) under the cardiac medium. I kept supplements as the Methods' wording (B-27 supplement + ascorbic acid + penicillin/streptomycin + aprotinin) rather than expanding B27.
- **Not itemised.** These have no numbers or tests to record:
  - GO/KEGG/PGSEA term lists with FDRs (Supplementary Figs. 4a,b and 5; Extended Data Figs. 7d–f and 8f)
  - heat maps (Figs. 5c, 5d; Extended Data Fig. 8e)
  - volcano plots (Supplementary Fig. 2b; Extended Data Figs. 7a–c and 8d)
  - the Extended Data Fig. 8c PCA (PC1 72.26%, PC2 7.34%)
  - immunostaining and histology panels
  - the Fig. 5a Venn intersections other than the per-tissue unique counts
  - the Fig. 3e flow quadrants beyond those recorded
  - the ED Fig. 1h computational shear model

  Claims resting on heat maps or images have empty `rests_on`, with support "cannot judge".
- **Context values from figure text.** Some values come only from outlined or raster figure text: ED3c calcium levels, ED3e "2D monolayer"/"3D tissue", ED3g time points, ED5c platform labels, ED5d/ED6 days, ED4e and Supplementary Figs. 7/8 time points, and Supplementary Fig. 4c drug names. Their context anchors are `kind: unit`, with the quote transcribed from the figure.
- **Day vs time point.** Days in the chip (Fig. 4 "Day 0/14/28", ED Figs. 5–6) are `day_of_measurement`, counted from integration in the chip, not from differentiation. Hours after a drug or injury are `time_point`. "Day 1/Day 6" in Fig. 3m are `time_point`: they are days after cryoinjury, although the text says 1 week and the legend of 3n–p says 7 d.
- **Fields left `not stated`:**
  - `number_of_lines`. The text says "hiPSC from a single donor", but in a sentence whose scope (which experiments) is unclear.
  - genotype, disease_model, pacing, temperature, measurement medium. ED3b/ED8 excitation threshold and capture rate imply field stimulation, but pacing is not stated as a condition.
- **Cross-references and citations.** The ED Fig. 5d citation for "Over 4 weeks" shows two weeks. The text cites ref 17 for the paediatric miRNA set where ref 16 (Oatmen) is meant; I did not log this as a cross-reference because it is a citation, not a panel. Fig. 4f/4g source data hold 3 values per Mixed/Isolated group while the legend prints n = 6; this is noted on F4f_trap for the figures-and-data reader.

## Formula agent

- **TEER as a product, mapped through `ratio_def`.** No entry states "product of two quantities", so `TEER = R*A` is mapped to `ratio_def` as `R = TEER/A`, which is the same equation. I did not propose a new TEER definition because the field is already above its target for given entries. For skin the methods name the multiplication. For endothelial barriers they only say "from the measured resistance and the effective barrier area", so the barrier derivations are `formula: not stated`, with the product and a blank-subtracted product as labelled alternatives. Whether a blank (cell-free insert) was subtracted is not stated.
- **Proposal `apparent_permeability` (model).** It covers the paper's `Pe = Ct·V/(C0·A·Δt)` and cites the Hubatsch/Artursson 2007 Nature Protocols paper by URL only. I quoted no text, because I could not verify the exact wording. The paper reports none of its three assumptions (sink conditions, constant donor concentration, linear accumulation). Which Δt enters the formula is not stated: samples were taken at 0–48 h. V, A and C0 are not printed for this assay.
- **Percent outputs written as fractions.** The specificity is printed as `TN/(FP+TN) × 100`. I wrote it without the ×100, because tab 4 treats a % output as a fraction ×100. The TN/FP tallies are not printed: 81.8 % fits 9/11 and 40 % fits 2/5, but that is a guess and I did not record it.
- **The denominators 17 and 7 inflate the printed-precision interval.** They are exact counts, but code gives them a ±0.5 interval, so the "k out of 17 (p %)" checks are weaker than they need to be. The ratios rebuild exactly anyway: 10/17 = 58.8, 5/17 = 29.4, 9/17 = 52.9, 4/17 = 23.5, 6/7 = 85.7, 2/7 = 28.6, 5/7 = 71.4. The "Mixed 1-tissue: 5/7" line of the Fig. 7m summary box is not in the extracted text, so its denominator is anchored on the line above.
- **Fig. 5b overlap.** The "total number of proteins" could be the engineered set, the adult set or their union. These are labelled alternatives; no counts are printed, so nothing can be checked.
- **Per-miRNA fold-change tables (S10–S12, about 130 items) are not written as derivations.** They are signed linear fold changes from the array software, and no inputs are released. Observation for code: under a signed-FC convention, |FC| ≥ 1. S10c miR-181a-5p (0.72) and S12c miR-150-5p (0.99) break that, so either the convention is mixed or these values are not signed FCs. There is no library bound to flag this.
- **ED3c "force" in mN/mm²** is per area (a stress), but the method is given only by citation (ref38), so it gets `no_formula`. IVIS radiant efficiency (a sum over ROI pixels), the S4c EC50 curve fits and the ED4e "relative % of total" (named only in an image axis) also get no formula. Flow-cytometry gate percentages (ED5c/d) are not written as derivations; their printed values get `fraction_bounds` only.
