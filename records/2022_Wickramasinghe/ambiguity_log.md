## Record reader

- **Two copies of the main text.** `journal_version.pdf` (with STAR Methods) and `nihms-1982259.pdf` (author manuscript) carry the same text. The journal PDF loses μ (as "m"), ≤ (as "%") and minus signs ("SIRPA+CD90 ", "p = 3.42E 1"), so I worded values and quotes from the manuscript (e.g. "5 μM", "p = 3.42E−1"). Both are in `source.files`; quotes match in either.
- **Supplementary xlsx files.** Table S1 and Table S2 are gene-level lists (per-gene log2 fold changes; Venn membership flags), with no group values, so both are under `not_used`. I counted their rows only to check printed counts: S1 agrees with the text (695/1,160, 13/33, 39/44). S2 gives 160 and 207 where Fig. 3F prints 156 and 204. That is recorded as a suspected error on `F3F_up`. The schema has no way to anchor a row count of a data file.
- **Supplement numbering.** The text cites "Table S2" for collated KEGG pathways and for culture-age comparisons, and "Table S3" for the Venn genes. The files hold Venn genes (S2) and a protocol comparison (S3). These are recorded as cross_references.
- **Differentiation factors, EHT supplements, EHT dissociation.** Each is stated across several sentences, and no 30-word quote holds the whole set. I used a ContextObj with its own `anchor` (the first sentence) and a `note`, so these values have no `context_anchors` holds-check. A context anchor that can take several quotes would fix this.
- **Cell line per experiment.** Methods list MSN02-4, H9 and WTC11, but the line is never given for the main experiments. Only S4B (H9) and S4C (H9 + WTC11) name lines, so `cell_line` is "not stated" elsewhere. I did not fill `donor_age` from the lines' donor ages (36 y, 30 y), because the line per unit is unknown.
- **"Far left" reference.** Several legends say "relative to control (far left)". Readings I chose:
  - Fig. 1A: the far-left bar is day 0. The asterisks over fetal and adult samples are recorded against day 0, although the dashed divider suggests they may be against day 42.
  - Fig. 4C: atrial pairs are recorded against the atrial control. The far-left bar is the ventricular control.
  - Fig. 7D: every mark is recorded against the 2D control, as the legend says, even within the EHT and lactate formats.
  - S4A: each GW0742 bar is recorded against the control bar at the same LCFA concentration.
- **Statements of no difference under Student's t legends.** For panels the text calls unchanged (F1F, F1H, S5D basal, S6C, F6H developed force, T2Rlx90/T2Pk and capture frequency, S5C DNM1L), no pair is marked. I wrote one omnibus item with the legend's test and `stated_direction: none`, because there is no item form for "all pairs vs control n.s., P not printed".
- **Fig. 7D and S5C asterisks.** The Fig. 7 legend defines no asterisk levels, only DESeq2 p < 0.05, so p carries that threshold. The S5 legend names both Student's t-tests and DESeq2. I used its t-test asterisk levels.
- **Representative flow-plot percentages** (Fig. 1C–E, 4A, S4B) are printed in the figure image. They are recorded as `kind: value` per group with unit anchors. They come from one representative plot each, not means.
- **P values printed in the figure** (Fig. 1G, 1H, 1J: "p=0.1017" etc.) are anchored to the unit (image text).
- **Fig. 3C** is a table inside a figure. Its P for LCFA + GW0742 are swapped relative to the text (Up/Down). The text P is in `p` and the figure P in `p_values`, with a suspected error. The test for this overlap is not stated.
- **Lactate** is a compared exposure in Fig. 7, so it is a `treatment` member ("lactate", 4mM) there. In the scRNA-seq unit (F1LMN) the cells are "Lactate-treated" without comparison, so it is `purification` there.
- **Blebbistatin** (10 mM in Tyrode's during AP/CaT) goes in `other_conditions`, not `treatment`, because it is applied to every group and is not compared.
- **time_point vs day_of_measurement.** "4 weeks after PPAR modulation" went to `time_point`. "approximately 10 weeks old at the time of analysis" (Limitations) went to `day_of_measurement` for the 4-week-treatment units. That sentence is paper-wide, and I applied it to every analysis after 4-week treatment.
- **Wnt activation/inhibition days and insulin day** are "not stated". The protocol uses BMP4/Activin A then XAV from day 3, but no duration is stated, so I did not compute one.
- **Preparation for RNA-seq, ATAC-seq and EHT measurements** is "not stated": the paper never words it (lysate, intact tissue).
- **Atrial differentiation** is stated only by citing three papers at once (Devalla 2015; Lee 2017a; Zhang 2011). `by_reference` takes one citation key, so I made one citation entry, `atrial_protocols`, for the group.
- **EHT mechanical load** is recorded `by_reference` to Schaaf et al., 2014. Posts are mentioned, but load and stiffness are not.
- **Fig. 6A–B n**: the legend's 4–10 cells/condition/replicate conflicts with the Methods' 10–15 cells per coverslip (30–45 per condition). Recorded as a suspected error on the measurement items.
- **Fetal age**: the legend says week 16 of gestation and the text says "20-week-old fetal". Recorded as a suspected error on the donor_age of the Fig. 1A native groups.
- **Software version**: Methods say "GraphPad Prism 6", the key resources table "GraphPad Prism 5.0". Not written to any item. No field holds the statistics software separately.
- **LCFA concentrations**: the manuscript prints palmitic acid "12.5 mM" next to oleic and linoleic "12.5 μM" (a likely unit typo). LCFA concentrations are not recorded as qualifiers, so this is only logged here.
- **Table S3** is an image-only summary of the paper's own LCFA + GW0742 results. Each row is recorded as a pair item with `stated_direction` anchored to the unit. The ploidy row ("NA") is not an item.
- **Fig. 6G 1.48-fold** is an average over all EHTs, week 4 against week 0. It is a main-effect item with the value unattached, because `between` takes two groups only.

## Figures and data reader

- **F1A (Fig. 1A, journal_version.pdf p. 4; raster, 300 dpi)**: one script reads all three stacked panels (PPARA, PPARD, PPARG; 26 bars each). Axis columns and tick marks are found in the image; the tick values are the printed labels (6/4/2/0; 8/6/4/2/0; 2.0/1.5/1.0/0.5/0). Bar positions come from a regular grid fitted to the bar outlines at the baseline. The mean is the bar's top outline and the error is the cap of the upper SD bar. Markers are found by matching a ring cut from the figure itself, then refined to the ring's darkness centroid.
- **Points only where every marker stands alone.** A bar is countable only if each marker-coloured ring matches well, no two are within one marker width, and no marker ink is left unexplained after masking the bar edges, top outline and matched rings. Only PPARA Apex passes: 5 points. In every other bar some markers touch each other, the bar top, the cap or the baseline (all differentiation days; most tissue bars). Those bars return the mean and error only, with `countable: false`.
- **Bars with no mean**: PPARA d1, d2 and PPARG d0–d3 are 1–2 px tall and their tops are covered by markers. Neither mean nor error is read for them. Caps hidden under markers give no error (several bars).
- **Points vs printed n**: the n = 4 in the legend belongs to the hPSC-CM differentiation ("during hPSC-CM differentiation (n = 4) and in human fetal heart tissue"), and the record copies it to the tissue items. PPARA Apex shows 5 separate markers. Their mean (2.144) matches the bar (2.136) and their SD (0.934) matches the error-bar length (0.933), so that bar is mean ± SD of 5 values. Other tissue bars also show 3–5 markers by eye (not counted: they touch). The tissue groups' n is not stated, and the record's `n: n = 4` on the F1A tissue comparisons (and on the tissue groups of F1A_PPARA/D/G) looks wrong. This is for the record reader.
- **Labels**: each label is `<item>/<group>`. The comparison items (e.g. F1A_PPARA_LV: LV vs d0) repeat the readings of the same bars under their own labels, because one label links to one item.
- No released data cover Fig. 1A (Tables S1–S2 are gene lists, already in `not_used`).

## Vocabulary agent

- **Flags (14, all *several measures*):** items that name several genes, pathways, levels or measures with no set name the paper gives: PPAR isoform expression (F1N), the six coactivator/RXR genes (S1E), the four-category cardiac gene heatmap (S3B), the four KEGG items (F3A, F3E, F7A, F7B; F7B names no pathway at all), the promoter/enhancer peak stacked bar (F3G), the 50-locus accessibility heatmap (F3H), PPARa/d/g IF (F1B), tissue compaction and gross morphology (F6F), and the Table S3 rows "OXPHOS and active FAO", "APD (20th and 50th percentiles)" and "systolic and diastolic force". Where to draw this line was a judgement.
- **Split request (1):** "developed force (spontaneous)": "spontaneous" belongs to `pacing` (the unit's pacing reads "none", which agrees, so this is not a contradiction).
- **LCFA** is mapped to the set palmitate + oleic acid + linoleic acid (Methods: "Palmitic Acid…, Oleic Acid…, Linoleic Acid…; LCFAs used in 5:4:1 ratio"), not to ChEBI *long-chain fatty acid*.
