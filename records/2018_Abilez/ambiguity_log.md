## Record reader

- **Which cell line per experiment.** The paper says EHMs were made from "either hESC-CMs (H7 line) or hiPSC-CMs (IMR-90 line)" and never says which line went into which figure. `cell_line` is *not stated* (with a note) for every EHM unit, including F7, even though its axis reads "vs. iPSC". S3 uses H7 because its legend says "hESC-CM" and H7 is the paper's only hESC line.
- **Fig. 6 and Fig. 7 significance marks are image-only.** I matched each bracket in Fig. 6E–H to its two bars by eye from a render, and each `*`/`n.s` in Fig. 7B to the D50 bar it sits over. Those items are anchored `kind: unit`. The bracket endpoints in 6E (5 mm 1 Hz vs 9 mm 1 Hz) and 6H (No Tension 1 Hz vs 5 mm 1 Hz) were the least certain.
- **Fig. 6 "*#" marks.** `*` means Student's t test and `#` means one-way ANOVA with Tukey. A bracket marked `*#` is written as two items, one per test (`_t`, `_tukey`). This is not a second P for one test, so `p_values` was not used.
- **Fig. 6E–H sampling day and n.** These are not stated: the text says transients were seen "at day 5" for panels B–C, and the n = 12 refers to the representative traces in panel B. I set both to *not stated* for E–H.
- **Fold values in F7 ("3-fold and over 15-fold versus 1D culture at day 50").** The text says "in a 3D structure" without naming EHM− or EHM+. I recorded the values on a measurement item over [EHM− D50, EHM+ D50, Mono D50] with no `group` or `between`, rather than picking a pair. The schema has no form for "3D versus 1D" pooled over two formats.
- **iPSC bar in Fig. 7** is the normaliser ("vs. iPSC"), not a group. The fibroblast-only controls are written `platform: 1D` / `3D`, because the only extractable wording is "1D and 3D controls consisting of only fibroblasts". The figure's "fibroblasts cultured in EHM shape" is image text.
- **Rat EHMs (S9)** are `kind: engineered` (rat neonatal CMs in EHMs) and override every hPSC-specific field with *not applicable* or *not stated*. Because of this, most hPSC fields cannot sit in `default_context`. The Results say "rat neonatal derived-CMs" but the S9 legend says "rat stem cell-derived EHMs" (logged as a suspected error). The S9 test is taken from the Methods default ("Unless otherwise specified … Student's t test").
- **Conflicting n in Fig. 3.** Results print "n = 5, 3, 4, and 3 … (Fig. 3C–3H)" and then "n = 6, 6, 8, and 6" "For Figure 3C–3G". Both are recorded, the first on a condensation item and the second on the panel items, with a suspected error on each.
- **Text vs figure conflicts** are recorded and not resolved. Amplitude is called "not statistically significantly different" while Fig. 6E marks 7 mm vs 5 mm spontaneous `*`. ADRB2 is called "similar in 1D and 3D" while Fig. 7B marks EHM− D50 `*`. These are recorded as `opposes` on the claims.
- **Vmax spread.** "(± SEM)" is printed after the MDP value in the same sentence, while the Methods default is SD. I applied SEM to Vmax too. The MDP has no printed unit (logged as a suspected error).
- **Qualitative items (Fig. 4, 5, S6, S7, S8, S10).** These are stated observations from images with no values or tests. Pairs whose direction the text states are written as `compares` with `stated_direction`. The rest are measurements.
- **Values like "less than half", "approximately half", "50% or more"** are kept as printed strings. Code may not parse them.
- **Fig. 1 finite-element model** (stress fields, % compaction, "35%–55% higher stresses") is a model output. It is left out: no unit, groups or items.
- **Passive stretch** is written in `mechanical_load` (`passive stretch` with an `inter_post_distance` qualifier; control `no tension` with `posts: one post`). `maturation_strategy` is left *not stated* so that one fact sits in one field.
- **Hydrogel concentration.** The supplement says "final concentration of 20 mg/ml" and also "200 µl of 10 g/ml Collagen Type-I" in a mix of about 392 µl. I recorded the stated 20 mg/ml with a suspected error.
- **Docx image.** `NIHMS926341-supplement-Supplemental.docx` holds one embedded image (50 kB). I could not view it, and judged it most likely to be Supplemental Table 1 (antibodies), which has no group values. Supplementary figures S1–S10 and movies S1–S3 are not in the folder and are listed as missing.
- **Stress–strain method.** The rendered journal Methods read "15 µm" per 15 second interval, the extracted text reads "15 mm", and the S9 legend reads "15 m" (µ lost). I could not settle the step size, so the method is written without it.

## Vocabulary agent

- **`insulin_day: D7` flagged *not a context*.** It is a quantity field and code does not read "D7" as a number. None of the flag kinds fits a quantity that the parser cannot read, so I used *not a context* and asked for it to be written as "day 7".
- **Passive stretch → `mechanical_load:end_anchored`** (held between two end anchors, regime not stated), with a new `inter_post_distance` qualifier. The paper never says whether the PDMS posts are rigid or flexible, so neither *isometric* nor *auxotonic* is supported. "passive stretch" got no synonym, because other papers use it for applied stretch.
- **Control "no tension" (one post) → `mechanical_load:unloaded`**, as a synonym. A tissue around a single post carries no load. S10's released EHMs map to the same term.
- **"engineered heart muscle" → `platform:engineered_3d`** (synonym). The unit's platform covers the one-post control too, so *tissue between two pillars* would be wrong for that group. If the reader moved the platform to the groups, the stretched groups could map more precisely.
- **"1D" → monolayer, and "3D" (fibroblast-only EHM) → engineered_3d**, as stored mappings with no synonyms. This paper's "1D" means monolayer, which other papers would not mean by it.
- **"rat neonatal derived-CMs" → CL:0000746.** CL:0002495 *fetal cardiomyocyte* lists "neonatal cardiomyocyte" as an exact synonym, but adopting it would merge fetal and neonatal. I sent the split requests to species and donor_age (both already stated as rat and neonatal).
- **"extent of compaction"**: I added `measure:compaction_extent` under tissue_condensation, because the value is a percentage ("50% or more") and not a yes/no judgement. I wrote its standing key loose. "EHM condensation" and "EHM compaction" both map to tissue_condensation, so code may report them as wording copies.
- **F3 observation measures** (wall detachment, opacity, V-neck) → `constructs_with_feature` with a criterion. Detachment from posts → `anchor_detachment` (synonym).
- **"tissue morphology organization" and "cardiomyocyte alignment"** both go to tissue_anisotropy, which is qualitative. Sarcomere alignment and presence go to sarcomere_organisation, with a new `aspect` qualifier.
- **Calcium amplitude** is stored with `expressed_as: fold`, `normalised_to: F0 at the first frame`, because code would otherwise match it with no qualifiers. Time to peak → calcium_transient_rise_time, with no level stated. TD50 → decay time, `level: TD50`.
- **qPCR genes**: `expressed_as: fold`. `normalised_to` is left out because EHM+ groups are also normalised by the 3:1 cell ratio.
- **Flags, *several measures* (5)**: "FSP-1 and CD31 distribution…", "FSP-1 and CD31 positive cells", "calcium transient waveform properties", "ADRB1 and CAV3 expression", and "ADRB1, CAV3, CACNA1C and TNNT2 expression in fibroblasts".
- **Record reader**: S3 MDP (−63.3) has no printed unit, which is already labelled. The beating-rate axis reads "Duration (s)", which is already labelled. I fixed neither.

## Formula agent

- **No inputs anywhere.** The record has no data.yaml or readings.yaml (Fig. 3, 6, 7 have no per-sample markers; S9 and S10 figures are not in the folder), so every derivation is *cannot check*. They record the method only.
- **Young's modulus as `E = sigma/eps`.** The paper takes E as the slope of the linear region; I mapped it to `linear_elastic_stress_strain` with stress and strain *not stated*. Stress needs a cross-sectional area the paper never gives, and the stretch step is printed three ways (15 mm, 15 μm, 15 m), so I wrote no stress or strain step. "small strain" is *not stated*; "linear elastic" is reported by the "linear phase before the plateau" sentence.
- **Maximum active force** is a two-step chain: `Fa = Fs - Fd` per twitch (`sum_of_parts`) and the mean of the 10 highest (`mean_def`, n = 10 printed). The sum S cannot point at the per-twitch derivation as a `derived:` input (it is a selection of 10 values), so it is *not stated* with a note. Averaging over EHMs and the number of EHMs are not stated.
- **qPCR folds (3-fold, over 15-fold).** Written as `ratio_def` of day-50 3D over day-50 monolayer relative expression, with alternatives for EHM− vs EHM+ (the text says only "3D") and for 2^-(mean ΔΔCt difference). The 2^-ΔΔCt step itself is a separate derivation with no output. Both qPCR model assumptions are *not stated*.
- **"Normalized by the ratio of cardiomyocytes to fibroblast (3:1)"** (Fig. 7 legend) has no stated arithmetic: written `no_formula` with three labelled alternatives (÷3/4, ×3, ÷3).
- **"Decreased to less than half of their original length"** read as Ld/L0 < 1/2, with (L0 − Ld)/L0 as an alternative. **"Compacted 50% or more"** has no definition of what was measured: `no_formula`, with relative decrease and remaining fraction as alternatives.
- **Calcium amplitude f/f0** is recorded on F6E_amp_7mm_text with no output. "Background intensity was subtracted" and "plots were normalized to zero" give two alternatives (background-subtracted ratio; ΔF/F0).
- **Percentages of cells** (S3 subtypes, S5 troponin-T) are written as n/N with N not stated, plus `fraction_bounds` on every percent value, including "50% or more" for compaction. Observation for code: the S3 subtypes sum to 57 + 24 + 19 = 100 %. That is consistent, but I did not write it as a derivation because the paper does not state it.
- **Not recorded:** "This amount of strain is about twice the physiological systolic strain … 12% to 22%". The relaxed length is 40 % of the stretched length, which gives a strain of 60 % (stretched reference) or 150 % (relaxed reference), not about 2 × 12–22 %. The strain is not an evidence value, so this is noted here only, as a possible inconsistency for code or the record reader. The beating-rate axis "Duration (s)" (already labelled) may mean that a period was plotted and not a rate. I wrote no `frequency_def` step because no value is printed.
- **No proposals:** every step was covered by an existing entry.
