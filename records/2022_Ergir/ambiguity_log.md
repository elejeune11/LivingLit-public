## Record reader

- **Two cell lines, unassigned.** Methods name DF 19-9-7 T and TNNI1-iPS, but only the GFP-reporter experiment (Fig. 1B) says which line it used. I set `cell_line` on F1B only; every other unit is `not stated`, and `number_of_lines` is `not stated` (not "2"), because the paper never says how many lines any one experiment used.
- **Wnt days not stated.** Methods give CHIR99021 on day 0, IWP-2 on day 2 and a medium change on day 4. Working out the activation and inhibition durations would mean computing them from the protocol, so both are `not stated`. `insulin_day` "day 7" is quoted directly ("beating cell clusters were observed (day 7)").
- **2D controls replated three ways.** 2D controls were replated on Matrigel at 200,000 cells/well, replated at 1.5 × 10⁶ cells/well, or left undetached. No figure says which was used, so 2D groups have `replating_coating: Matrigel` and `seeding_density: not stated`. Day-15 2D (before any replating) has `not applicable`. I wanted a way to state "one of several stated options".
- **Long-term time points are loose.** F7B–C is "after day 50" and F8 is "> day 50". F7A is "long-term", taken as day 50 from the text's "3D long-term culture (day 50)" (noted on the unit). The doxorubicin videos say day 53, but Fig. 8 does not, so F8 stays at "> day 50".
- **Fig. 8 incubation time.** Methods say 10 min per concentration; Results say 15–20 min. I recorded 10 min with a `suspected_error` on the unit's `time_point`. The 0.1 µM isoproterenol level is anchored to the axis tick (image only).
- **External RNA-seq datasets** (adult heart [76, 77], EHT [78]) are `other study` groups. The adult group cites only c76, because a group takes one citation; c77 is named in its note.
- **Supplementary Fig. SI-4** (day 50 vs day 30 within 3D and within 2D) was added as unit S4 in this pass, with DE and GO items per dimensionality. Its text is image only, so the anchors are `kind: unit` on the panel titles. The per-gene data are in Supplementary dataset 1 (sheets `3D-d50_vs_3D-d30` and `2D-d50_vs_2D-d30`), which I left for the figures-and-data reader. C23 ("maturation … strongly promoted by time in culture") now rests on S4.
- **Transcriptomic items without values.** DE, GO and heatmap items have direction words or gene lists but no group values. I used `stated_direction` only where the text gives a direction. The GO items have no stated test (`test: not stated`, `p` from the legend's p.adj).
- **Z-band width.** The text says it increased "but not significantly" yet prints p < 0.0001. Both are kept; the contradiction is a suspected error on F5E and C18 is judged support: does not follow.
- **"4 times fold" (FABP3, LPL).** Supplementary dataset 1 gives a log2 fold change of about 4 (about 15-fold). I flagged this as a suspected error and kept both values, the text's and the data's.
- **Cross-references.** "Supplementary Fig. 4" is cited for mitochondria counts, and Fig. 5F for t-tubules. Supplementary Fig. 1B shows day 30 but is cited for day 50.
- **Model outputs:** none in this paper.

## Vocabulary agent

- **Main cell line looks missed.** Methods name DF 19-9-7 T (WiCell; Cellosaurus CVCL_K055, iPS-DF19-9-7T) as the line used, with TNNI1-iPS (CVCL_VK80) only for the reporter in Fig. 1B. Every other unit has `cell_line: not stated`. I did not map the line from the Methods. The record reader should read `cell_line` again.
- **`static` (mechanical_load).** The paper only says "static culture conditions". I added `mechanical_load:static_culture` (no dynamic stimulus; load regime not stated) rather than `unloaded`. Calling the free aggregate unloaded would fill the field from the platform.
- **3D hOCMT → `platform:cell_aggregate` (synonym).** I treated it as the same thing as a scaffold-free spherical microtissue. The paper argues hOCMTs qualify as cardiac organoids; if comparisons ever separate organoids from spheroids, this needs a narrower term.
- **Preparation of live imaging and whole mount → `intact_tissue`, with no synonyms.** Both words are methods that also fit live 2D cells or fixed whole mounts.
- **Seven marker-panel measures flagged `several measures`** (WT1/TBX18/TE-7/SM22a, TNNT2/ACTN2, NFAT2/CD31, MYL2/MYL7, MESP1/Brachyury/SOX2/ASCL2, GATA4/SERCA2/α-SMA/Cx43). `protein_staining` takes one protein. MYL2/MYL7 describes two co-stained pools, so it may need a co-staining measure instead of a split.
- **`DMSO or methanol` flagged `alternatives`.** It is the vehicle of one untreated group serving two drugs.
- **TEM items.** Myofiber organisation, amount of mitochondria and glycogen accumulation map to `ultrastructure` with a `feature`. The necrotic/apoptotic core maps to `cell_death`, with the region qualifier added. Code matched `sarcomere organisation` (F5A, TEM) to `measure:sarcomere_organisation`, whose definition says immunostained light microscopy. That is a near miss in the existing term, which I left alone.
- **Basal mitochondrial respiration** gets its own term under OCR, because this paper prints both the raw last-baseline OCR (111.83) and basal respiration (94.38). Both are normalised to 300 ng DNA. I left `normalised_to` out, since the units carry it.
- **Beating onset reference.** I wrote the reference as seeding in 3D, with beating starting at ROCK-inhibitor removal after 48 h. The paper's wording leaves the reference event loose.
- **No ChEBI id for DNase I** (an enzyme): `adopt_own treatment:dnase_i` under `treatment:endonuclease`. `Ins + P/S` maps to insulin, penicillin and streptomycin, with no synonyms: "Ins" is too short, and "P/S" is split by the "/" separator.

## Formula agent

- **Seahorse parameters by reference only.** Methods say the Mito Stress Test "was performed according to the manufacturer's instructions"; basal respiration, ATP production, proton leak and spare capacity are never defined, and the kit manual is not a citation in the record, so the four steps are `no_formula` with the Agilent definitions as labelled alternatives (code does not evaluate them). Under those definitions ATP production + proton leak = basal respiration: 3D 32.62 + 37.37 = 69.99 vs 94.38, 2D 2.59 + 8.02 = 10.61 vs 40.22. An observation for code; it can only check this once kit-defined parameters get a library entry and a way to be cited.
- **Spare capacity: difference or percentage?** The kit reports it both ways. Both are written as alternatives. 2D 125.35 is larger than 2D basal 40.22, which fits either.
- **DNA normalisation per 300 ng.** The paper prints "/300 ng DNA" but gives no formula. I wrote `R/D` (the named operation). The 300 ng scale sits in the unit, which code cannot parse, so nothing is rebuilt.
- **Viability normalisation.** Whether each sample was divided by the untreated mean or by a per-plate value is not stated. The untreated SD of 4.1 % rules out each sample being normalised to itself. Aggregation is `not stated`.
- **"4 times fold" for FABP3/LPL.** The fold change rebuilt from the dataset's log2 values (15.6, 14.7) is inconsistent with the printed 4. The record reader's alternative (the 4 is the log2 value) is written as a labelled alternative. I proposed `fold_change_from_log2_fold_change` (r = 2**l, derived from ratio_from_log2_values), because no entry takes a single log2 fold change.
- **Flow-cytometry marker sum.** 3D day 50 TNNT2 + CD90 + CD31 = 83.27 + 23.38 + 12.78 = 119.4 %. Each marker was gated separately and the paper does not say the markers are mutually exclusive, so no bound applies. Only per-marker fraction bounds are written. The representative histogram values use a decimal comma ("54,3"), so I left them out of the bounds in case the comma is parsed as a thousands separator.
