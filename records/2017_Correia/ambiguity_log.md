## Record reader

- **Medium conditions as fields.** GLCM/FAM/GFAM/LACM differ in glucose (base medium) and added substrates. Chose `cm_medium` "RPMI" vs "RPMI without Glc", and `treatment` as the complete set of added substrates (Gln, galactose, OA, PA, sodium L-Lactate; concentrations as qualifiers); GLCM `treatment: none`. Glucose removal therefore sits only in `cm_medium`. A set with a schedule is not expressible: LACM&GFAM (10 days lactate, then 10 days GFAM) is written as the GFAM set plus qualifier `first_10_days: "4 mM sodium L-Lactate"`, so code sees GFAM vs LACM&GFAM differ in a treatment qualifier only. A sequence field would be cleaner.
- **"LACM&GFAM d10" groups** (Figs 2C, 3C, 4D, 5G–I, Table 1 "LacM d10", Table S6 "LACM&GFAM d10") are cells still in LACM; recorded with LACM context and the printed label. In Fig. 1K the "LACM&GFAM d10" pie is labelled with Gal/OA/PA, so there it is the GFAM set plus the lactate qualifier (cells just switched).
- **Days.** d0/d10/d20 are days in the test media, after 15 days of differentiation; written as "day 0/10/20" (`day_of_measurement`). Fig. 2B's legend says "day 15", which is not a sampling day in the Methods; kept as printed.
- **Platform "monolayer"** is stated only for TEM ("Monolayers of hiPSC-CMs"); applied to all non-functional units (metabolism, transcriptome, morphology) because cells were differentiated and cultured in plates. Functional units (Fig. 6, S8, Table S6) are "single cells" on 5 kPa PDMS.
- **Pacing** during contractility/calcium/AP recordings: "Movies were acquired without and with field-stimulated with 10 V at 1 Hz", without saying which data are which. `pacing: not stated` with the sentence as an other_condition.
- **Bracket reading.** All post-hoc pairs were read by eye from raster figures (Figs 1, 2, 5, 6, S6, S7, S8) at high zoom; dense brackets in Fig. 5H–I and S8 were the hardest. The bottom-row run in Fig. 5H ("*********" over three brackets) was read as three "***".
- **Test per figure.** The Methods say "either unpaired Student's t test or one-way ANOVA" without saying which per panel; written as that alternative on every figure item. Fig. S6 has no star key: the Methods threshold (P < 0.05) is written.
- **13C-MFA fluxes** (Figs 2D, 3A–B, Table S1, text flux and ATP values, Gal vs Glc uptake 30.42 vs 254.17) are model outputs and are not recorded; claims C35 and C36 rest on them and are judged cannot judge. PCA variance percentages are not recorded either.
- **Heat maps, Venn regions, IPA bars.** Gene-level heat maps (Figs 2C, 3C, 4D) are recorded only as the directions the text states, with d0 as reference (Fig. 2C's legend says d0; Fig. 3C's does not). Venn total counts are recorded; the overlap-region counts are not. IPA −log(p) bars (Figs 4F, S6C) are plotted only.
- **Correlations and distances** relate two groups: each Table S3/S4/S5 row is an item with `groups: [a, b]` and values `between`. The text's "significantly higher correlation with HAV" is an extra pair item (GFAM vs GLCM d20), with each group's r as its value; no test is printed for the difference.
- **Text values with day not named.** "Gln decreased to 38%" and "Gal approximately 40%" (GFAM, Fig. 1J) are attached to GFAM d0, whose pie prints 38%. The 4-fold lipid increase "compared with other media" is written as FAM's fold over GLCM, the axis normalisation.
- **Spread of text ± values** (forces in Results) is not stated in the sentence; Fig. 6's legend says SEM but Table S6 prints the same LACM&GFAM force as mean ± SD. Recorded as not stated. Table 1 does not say what ± is.
- **Table 1 other-study rows** are `other study` groups (citations 8, 54, 5, 7, 50, 4, 1); the adult row cites [1, 2] but a group takes one citation (ref1; noted). The let-7 OCR range is one value for both let-7i and let-7g (`over`). Table 1's categorical "Metabolism" column is not recorded.
- **number_of_lines** is "not stated" paper-wide: the paper uses two lines, but most experiments use only the hiPSC line, and writing "1" would be computed.
- **Workbook (MOESM4 .xls)** holds per-gene fold changes for the heat-map categories (likely Table S2); listed in not_used because no recorded item has a group value in it.
- **Replication judgement.** Functional and morphology n are cells or sarcomeres pooled from 3–5 experiments; the replication dimension is judged overstated for claims resting on them.

## Vocabulary agent
- **HES3 NKX2-5(eGFP/w)**: Cellosaurus 57.0 has no single-reporter HES-3 NKX2-5eGFP/w line. It has only the double reporter CVCL_A8JT. I mapped the line to its parent HES-3 (CVCL_7158) with `no_ontology_term`, and sent the reporter (`NKX2-5(eGFP/w)`) to `transgene` as a split request.
- **Palmitic acid and sodium L-lactate**: I added these as synonyms of the existing treatment terms hexadecanoate (CHEBI:7896, "palmitate") and lactate (CHEBI:24996), so that papers meet on the substrate. ChEBI has separate ids for both: hexadecanoic acid CHEBI:15756 and sodium L-lactate CHEBI:232798. A re-map may prefer the exact ids.
- **OA, PA, Gln**: I gave a synonym only to "Gln". "OA" and "PA" are too ambiguous in a treatment field, so the two treatment sets that use them are stored as mappings.
- **Tracers disagree inside the paper**: Results print [U-13C]OA and [U-13C]PA; Methods list [18-13C]Oleic Acid and [16-13C]Palmitic Acid. The record's measure wording follows Results. The `tracer` qualifier names only the substrate.
- **Possible record issue (not fixed)**: GFAM, FAM and LACM differ from GLCM only by medium, and they are a metabolic maturation strategy by the paper's own framing. Yet `maturation_strategy` is *not stated* for them, and the substrates sit in `treatment`. That matches earlier papers' use of `treatment` for energy substrates, so I left it.
- **Two wordings of one measure**: the record has "cardiac gene expression (contractile, calcium cycling, electrophysiology)" and "(…calcium signaling…)" for the same Fig. 4D heat map. I gave both the same `gene_set` rather than flagging them; the reader may want one wording.
- **Flag**: "GPI and TALDO1 expression" is flagged *several measures* (two named genes in one value). "Oxidative stress resistance and heat shock gene expression" was not flagged, because the Fig. 2C legend shows it as one heat-map category.
- **Single cells on PDMS**: mapped to `platform:culture_2d` with qualifier `cell_contact` rather than a new term (step 4 before step 5). "single cells" was not made a synonym, because it would also fit micropatterned single cells.
- **TrypLE Select**: mapped to TrypLE. No synonym, because the PDF text prints "TrypLE™Select" and that cannot be anchored as "TrypLE Select".
- **Single-cell contraction measures**: `contractile_shortening`, `contractile_velocity` and `relaxation_velocity` are defined for tissues, but I used them for single cells. The difference is carried by platform and preparation, not by the measure. Max and average velocities are separated by a new `statistic` qualifier.
- **Table 1 rows from other studies** (T3, Pluricyte, ES 3D, mechanical + electrical, let-7, time in culture) are mapped as maturation strategies. "Electrical stimulation 3D culture" sends "3D culture" to `platform`, "T3 hormone treatment" sends "T3" to `treatment`, and "Overexpression of let-7" sends "let-7" to `transgene`, all as split requests.
- **PrestoBlue metabolic activity**: the legend gives neither % nor fold, so I used `expressed_as: normalised` with `normalised_to: d0`.
- **Re-map (2026-11-12, vocabulary v52)**: a later paper added "circularity index" as a synonym of `measure:cell_circularity`, so code now matches it. I removed its stored mapping. No other value or edit changed; the edits logged at version 39 stay in the file and are not appended again.

## Formula agent
- **Only F1IK has printed inputs.** FA = OA + PA (6 groups) and the closure of the normalised contributions (Gln = 1 − FA in FAM; Gln = 1 − (Gal + FA) in GFAM and LACM&GFAM) all rebuild within the printed digit. The closure rests on the Methods sentence "normalized by the total incorporation provided by the main substrates", read as: the pie's substrates (Gln, Gal, OA, PA) sum to 100 %. For LACM&GFAM d10 that excludes Lac, though the cells had been in Lac medium for 10 days; the numbers still close (36 + 31 + 33 = 100).
- **"approximately 30%" over two groups**: the LACM&GFAM FA value is printed once for d10 and d20. I wrote one derivation per day, both outputting that one value (the rebuilt values are 33 % and 29 %).
- **Force is by citation only** (Kijlstra et al., ref. 37; PDMS, ref. 38). The inputs are named (cell width, length, shortening, E = 5 kPa), but the formula is not printed. The unit is mN/mm² (a stress), yet the inputs include no area or thickness term, so the step to a stress is unknown. All force items are marked `no_formula`.
- **Circularity index**: "ImageJ standard analysis plugins" is named without its formula. I gave `no_formula` with the ImageJ 4π·A/P² as a labelled alternative. I did not propose it to the library, because no paper value can test it here. I also put no ≤ 1 bound on circularity, because no library bound covers it except fraction_bounds, which is meant for fractions.
- **Comparison items without values**: most items are one pairwise test with no printed value, and the figures carry no readings. I wrote their derivations (OCR/ECAR, APD50/APD90, F/F0, % of d0, fold over GLCM, % shortening, length/width) as `cannot check`, with inputs not stated. They become checkable once the bars are read.
- **Fold changes**: for KCNJ2 "2-fold", I used ratio_def, with fold-from-log2 intensities (ratio_from_log2_values) as a labelled alternative, because the arrays were variance-stabilised. I gave 1.6-fold binucleated `aggregation: of means` (a ratio of two percentages), which is my judgement.
- **S7 OCR/ECAR**: the record calls S7 OCR and ECAR "fold change". OCR/ECAR as a ratio of the two fold changes equals the fold change of the ratio only if both are normalised to the same group, and the legend does not say what they are normalised to.
- **S6A PrestoBlue "relative to d0"** and **S7 OCR/ECAR "fold change"**: the legends do not state the normalisation, so I wrote no derivation (I found no anchor).
- **Not recordable**: qMet = ΔMet/(Δt·Xv) (Methods) is stated, but the record has no Fig. 1B rates. The text's "ATP production increased 2.7-fold in GFAM (1843 nmol/h) comparing with GLCM (687 nmol/h)" (1843/687 = 2.68) and "98% … Gal (64%) and FA (34%)" are derivations with printed inputs, but they have no evidence items (Fig. 3A,B pies are not in the record). The record reader should add them.
- **Pearson r with CI95 (ST3, ST5)**: the CI is from the Fisher z transform, but n (genes) is not stated and the library has no Fisher z entry, so I wrote no derivation.
