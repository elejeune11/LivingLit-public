## Record reader

- **Significance marks read from panels.** Several pairs are printed only as asterisk brackets inside a figure (Fig. 3C, Fig. 4E/F, Fig. 5F, Fig. 7F, Fig. S2B, S3A, S3B). I anchored these with `kind: unit` and gave the legend's threshold where the bracket's ends were unambiguous (Fig. 3C: ascending `*`, `**`, `***` from baseline to 100 nM, 500 nM, 1 µM). Where a mark had no stated threshold (Fig. S2B shows `***` but the legend defines only `*` and `**`; Fig. S3's legend gives `**p<0.001, **p<0.05`) I wrote `significant, threshold not stated` and a `suspected_error`.
- **Fig. 7D (quinidine).** The panel prints per-dose marks against baseline (black) and against 0.1 µM (red); which mark sits on which dose was not legible at the resolution I could render (page renders outside the repository were blocked by the sandbox, so I read the pages through the Read tool only). I recorded one omnibus item per measure (APD90, CV) with `significant, threshold not stated` instead of inventing per-dose pairs.
- **Fig. 7A (restitution).** Same reason: several brackets are drawn over the six frequencies with no legible assignment. I wrote one omnibus item over the six pacing-frequency groups with the text's `P < 0.05, n = 10`. The frequency levels themselves are only axis labels, so their context anchors are `kind: unit` quoting the axis.
- **µ-sign extraction.** Both extractors mangle the micro sign in some sentences ("0.1, 0.3, 1, 3, 10, and 30 µM" comes out with an "l"), so the quinidine dose anchors are `kind: unit` with the legend's verbatim wording. Other µ quotes matched normally.
- **Statistical test per item.** No item names its test. The Methods state paired t-test for two treatments on the same group, unpaired t-test between two groups, and repeated-measures one-way ANOVA with Tukey for more than two groups. I applied that rule and wrote the test with "(Methods)".
- **`time_point` beside `treatment`.** The Methods say recordings were made 15 min after adding drugs, so every drug group states a time point and every baseline group states "baseline". Code therefore sees two differing fields in each drug pair (mostly labelled aliased). The alternative — leaving the time out — would drop a stated fact, so I kept it.
- **Fig. 4A/B (L0 vs Lmax).** Tissue length is not a context field, so the two groups have identical context and code reports "no field differs". I put the lengths in each group's `other_conditions`.
- **Fig. 4C and S4B/C dose-response curves.** The calcium levels are plotted but never printed, so I could not write one group per level; F4C has a single group whose `measurement_calcium_mM` is the stated range 0.2–4 mM. The per-level points are left to the figures reader.
- **"of similar age".** The text compares ECM-EHTs with "2D cultured cells of similar age" without naming the day; I took the day-30 pair and noted it on the items.
- **Purity stated three ways.** ">85%" (Methods), "at least 80% cTnT+" (Results) and 87.2% in Fig. S1A. I put ">85%" in `default_context` and recorded 87.2% as the panel's value with a `suspected_error`.
- **`preparation` mostly "not stated".** The paper never words how the tissue was prepared for the confocal, force or mapping measurements; I left those "not stated" rather than inferring "intact tissue". Where it is worded ("whole EHT level", "dispersed single-cells", "Total RNA", "dissociated cardiomyocytes") I used the paper's words.
- **Qualitative panels left out.** Fig. 1B/C/D, Fig. 2A/B, Fig. 3A/B, Fig. 6, Fig. 7E and Fig. S1B–D are images with no measurement and no test; I recorded no units for them. Fig. 2E–G are also images, but the text asserts a direction for them, so they are units with `stated_direction` items and no values.
- **Cell lines by reference.** The genotypes of the LQTS2 and CPVT2 lines are given only by citing [9] and [14]; both are `by_reference`. The healthy-control line is written with the declared state `healthy control line`.
- **ATX-II dose.** Text says 30 nM, the Fig. 3 legend says "30 ng"; recorded as a `suspected_error`, not corrected.
- **Fig. S4C key.** The older tissues are labelled "Day 28" in the key while the legend and text say 30 d; recorded as a `suspected_error`, and the group keeps its printed label.
- **Fig. 5B / S3B axis labels.** The healthy-control bar is labelled "Baseline" in both panels although the legends say the comparison is with healthy-control EHTs; recorded as `suspected_error`.
- **Movies.** Supplementary Videos 1–6 are named but not in the paper's folder. No item rests on them (each is also stated in the text), so they are not in `source.missing`.
- **`number_of_lines`.** Three lines plus an ArcLight transgenic line are named, but no sentence states a count for any one experiment, so the field is "not stated" rather than computed.

## Figures and data reader

- **Data files.** None: the sources are `main.pdf` and `mmc1.pdf` only; no `data.yaml`.
- **Fig. 4A, B (`fig4b_foc`, raster).** Panel A is a representative trace (no group values). Panel B is a greyscale JPEG; the y axis is calibrated from its six tick marks (0.0–0.5). Means and upper SEM ends were read for L0 (0.132; 0.148) and Lmax (0.368; 0.394) mN/mm². No value is printed in the text to compare against.
- **Points not counted.** About six markers per group are visible, but several in both groups touch or sit under the mean line and the SEM caps, so the markers do not each stand alone; both groups are `countable: false` with no points. No n is printed for this comparison, so no count could be checked either.
- **Error bar type** taken as SEM from the methods (the legend does not say).

## Vocabulary agent

- **Cell lines not in Cellosaurus.** The paper names none of its four lines (healthy control, ArcLight transgenic, LQTS2 patient, CPVT2 patient; refs 9, 13, 14). Searches for ArcLight, LQT2/LQTS2, CPVT/CPVT2, CASQ2, D307H found only other labs' lines, so all four are `adopt_own`. Whether the ArcLight line comes from the healthy-control line is not stated, so they are separate terms.
- **"static stretch" is not printed.** The paper says "passive-stretcher devices" and "passive stretch". I mapped the stated value to `mechanical_load:isometric` (held at a fixed, stretched length) without adding a synonym. The reader may want to restate it as the paper words it, and the load regime may really be unstated.
- **ECM-EHT → ring tissue.** The tissues are cast as rings in circular moulds and moved onto stretchers. No synonym was added because the name includes the substrate (split request to `substrate`, which agrees).
- **Albumin.** CDM3 uses Oryzogen recombinant HSA. I adopted CHEBI:760525 (rice-derived recombinant HSA) without the generic synonym, which would be broader than the id.
- **Blebbistatin bath.** The measurement medium is "medium containing Blebbistatin" with the medium unnamed. I mapped it to `culture_medium` (medium: not stated) and added the qualifier `additive`. Blebbistatin is not a varied treatment, so no split was made. The printed "5 mmol/L" is probably µmol/L (a glyph loss); I recorded no dose.
- **Pacing.** "rapid pacing (2 Hz)" (Fig. 5 calcium imaging) is left at `electrical_pacing` because the delivery is not stated there. "rapid stimulation" (optical mapping) is `point_stimulation` per Methods 2.9. In F5F the CPVT single-cell group has pacing "none" while the EHT groups have rapid pacing, yet the Fig. 5E legend shows the single cells at baseline and after isoproterenol only. Please check this.
- **Gene ratios.** MYL2/MYL7 and MYH7/MYH6 are mapped as `gene_expression` with gene = numerator and `normalised_to` = denominator, rather than as a new ratio term.
- **Fig. 2G measure** ("spatially abnormal diastolic calcium release and triggered-activity events") names two kinds of event in one panel and one stated direction. I kept it as one feature of the new `calcium_transient_irregularity` and did not flag it as several measures. This was a borderline call.
- **Spontaneous activation rate** (voltage-dye mapping under blebbistatin) is mapped to `contraction_frequency` (beat rate), although contraction is blocked.
- **Termination by field stimulation** is mapped to `arrhythmia_incidence` with outcome "arrhythmia terminated by field stimulation", not to a new term.
- **Calcium decay level** is written as printed in the record's definition: "reduced to 20% of peak amplitude" (= decay to 80 %).
- **"non-essential amino acids"** already normalises to the existing label, so only "b-mercaptoethanol" needed a synonym.
- **Record readings to check.** In F4DEF the measurement calcium "half-maximal calcium concentration (EC50)" is coded as a quantity but holds no number. F5F's "percentage of cells" is counted against "analyzed sites", and the text says "19.1% of the CPVT-EHTs".

## Formula agent

- **Percentages with an unstated numerator.** I wrote the percentages of counts (TXT3, F7F, F5F, S1A) as `f = k/n` with `k` not stated, so code says *cannot check*. The spec has no way to say "k is an unknown whole number". If it had one, code could test whether some k/n falls inside the printed interval. TXT3_termination prints 85 % with n = 7, but 6/7 = 85.7 % lies outside [84.5, 85.5]: either the value was truncated or n is different (an observation for code). The other counts pass: 75 % and 12.5 % of 8 (6/8 and 1/8), and 58 % of 12 (7/12). F5F prints n = 50 sites, but neither 19.1 % nor 2.7 % is a whole number of 50, so I wrote its denominator as not stated.
- **F4D_foc_carb.** "reducing the isoproterenol-induced increase in FOC by 10%" can mean a drop of 10 percentage points or a 10 % relative reduction of the increase. I wrote it as `no_formula` with both readings as labelled alternatives.
- **CSA: one constant or per sample.** "Considering a mean cross sectional area of 1.3 ± 0.1 mm2" suggests one mean CSA, but the ± means CSA was measured for each tissue. I left the input's kind as *not stated*. The paper gives no CSA for the 7 d tissues (S4) and no n for TXT1_csa.
- **qPCR "normalized to GAPDH".** I mapped this to `ratio_def` for all 11 single-gene items and gave 2^-ΔΔCt as a labelled alternative (the calibrator and Ct values are not stated). The 11 near-identical derivations add little. If the spec allowed it, one method-level derivation per unit would be lighter.
- **Item inputs carry no values.** The F1E ratio derivations take items as inputs (MYH7, MYH6). Code notes "0 values for group None" because those items print directions only. MYL7 is not an evidence item.
- **CV, decay times and APD.** These are measured from traces and activation maps (custom software, Clampfit) with no stated formula. Only TXT2_cv is recorded, as `no_formula`, so that it appears on the completeness list.
