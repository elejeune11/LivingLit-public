## Vocabulary agent

- **Flag: preparation "whole cell patch clamp" (unit S8), *not a context*.** It names the recording method, not how the sample was prepared. Methods 2.15–2.16 describe cultured cardiomyocytes on coated coverslips, so the reader should record the preparation itself; the method already sits in the item's `method`.
- **Record issue: IWP-4 sits as a qualifier.** In `differentiation_factors` (unit S1 and its copies), IWP-4 is a qualifier `Wnt inhibitor: IWP-4` on CHIR-99021, not a second member of the set. The spec says `differentiation_factors` is a set that replaces wnt activator and inhibitor, so IWP-4 should be a value of its own. I did not fix this.
- **Record issue: platform "hydrogel".** Units F1d, F2ace, F2bdf, S3a, S3b and S4 have platform "hydrogel" for cell-free material tests. I mapped it to a new term, *cell-free material sample*, and added no synonym, because "hydrogel" as a platform would mis-match other papers.
- **Unsure: "IC50" mapped to EC50.** The paper's IC50s for cisapride (F7c, S9b) come from min–max normalised dose-response curves of APD90 prolongation and ΔBI increase, so they are half-maximal readouts. By this vocabulary's definitions that is EC50, not an inhibitory IC50 against a target. The paper's word is kept in the stated text.
- **Unsure: "% change in APD90" and "% change in ΔBI".** Fig. 7b calls these percent change, but Methods 2.19 normalised each curve so its lowest value is 0 % and its largest 100 %. I mapped them `expressed_as: normalised` rather than `% change`.
- **Unsure: "% coverage" (Fig. 1c).** I read it as each ECM component's share of the LC-MS/MS spectrum counts (*share of the matrisome*). It could also mean peptide sequence coverage, which is a different quantity. The text says only "protein composition" and "spectrum count data".
- **Unsure: "scaffold" (unit S5).** Fig. S5 says cells were cultured "on" the scaffolds; Methods 2.9 says scaffolds were "seeded with" them. 2D or 3D is not stated, so I added *cell-seeded biomaterial scaffold, format not stated* instead of choosing 2D or 3D.
- **Unsure: bioprinted tissues.** I mapped "bioprinted cardiac tissue" to *linear tissue held between two end anchors* (fabrication: bioprinted). The PCL posts' flexibility is not stated, so I did not use the flexible-pillar term. "passive tension" went to *held between two end anchors, regime not stated*, with no synonym.
- **Not in Cellosaurus: UC 3–4.** Lookup returned only UM-UC-3, a bladder carcinoma line, so I added an own term. HS-27A is Cellosaurus CVCL_3719, but `stromal_cell` adopts CL, so it maps to CL:0010001 (stromal cell of bone marrow).
- **ΔBI got its own term.** ΔBI is the beat-to-beat change in beat interval, a measure of irregularity, not the interval itself. I added *beat-to-beat change in beat interval*, and its keys require pacing.
- **Possible printed error (not corrected).** The rGO flake diameter is printed as "300 ± 79 μm", but Methods 2.3 gives the source GO flakes as 90–200 nm.
- **Transglutaminase.** It is recorded as a crosslinker qualifier on the cardiac ECM hydrogel (rule 4, a second dimension), not as a term. The graphene-coated glass is the set {glass, graphene}.

## Formula agent

- **Post stiffness: load case not stated.** Methods 2.12 says "slender-beam theory" with E (~3.8 MPa), the post's diameter (1.5 mm) and length (12.5 mm, Methods 2.10). I mapped k to `cantilever_tip_stiffness` with I from `circular_second_moment_diameter`, formula *not stated*. A load at the tissue's height (`cantilever_point_load_stiffness`) is a labelled alternative. The tissue's height on the post is not printed. These two steps sit on `F3b_force_d7`, since the schema needs an item for a step shared by every force item.
- **F = k·x read from "spring constant".** No force equation is printed. I took "spring constant ... k" plus "post deflection was tracked ... calculation of twitch force" as naming Hooke's law. One post is rigid (glass capillary), so no two-post sum applies. No deflections are printed, so force cannot be rebuilt.
- **"Nearly twice" / "nearly five times" (Day 14 force).** Code rebuilds 23.61/12.97 = 1.82 and 23.61/5.33 = 4.43. The output is printed in words, so code says *cannot check*. 4.43 called "nearly five times" is loose wording (observation, not a verdict).
- **Comparative Ct calibrator.** "age-matched" TCPS controls suggests one calibrator per day, but the figures reader notes the TCPS bars are 1 only at Day 14. Per-sample vs group-mean ΔΔCt is not stated (aggregation *not stated*). The two efficiency assumptions are *not stated* for all 98 ΔΔCt steps. The TNNI3:TNNI1 and N2B:N2BA ratios are chained as ratio_def over two ΔΔCt steps.
- **Min–max normalisation vs "% change".** Methods 2.19 normalises lowest → 0 %, largest → 100 % (proposal `min_max_normalised`, derived from `ratio_of_differences`). Whether min and max are per tissue, per group or pooled is not stated. ΔBI sign (signed vs absolute) and its per-tissue reduction are not stated.
- **No closed form:** IC50s (Prism least-squares dose–response fit), contraction velocity and twitch power (by citation [25] only), compressive modulus (fit not stated), % coverage (spectrum-count share or sequence coverage), % viability (ImageJ, not stated): all `no_formula`.
- **Conductivity.** Only "20 points ... averaged" is stated (mapped to mean_def). The conversion from probe reading to S/m (film thickness) is not stated.
- **Tip potential sign.** "subtraction of a 20 mV tip potential": I wrote v = vm − 20 mV. The sign convention is not stated.
- **RNA-seq fold changes (Table S5)** are mapped to ratio_def as named, but they come from DESeq2 (2^log2FC from a model; shrinkage not stated). That is a labelled alternative.
- **Bounds.** fraction_bounds on S1 purity (94.4 %) and the "> 90 %" viability: both consistent.
