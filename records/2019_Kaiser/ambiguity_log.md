## Record reader

- **Fig. 1B factor levels read from cell colours.** The table under Fig. 1B is an image. I took each gel's low/high level from the red/green cell colour (decoded in memory), and checked it against the four gels that Fig. 2B prints again (2, 8, 10, 16). Gel contexts are anchored as `kind: unit`.
- **Gel 11 in Fig. 1B (suspected error on `F1_Model`).** Fifteen of the 16 gels fit one half-fraction of the five two-level factors. Gel 11 does not, and temperature then has 9 high and 7 low runs. The glyph does read "37". Only temperature is unbalanced, so it is the likely culprit, but I did not correct it.
- **Fig. 1E–H panel titles say "1.0 mg/mL Col"; the Fig. 1B table says 0.5 mg/mL.** I used the table's 0.5 for the low collagen level. This was not recorded as an item because the panels are image-only interaction plots of the same data as Table S4.
- **Polymerization temperature has no field** (`temperature` is the measurement temperature). I wrote it as a group `other_conditions`. Code therefore sees no contrast on the `F1_D` main effect.
- **Regression terms as items.** I wrote the linear terms of the RSM and fractional-factorial models (Tables S4, S6, S8, S10, S12) as `main effect`, with the high level against the low level. Two-factor terms are `interaction` over all groups of the unit. Quadratic terms (A², C², D²) have no fitting form, so I wrote them as `omnibus` over all groups, with a note. Lack-of-fit rows are tests of the model, not of the groups, so they went into notes, except the purified-vs-unpurified lack of fit (`TXT1`), which is the paper's only test between those datasets. Sums of squares and mean squares have unit "not stated". The coefficient scales of the lactate compaction and peak-stress models are not stated (intercepts of 3.04 and −4.18 suggest a transformed response).
- **F4 groups.** These are the 13 Box-Behnken formulations crossed with the three unpurified batches (39 groups). Purity is confounded with batch (one batch per purity), and I flagged that in claim judgements (replication). Group 5 was cast twice per run (5-1, 5-2); I kept it as one group.
- **The Fig. 5 legend says "(15 × 10^6 cells/mL)"**, but the models include cell number and Table S1 gives 9/12/15 × 10^6. I followed Table S1 and noted this on unit F5. The schema has no `suspected_error` on a unit.

## Vocabulary agent

- **Platform "custom PDMS molds"** names a mould used for three different formats: 5 mm acellular compression discs (F1), 3 × 17 mm acellular tensile strips with end posts (F2) and 3 × 9 mm cellular constructs with 0.5 mm posts at either end (F4 onward). One wording is mapped once per paper, so it went to a new root *hydrogel cast in a mould, format not stated*, not to *tissue between two pillars*. The reader may want to give the cellular units their own platform wording (the trough with end posts) so they can meet other post-held tissues; F1/F2 may fit *cell-free material sample*.
- **"isometric compaction"** was mapped to *isometric load* on the paper's own word, although the constructs sit between 0.5 mm PDMS posts whose stiffness is not given.
- **Cell lines.** "Gibco human episomal iPSCs" took the existing id CVCL_RM92 (CBiPSC6.2, the Gibco episomal line per an earlier lookup). "GCaMP6 WTC hiPSCs" adopted CVCL_E6IL (UCSFi001-A-79, WTC11-AAVS1-GCaMP6-C1). The paper does not name the clone, so the id may be one clone too specific.
- **Flags.** "hiPSC-CM or neonatal rat/mouse" (alternatives, Table 1). "connexin 43 expression and gap junction distribution" and "construct compaction and beating" (several measures). "alpha-actinin-positive cardiomyocyte content" (several measures, because the paper glosses it as "cell number or size").
- **Split requests.** The Table 1 cell types carry species and age ("neonatal rat") and a line (C2C12) in `cell_types`.
- **New terms.** Substrate *collagen sponge* (Davol Ultrafoam, identified from product knowledge; the paper says only "Davol ultrafoam") and *poly(glycerol sebacate)*. Measures *maximal rate of stress rise* (Vup is printed in mN/mm2/s, so it is not force per time) and *beating uniformity* (0/1/2 visual score).
- **Possible record issues.** `treatment: aprotinin` in F1/F2 is a gel-formulation factor and also appears in `supplements` ("B27 + aprotinin") for the cellular units. The native rat "discs"/"strips" sit in `preparation`, which hides the shape; mapped to *excised tissue sample*. "peak twitch stress against strain" (S6) carries only an R² and was mapped to *Frank-Starling response*.

## Formula agent

- **SMA+ only (Table S2) as SMA+ minus cTnT+ and SMA+.** The paper states no formula. I took the column name, beside "SMA+" and "cTnT+ and SMA+", as naming the set difference, anchored on the header. All five batches rebuild as consistent (43.09 against the printed 43.1). The cells may have been gated directly, and the agreement only shows that the table is self-consistent. The Discussion's "43.6" matches neither (already flagged).
- **"as much as 5-fold" read as the largest over the smallest Table 3 mean** (g11 0.54 / g4 0.11 = 4.91). This is consistent at the printed precision. The pair is my reading of "as much as".
- **Moduli as the slope of the linear region** (`E = ds/de`, linear_elastic_stress_strain), with a secant through the origin as the labelled alternative. The stress–strain curves are not released, so code cannot check them. The assumption reports give the fit ranges as quotes: 0–5 % strain in compression and 15–25 % in tension. The paper itself reports strain stiffening and "distinct toe regions under 20% strain" (rat tension), so the tensile fit sits above the toe region and is not small strain. Code can only say "reported", because `limit` takes value paths and a printed strain window is not an item value. Observation for code: a printed range has no way to feed a numeric assumption limit.
- **Peak active stress = F/A.** The force (Fmax at 30 % strain) and the cross-sectional area are per construct and not printed. The area comes from bright-field "initial sample dimensions", and its shape and formula (rectangle, ellipse, circle) are not stated. I set aggregation to mean of per-sample on the assumption that each construct's stress was averaged. The paper does not say this.
- **Vup normalized by peak stress (T3_Vup_norm, TS3_fVup_norm).** No values are printed. Code's rebuilt ratio of means treats Vup's unit "mN/mm2/s" as unknown, so it shows 0.0047 1/s for g1 where 2.29 / 0.49 = 4.67 1/s (mN/mm2 = kPa). Observation for code: the unit table lacks mN/mm2/s (= kPa/s), so these rebuilt values derived by LivingLit are off by a factor of 1000.
- **Upstroke velocities** (force and calcium) are `no_formula`. The paper names a "rate of rise", but not whether it is the maximum slope, and the calcium normalization ("normalized calcium amplitude/time") is not defined. T50/T90 are times read from traces, not derived from other items, so they have no derivation. Regression coefficients, model predictions and moduli items without values (F2B_gel*, S7) are also left out.
- **Bounds:** the 20 Table S2 percentages are under fraction_bounds, and all are consistent. No proposals: every step had a library entry.
