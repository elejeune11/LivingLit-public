## Record reader

- **Most units have no hiPSC-CMs.** Figs. 1–3, Online Table I and Online Figs. II and VI describe acellular scaffolds and cadaveric hearts. In those units every cell field is `not applicable`. Because these fields differ between units, `default_context` is empty and every unit writes its full context.
- **Kind of the decellularized scaffold.** Decellularized human or porcine matrix is written as `engineered`, since it is the paper's processed product. Cadaveric human myocardium (C) is written as `native tissue`, so every D-vs-C pair is *against benchmark*. Cadaveric tissue implanted in rats (Fig. 2, Online Fig. VI) also stays `native tissue`, though there it is an implant condition.
- **Host species vs. material species (Fig. 2, Online Fig. VI).** The macrophages and blood are from rats, but `species` holds the species of the implanted material (human, porcine). The rat host is in `other_conditions`. Sham rats have `species: not applicable` and `platform: none`.
- **No field for donor cause of death or myocardial region.** DCD vs. DBD (Figs. 1C and 3A–B, Online Fig. II) and epi/mid/endo (Fig. 3H–J) are written only in `other_conditions`. Code therefore sees no difference between those groups. In Fig. 3A–B the perfused solution (SDS, water, Triton, PBS) is written as `treatment`, though it is a stage of the decellularization process rather than a compared drug.
- **"No statistical difference" is not a P the parser reads.** Where the paper only prints a phrase ("no statistical difference", "did not reach statistical significance", "differences were not statistically significant"), I wrote the phrase as printed in `p` (Fig. 3H, Fig. 3A–B). `parse_p` does not recognise these phrases, so the items stay uncheckable. Table P values are printed bare under a "P Value" header, so I wrote them as `P = 0.3895`.
- **The asterisk on Fig. 5H.** There is an asterisk beside the 2 Hz fiber amplitude, but the legend does not say what it compares. I recorded it as fibers at 2 Hz vs. unpaced fibers (`F5G_fiber_2Hz`) with the Methods' paper-wide threshold (`*P < 0.05 (Methods)`). It could also mean fibers vs. slices at 2 Hz.
- **Test for fibers is not stated.** Methods name a two-way ANOVA with Tukey only for slice strain and slice force metrics. Fiber items have test `not stated`.
- **Gene time course (Fig. 4A).** The text describes direction changes between days without tests. I recorded each as a pair with `stated_direction`. "By day 7 through day 14 ... became expressed" was read as day 14 vs. day 5, which is an arbitrary choice. Two vaguer statements are not recorded as items: "tended to increase by day 30" and "by day 180 most cardiac genes appeared to taper".
- **Matrix strain at 2 Hz (Fig. 5B).** The text says it "decreased to 1.02%", but not against which group (1 Hz or unpaced), and no mark is printed. The value is in `F5B_matrix`; I made no pair.
- **Cell vs. matrix strain.** Cell strain and matrix strain are measured in the same slices, so they are recorded as two measures, not as groups. "Translated to" is left to the formula agent.
- **Proteomics lists not itemised.** Online Tables II and III are peptide lists of about 200 and about 970 proteins, with spectral counts and relative abundance. They are not recorded protein by protein; this omics list is treated like gene lists. The pie-chart and stacked-bar percentages printed in the Fig. 1E image are recorded with unit anchors.
- **Per-donor rows not recorded.** Online Table I's per-donor demographics (age, sex, height, weight for 73 hearts) are not recorded row by row. Its summary row is recorded, plus the recellularized hearts' cell numbers and reseeded volumes (in unit F7).
- **Supplement Methods describe an older cohort.** It gives n = 67, 38/29 allocation, 24 DCD, 32 M / 30 F, etc. These values are recorded with `suspected_error` beside the Results / Online Table I values. The supplement also cites old figure numbers (Fig 2A,B; Fig. 1K; Fig. 4; Supplemental Fig. VI); these are recorded as `cross_references`.
- **Model and validation outputs left out.** Fig. 6C is a representative LV pressure vs. strain loop in a decellularized porcine heart (bioreactor validation). Fig. 6F is bioreactor traces. Neither is evidence.
- **Image-only results left out.** Immunostaining, TEM, histology and representative traces (Figs. 1F, 2A, 2D, 3C–F, 4B, 5J–K, 7A, 7E, 7H–I) carry no values and are not items. Their qualitative statements appear only where a claim rests on them.
- **Context choices.**
  - `cm_day_at_formation` is "days 30 to 60" (Results), flagged because Methods say "at least 20 days".
  - `donor_age` is "postnatal" (Methods), flagged because the text calls the cells "adult-derived".
  - `transgene` is `none` from "nontransgenic".
  - `purification` is "unsorted".
  - `mechanical_load` for whole hearts is "matrix strain stimulation" from day 7. `maturation_strategy` is left `not stated`, so "biomimetic culture" is not written twice.
  - Fiber posts and preload are in `other_conditions`.
- **The 33.8% ratio does not match.** "33.8% of native murine embryonic heart tissue (53.0 µN)" does not follow from 14.93 µN (that gives about 28%). It is flagged as a suspected error on the value, not corrected.
- **Year.** The folder says 2015 (online publication and copyright); the print issue is Circ Res 2016;118:56-72. I wrote year "2015" and explained it in `version_note`.

## Vocabulary agent

- **Acellular matrix as a platform.** No platform term covered a decellularized matrix studied without cells. Added `platform:decellularized_cardiac_matrix` at the top level, not under `native_tissue`: putting it there would make acellular matrix "same at level 1" as native hearts. Its child `whole_heart_perfusion_decellularization` takes 'custom organ chamber' (F3AB). The record reader wrote an apparatus here, not a construct. Reseeded slices, fibers and the whole heart went under a new `platform:recellularized_matrix` (child of `engineered_3d`). 'myocardial slice', 'cardiac fiber' and 'whole-heart scaffold' are stored as mappings, not synonyms, because those words alone are broader than the terms.
- **Split requests.** 'decellularized human/porcine cardiac matrix' in `platform` also carries species. Species is already filled in the same contexts and agrees, so these are split requests, not flags.
- **Flag, donor_age 'postnatal'.** This is not a declared donor_age state, and the record's own suspected error gives a second wording ('adult-derived'). I used the kind *alternatives* because no kind fits a non-state word in a quantity field. If BJ fibroblasts count as neonatal, a declared state ('neonatal' or 'newborn') would hold this, if the reader confirms it.
- **Flag, measure 'extracellular matrix components'** (F1C_dcd_dbd). One item covers all four ECM assays: *several measures*.
- **'murine' → species:mouse,** with no synonym, because 'murine' can include rat. The words are the paper's, quoting a cited benchmark.
- **'full-thickness samples'** covers both biaxial testing (F1GH) and µCT (F3GJ), and it is mapped once. Neither `intact_tissue` (living, whole) nor `material_specimen` (cell-free, standard shape) fit, so I added `preparation:excised_tissue_sample`.
- **'adhered slices' → intact_tissue,** not `tissue_slice`, which is defined as an organotypic native slice.
- **'media samples' (T1)** covers both fresh baseline medium and coronary-sinus perfusate, so it is mapped to the broader `collected_medium`. T1's HCO3, TCO2, Lac and Glu go to `spent_medium_concentration`, but the baseline group is fresh, unspent medium. pH, pO2, pCO2 and sO2 got new medium measures.
- **'unsorted'** became a new purification term. The paper states no enrichment step, but 'unsorted' only says that the cells were not sorted.
- **Ids not found:** 'endonuclease' is not in ChEBI. Only the narrower desoxyribonuclease is, and the paper does not name the enzyme, so it was added as an own term. 'differentiating iPS cells' is not in CL, so it was added as an own term.
- **Haematology (S6):** I added a small `blood_count_parameter` family. The dimension of RDWa is a guess (volume, fL), because the record carries no unit.
- **Possible record issues (not fixed):** F2B_counts holds a second quantity ('fields/group'). F1D_total holds both a % reduction and a protein count. F2C holds both MFI values and a count of reactive beads; I mapped each to one measure. The ECM assays (F1C) carry `normalised_to: tissue wet weight` from the legend, but the dsDNA wording is per wet weight in F1B and per dry weight in S2, so its normalisation is left to the values. The record reader already flagged the F5I blebbistatin concentration ('20 mmol/L') as a suspected error.
- **Standing keys:** I wrote 31 keys, loose (platform@1 plus preparation/treatment, and species may vary for matrix measures). Whole-heart LV pressure measures use the functional template with pacing. No key requires `method` or `derivation`.
- **Re-map against vocabulary v52 (2026-11-12).** The stored mapping 'homogenized' → lysate is removed: v46 added 'homogenized' as a lysate synonym, so code now matches it. I checked every other mapping against the terms added in v45–v52, and none gives a more specific term. `treatment:dnase_i` (v49) sits under endonuclease, but the paper does not name its enzyme, so 'endonuclease' stays. The 'postnatal' flag stays. No new edits.

## Formula agent

- **33.8% of murine force (F5F_slice_murine.pct):** 14.93/53.0 = 28.2% and 10.30/53.0 = 19.4%; neither gives 33.8%. Code reports the derivation as inconsistent (ratio_def). The record reader had already labelled the value a suspected error. Which slice force was divided is not stated, so the paced force is listed as an alternative.
- **Anisotropy ratio:** the Methods define |Ex − Ey|/(Ex + Ey) per tissue sample, but not which axis is x. The formula is symmetric, so I mapped Ex to SFW and Ey to BA. I proposed `normalized_absolute_difference` (derived from ratio_def). Applying it to the group means gives 0.214 (C) and 0.098 (D), against printed 0.314 and 0.167. That gap is what a mean of per-sample ratios can produce, so it is not a flag. No per-sample moduli are available, so code cannot check it.
- **BMI:** the paper never says it computed BMI, and it may come from donor records. I treated "body mass index" as a named formula and proposed `ratio_to_square` (derived). Aggregation is a mean of per-donor values. Online Table I lists per-donor height, weight and BMI, but there is no data.yaml, so code cannot rebuild it. The ratio of means is 27.9 against a printed 27.76.
- **Vascular resistance (F3B):** the formula is not printed. I read "resistance calculated under constant pressure" as P/Q with P = 60 mmHg (Online Methods). The flows are plotted only and have no readings, so code cannot check them.
- **Not recorded as derivations:**
  - max/min dP/dt (a signal derivative) and the dominant frequency (FFT): these are signal processing, not closed-form steps.
  - Cell coverage (F7F): linear interpolation of MHC-positive area.
  - The "≈500 million" cells: the mean of the six injected counts is 497.6, but the text does not call it a mean.
  - HLA MFI "normalized to the sham group": whether this is a ratio or a subtraction is not stated.
  - F1E relative abundances: each group's five categories sum to 99.9% (C) and 100.0% (D). No total is printed, so there is no output to judge.
- **The tau fit cites Online Methods ref. 19, which is not in the record's citations.** The input is therefore marked not stated instead of by reference.
- **Spec/gate:** derivations whose inputs are all not stated (normalisations, ddCt, percentages of cells) can only be checked against the library symbolically. I wrote one per item without an output where the item has no printed values. For items that print several groups (F2B, F3H) I wrote one derivation without a group or output, because no group has an input that could be rebuilt.
