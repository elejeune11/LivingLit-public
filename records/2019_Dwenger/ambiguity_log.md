## Record reader

- **One file only.** The folder holds the main PDF alone. The paper names no supplementary files, so `missing` and `not_used` are empty.
- **`number_of_lines`.** The paper uses one line (201B6) but never prints a count, and the rule forbids computing a value, so I wrote *not stated* rather than "1".
- **Cell fractions: `composition` not `purity`.** The sentence "The final average CM/EC/MC% concentration was 60%/13%/12% …" gives three cell-type fractions, so it fills `composition` (per group); `purity` stays *not stated* rather than copying the CM share into a second field. The same numbers are also the measure of unit TXT1, so they appear both as group context and as item values.
- **`composition`, `day_of_measurement`, `maturation_strategy`, `transgene` differ between the three tissue groups, so they are written in every unit (in groups, or in the unit's context where a unit has one group or an unidentified tissue). They are therefore absent from `default_context`, as the structure rule requires.
- **Combination values must fit one quote.** Context anchors need a quote holding every word of the value, so `differentiation_factors` is "Activin A + Wnt3a" with the later factors as qualifiers (`added_at_d1: BMP4 + hbFGF`, `added_at_d5: VEGF165`); the same sentence-bound wording shaped `supplements` and `substrate`.
- **`purity_method: "flow cytometer"`.** The paper prints "BD LSRII Flow Cytometer", never "cytometry", and a context value must be worded from its quote.
- **Culture temperature has no field.** `temperature` is the measurement temperature, which is nowhere stated (the bath is "temperature-regulated" and "warmed"), so it is *not stated*; the incubator's 37 °C sits as a qualifier of `incubator_atmosphere`.
- **BDM.** 2,3-butanedione monoxime is used to halt shortening during mounting and then washed out, so it is an `other_conditions` entry on the force-testing units, not a `treatment`.
- **D28 is "prolonged culture", not a maturation strategy.** The D28 group's difference is carried by `day_of_measurement: D28`; its `maturation_strategy` is *none*.
- **Figure 2, 3 and 5(b,c,h,j) are representative single tissues.** I recorded their printed numbers as items with `kind: value`, and the pacing-rate panels of Figure 2 as frequency groups with stated directions (no values are printed).
- **Stress–frequency and hysteresis curves (Fig. 4e, f)** print no values or tests; recorded as `stated_direction` items. A "less negative" relationship is written as direction *up*.
- **Per-gene items.** Figure 6 and 7 print a bar per gene; I wrote one item per named gene, with the legend's blanket sentence ("… were unchanged after C-OP") as the anchor and `stated_direction: none`. Only KCND3 carries a value ("twofold") and a P.
- **Conflicting replicate counts (Fig. 6).** Methods say "2–3 biological replicates", the Figure 6 legend "2–4 biological experiments", the Results "biologic triplicates". I kept the legend's wording in `n.printed` and logged the conflict as `suspected_error` on the KCND3 item; the schema has no place for several printed n.
- **Judgement convention.** For claims that assert no direction (a transfection worked, a protocol was demonstrated) I judged *direction: follows* rather than *cannot judge*, so that a non-directional claim is not pushed to a "cannot judge" verdict by a dimension it does not engage.
- **"Force–length … confirm functional maturation" (section title)** is judged *presence: does not follow*: no force–length result is reported, only the determination of Lmax in the methods.
- **Six cross-references** point at the wrong figure (Figure 1 for Figure 4 twice, Figure 4 for Figure 5, Figure 5 for Figure 6, Figure 3 for Figure 6). They are recorded, not corrected.
- **Left out and logged:** the stated limitation that no transfected non-paced subgroup exists (a limitation, not a claim or an item); "3 Hz (180 beats per minute)" in the Figure 2 legend (a unit restatement of the pacing rate, not a measurement).

## Vocabulary agent

- **"normalized systolic energy" has two units.** Figure 3 prints it as 0.53 dimensionless and Figure 4(h) as J/m3-s, for a quantity the paper defines as the systolic stress-time integral normalised to time and to maximum stress. I gave the new measure `dimension: dimensionless` (its definition) and left the printed units as the record has them; a formula or audit job may need to reconcile the J/m3-s readings.
- **"cardiomyocyte fraction" counted from one marker.** The paper states a CM fraction but counts cTnT-positive nuclei. `measure:cell_type_fraction_by_number` says "by any counting method" while its scope excludes marker-positive fractions; I mapped to it (the paper states a cell-type share, not a marker share) and logged the tension between the two terms. The same term, with `cell_type`, takes the flow-cytometry CM/EC/MC percentages of the cell mixture.
- **"L-type calcium and sodium channel mRNA expression"** names more than one gene but is a single summary statement in the legend, so I mapped it to `measure:gene_set_expression` (gene_set: L-type Ca2+ and Na+ channel genes) rather than flagging *several measures*; the genes behind the summary are not listed. Spec leaves the line between a gene set and several measures unclear.
- **"beat-to-beat hysteresis" is normalised in some figures and not in others** (Fig. 2 is amplitude variation in the force trace; σbb in Figs 3–4 is divided by mean active stress). Following the rule for wordings normalised differently across figures, the new measure carries no `expressed_as`/`normalised_to` in its mapping; its standing key requires both.
- **"transfection efficiency"** (pilot experiments, read-out not stated) was mapped to `measure:marker_positive_cells` with marker ChIEF-tdTomato rather than given a term of its own; if later papers measure it by other means this may want splitting.
- **"patched single cells within the ECT"** → `preparation:intact_tissue`: the cells are patched in situ, not dissociated. **"stained cells"** (flow cytometry of the pre-formation cell mixture) → `preparation:dissociated_cells`.
- **No synonyms for "linear ECT", "thin-coat Matrigel" or "optical MCR".** Each is broader than (or a modality of) the term mapped: linear ECT > TissueTrain, thin-coat > growth-factor-reduced Matrigel, optical MCR is the maximum capture rate with the stimulus carried by `pacing`. They are stored as mappings instead.
- **"penicillin-streptomyocin" (sic)** is one wording for two ChEBI ids (penicillin, streptomycin), so it got a mapping and no synonym: a synonym belongs to one term.
- **No values were flagged**; one judgement that could have been a flag is the L-type summary above.

## Formula agent

- **"Normalized systolic energy" printed in J/m3-s.** The methods normalise the stress-time integral "to both time and σmax", which is dimensionless (Figure 3 prints 0.53 dimensionless); Figure 4(h) and the Results print J/m3-s. Neither normalisation reading gives J/m3-s (time only gives J/m3). I wrote the stated formula (`normalized_time_integral`, dimensionless) with "normalised to time only" as a labelled alternative and left `output_unit` unset, so code meets the unit conflict. Observation for code, not a correction.
- **σbb pairs.** The methods say "for each pair of successive cycles"; the Figure 3 legend says each even-numbered cycle against the preceding odd one. Same formula, different pairs: logged as an alternative label only.
- **Pacing frequency of the σA means (0.72, 0.52, 0.17 mN/mm²) is not stated** (stress is computed "for each electric field pacing frequency"); RT50 and energy are at 3 Hz. The derivations carry no x.
- **Shared stress/area steps.** The area and stress steps are written once (keyed on F4ch_sigma_a_cop_ctrl, no group) and every σA, σbb and energy step points at them, since their inputs (force, diameter) are per sample and never printed. Spec says one derivation per group only where inputs differ; I read "all not stated" as not differing.
- **Means of group means.** The printed MCR increase "1 Hz" (Discussion) is the only derivation code can rebuild (5.37 − 4.35 = 1.02, consistent). `kind: constant` for group means: the spec's kinds fit parameters better than summary values.
- **CM/EC/MC percentages sum to 85 %, 80 % and 87 %**, not 100 %; the remainder is unexplained (other cells or unstained). No library entry bounds a sum of parts, so only each share's 0–1 bound is applied.
- **F5 fractions.** "20 ± 6% of CM were transfected" and "67 ± 14% of all transfected cells were CM" are written as double-positive / cTnT⁺ and double-positive / tdTomato⁺ nuclei; the methods record only "percent cTnT-positive, tdTomato-positive and double-positive nuclei", so the denominators are read from the Results wording. Together with the 48 % CM fraction they imply a mean transfected share of ~14 % (the representative section shows 13 %), but that mean is not printed, so no chain was written.
- **qPCR by citation.** Relative expression is "as described in our previous work (ref 12)"; every gene item gets a `no_formula` step pointing at r12. KCND3 "twofold" maps to `ratio_def` (named operation), with 2^-ΔΔCt as a labelled alternative rather than the library formula, since the paper never names ΔΔCt.
- **Not written:** RT50 (a time read off the trace), MCR and intrinsic beat rate (determined from 2:1 block / Fourier analysis / video, not computed from other values).
