## Figures and data reader

- **Panels read (vector, 23 entries, exact counts).** Figs. 1D, 1F, 2C–E, 3B, 3D, 4C–D, 5D–E, 6D–E, 7C, 8C–E, 9C–E, 10B, 10D–E. Prism plots: each marker is a vector sub-path. ## Formula agent

- Force and stress are stated only as "calculated ... based on the deflection of the pillars and the measured pillar spring constant ..., as described previously (41)". I wrote force as F = k*x (hooke_spring) from those words and stress as s = F/A (stress_def) with the area by reference to ref41. Whether the area is one constant or measured per CMT is not stated, so its kind is `not stated`; code's P check finds F4C_het (P = 0.01) and F4D_het (n.s.) differ in significance, which rules out one constant area.
- Which deflection enters the force (one pillar, mean of both, or the sum) is not stated; labelled alternatives.
- The spring constant is printed as 2.68 µN/m (recorded as stated); for microtissue gauges this is implausibly soft (µN/µm would be expected). Observation for code; not corrected.
- 2^-ΔΔCt has no library entry; proposed `ddct_relative_expression` as a model (assumes ~100 % and equal efficiencies, citing Livak and Schmittgen 2001). The printed 63.2 % / 32.5 % are group means of per-sample values, so a single-sample derivation's `output` is only approximately the right target; no Ct values are printed, so nothing is rebuilt.
- RMS stress: proposed `root_mean_square` (S = sqrt(Q/n), Q the sum of squares). Cited to MathWorld, not a textbook; replace with a textbook citation if the given-entry rule requires it.
- TFM work (field sum of F·u), FRAP mobile fraction and order-parameter plateaus (curve-fit plateaus) have no closed-form entry: marked no_formula (work, mobile fraction) or left out (order parameter, displacement metrics from optical flow, which are reductions of image fields).
- The spec does not say how a derivation's `output` should name a group-mean printed value when the formula is per sample.
