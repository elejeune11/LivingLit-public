## Record reader

- **Medium is the experimental contrast, and calcium is borrowed from the 2D section.** The paper names its two 3D conditions by medium ("conventional" RPMI/B27+ vs "multilineage" Advanced DMEM/F12 + ascorbic acid). I wrote them as `cm_medium` + `supplements` + `insulin_day` + `culture_calcium_mM` in each group. The calcium values (0.42 / 1.05 mM) are printed only where the 2D patch-clamp experiment is introduced ("conventional cardiac media (Ca2+ = 0.42 mM), multilineage media (... 1.05 mM)"). I applied them to the 3D groups because they describe the same named media. A stricter reading would make them *not stated* for the 3D units.
- **Ascorbic acid dose.** Methods give "100µM L-Ascorbic Acid" for the 3D medium. Results and the Fig. S4 legend give "AA = 100 µg/mL" (about 570 µM). For the 3D groups I recorded 100 µM, with a `suspected_error` on the context value. For the 2D groups I recorded 100 μg/ml, as their legend prints it.
- **Significance bars spanning several days** (Fig. 2C: **** over days 10–50; Fig. 4C amplitude: **** over days 30–50; upstroke: days 40–50; downstroke: ** over days 70–80). I wrote one pair item per day under the bar. In Fig. 4C the amplitude bar seems to start at day 30, but it could start at day 40. Fig. 4C has no day-60 multilineage point.
- **Two thresholds for "\*\*" in one legend** (Fig. 4C: "**p < 0.01, **p < 0.005"). I wrote `p: **p < 0.01` and the second threshold under `p_values`. Figs. S1E and S1I print **p<0.005 in the legend but ** p < 0.01 in Methods; both are recorded the same way. The "##" in S1I has no stated threshold.
- **Patch-clamp subtype groups (Fig. 4J, S4D).** "Vent." and "Atrial/Nodal" are classes found by the measurement itself, and no field holds them. I made them groups distinguished by `other_conditions`. Code will therefore see no differing field between the two subtypes of one condition. Subtype is a measurement outcome, so a "cell subtype" field would be needed if these are ever to be compared.
- **Frequency as group or x.** Fig. 4D and S4A–B plot beat rate against stimulation frequency and day. I made one group per medium × frequency, and day is the x of the points (Fig. 4D unit `day_of_measurement: not applicable`, with a note). The 3, 4 and 6 Hz levels appear only in figure text, so their context anchors are `kind: unit`.
- **Qualitative image comparisons** (Fig. 1H Ki67, Fig. 2J Ki67 decline, bulk RNA-seq "higher levels" statements in Fig. 5) are recorded as pair items with `stated_direction` and no test, because claims rest on them. Many other image-only observations are not items: epicardial layer, MLC2v compartmentalization, gut cell types, EM ultrastructure, Fig. S1A–B native heart tissue, S3, S5. The Fig. 5A statement "all spheroids (n > 200) within a single batch ... identical morphologies" is also not an item.
- **Classifier outputs** (Fig. 3H, S2G) are recorded as measurements of the day-100 organoids, not as model outputs. The classifier assigns identity to measured cells. The text's "98%"/"1%" are percentages of cardiomyocytes; the figure's 39.87%/0.43% are percentages of all 3012 cells. I named these values differently so they are not compared as one quantity.
- **Seeding density over several steps.** iPSC differentiation (3x104 cells/cm2), spheroid formation (2.4x104 cells/well) and replating for patch clamp or imaging (0.03x106 cells/cm2) are all stated. The value holds the step that forms the measured construct, and the other steps are qualifiers.
- **Fig. 1H day.** The panels show day 20 and day 30, but the comparison is stated without a day, so `day_of_measurement` is *not stated* there.
- **Fig. 4A conditions.** Methods for calcium imaging cover days 30–365 only, while Fig. 4A starts at day 10. Temperature, bath and calcium are *not stated* for this unit.
- **Treatment / disease / genotype.** No drug is applied anywhere, so `treatment` is *not applicable*. The paper never calls WTC11 a healthy or control line, so `genotype` and `disease_model` are *not stated*.
- **mmc12.pdf** is main.pdf followed by mmc1.pdf in one file (checked by text diff). It is listed in `not_used`.
- **Missing videos.** Videos S1, S2, S5–S7 are cited for spontaneous contraction, the SMC calcium transients and stimulation-only contraction. They are not in the folder.

## Figures and data reader (F4EG)

At day 30 the grey (conventional) and blue (multilineage) markers overlap into solid clusters, so no marker stands alone. ## Figures and data reader (F5)

- **Fig. 5C read (raster, main.pdf p. 11, `figures/f5c_mds.py`).** The MDS plot is part of one embedded raster image at its native 300 dpi. Each point is one sample: value = leading logFC dim 2 (y), `x` = leading logFC dim 1. The axes are calibrated from tick marks the script finds in the image. Points counted: multilineage 1 and cardiac 2, matching the printed n (1 and 2).
- **Gut markers overlap.** The two gut markers merge into one blob, so they do not stand alone and give no points (`countable: false`). Only the blob centre is stored, as the gut `mean` (dim 2 value, dim 1 as `x`). For two equal circles that centre is the mean of the two samples. This is a judgement. A check that needs the two gut samples separately cannot use it.
- **Calibration.** The x tick marks are unevenly spaced in the image itself (gaps of 57.5, 65.5, 62 and 58 px). The x misfit is therefore about 0.046 logFC (about 2.8 px); the y misfit is about 0.012. `Reading.x` has no interval, so the dim 1 uncertainty (about ±0.056) is not stored. It is given here and in the figures.yaml note.
- **Not read.** Fig. 5B is a schematic. Fig. 5D/5E are heat maps with no per-group values. Fig. 5F/5G and S5H–S5K are outside the item groups this unit lists. No released data hold MDS coordinates: mmc2.xlsx and mmc3.xlsx are gene lists, and both are already under `not_used`.

## Vocabulary agent

- **Cell line GCaMP-WTC11.** Cellosaurus has no entry for the Conklin-lab GCaMP6f WTC11 subclone. CVCL_E6IL (WTC11-AAVS1-GCaMP6-C1, ISMMSi059) is another lab's clone, so I did not use it. Mapped to the parental WTC11 (CVCL_Y803) with `no_ontology_term`; the reporter is already in `transgene`.
- **Mesendoderm progenitor cells.** CL has no mesendoderm class, only mesodermal cell and endodermal cell, each narrower. Added own term `cell_types:mesendoderm_progenitor`.
- **'lysed with Trizol'** mapped to `preparation:rna_extract`, because the methods go on to say RNA was extracted (Direct-zol) for bulk RNA-seq. The reader may want to write the preparation as the RNA extract.
- **'ADP50'** mapped to action potential duration with level `APD50`, not the printed 'ADP50'. The record already labels 'ADP50' a suspected misprint, and the methods say APD50.
- **F3H_atrial_cm mixes denominators.** Its values are shares of cardiomyocytes (98 %, 98.44 %, >90 %) and a share of all cells (39.87 %, `pct_of_cells_fig`). Mapped to cardiomyocyte subtype proportion (a share of CMs). The all-cells value should go in its own item (or get `normalised_to`) from the record reader. The other F3H classes are shares of all cells: new measure `cell_type_proportion`.
- **'size' (≈3-fold).** Mapped to tissue projected area expressed as fold of conventional microtissues, because the sentence goes with the surface-area panel (Fig. 1E). 'Size' itself is vague.
- **'spontaneous beating' (F4A).** The text calls the multilineage values 'spontaneous calcium activity' and the conventional value 'spontaneous contractility'. I kept this as one measure (new `beating_construct_fraction`, mode spontaneous) and did not flag it.
- **'surface area'.** Mapped to tissue projected area (brightfield). I added no synonym, because elsewhere 'surface area' may mean a 3D surface.
- **Flags:** '% area SMA+ and Pecam-1+' is *several measures* (two markers in one item). 'cells analysed by scRNA-seq' (3439 cells) is *not a context*: it is the analysis's n, not a measure.
- **AP 'Frequency'.** Added `action_potential_frequency` under action potential and did not map it to contraction frequency (single patch-clamped cells versus tissue beat rate). A later re-map may decide they are the same measure.
- **'contraction size' (>100 µm).** The existing image-based contraction amplitude is dimensionless, so I added the child `contraction_displacement` (length).

## Formula agent

- **AP amplitude = peak − MDP (Fig. 4J, S4D).** The paper names amplitude, peak and MDP as Clampfit parameters but never prints how amplitude is computed. I treated 'amplitude' as a named operation (peak minus MDP, mapped to `sum_of_parts`) and anchored it on the parameter list. The take-off-potential definition is listed as a labelled alternative. Code rebuilds all 11 groups as consistent. F4J ml_d60_an (23.58 + 56.8 = 80.38 against a printed 80.44) and S4D suppl_v, ml_v and ml_an (each 0.1 off) are consistent only because the rebuilt interval just reaches the printed value.
- **Counts in image tables cannot be inputs.** The patch-clamp cell counts (Fig. 4J and S4D, `n.per_group`) and the classifier total (3012) are printed only in images. A `printed` input needs an anchor that is found in the text, and a value path cannot point at `<item>.n` per group. So the F4K shares (4/7 = 57.1 %, 8/11 = 72.7 %, 8/10 = 80 %: all match the printed 57/73/80) and S4F (12/13 = 92.3 % against a printed 97 %, which the record already labels a suspected error) are **not recorded as derivations**. Observation for code: allow `<item>.n.<group>` as an input path, or image-table anchors for printed inputs.
- **Classifier total rebuilt from the class counts.** The 3012 cells are rebuilt as the sum of the nine Fig. S2G counts (three `sum_of_three_parts` steps). The sum equals the record's n exactly.
- **'Organoid cardiomyocytes' denominator.** This is not stated. I took it as atrial CM + ventricular CM + 'cardiomyocytes' (1220 cells), which reproduces 98.44 / 1.07 / 0.49 %.
- **Inequality outputs skipped.** `pct_of_cm_results` ('>90') and F3H_subepi_capillary ('<4', rebuilt (99+4)/3012 = 3.42 %) are not outputs: the number parser drops the '<' and '>' and would judge them as point values. Observation for code.
- **Counts carry a ±0.5 printed interval.** A count is exact, so the rebuilt intervals of the shares are wider than they need to be. The unit 'cells' is also not known to `si_factor`, which gives a note on every count input.
- **Recorded but not checkable:** F1E 'approximately 3 times the size' (size taken as surface area, with the diameter alternative), the S1E cardiac:total volume ratio, and S1J % MLC2v area over cardiac area. These have no readable inputs. F4F circularity and aspect ratio, F4G class percentages and S1I % area have no values, and none of their formulas is printed, so I wrote no derivation.
