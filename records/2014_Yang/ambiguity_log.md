## Record reader

- **Differentiation factors span two sentences.** Activin A/BMP4 and CHIR 99021/Xav 939 are named in two consecutive sentences (36 words together), so no single quote of 30 words or fewer holds all four. I wrote the full set as one value and anchored it on the value itself (`ContextObj.anchor`, the 30-word quote from "BMP4)" to "Xav 939"). That quote does not contain "activin A". The schema has no multi-quote context anchor; this record wants one.
- **Second cell line (RUES2) in one text sentence.** The Methods describe only the IMR90 iPSC protocol. RUES2 cardiomyocytes appear once in the text, with fold changes only. So cell line and every protocol field (differentiation, media, purity, dissociation, day of formation) differ between units and sit in each unit's context, not the default. For TXT3 the protocol fields are `not stated`. The RUES2 control group is implied by "upregulation" and is not described; its T3 dose is not stated.
- **Order of "X vs Y" in the calcium paragraph.** The text gives no group names for "0.24 ± 0.03 vs 0.20 ± 0.02", "2.99 ± 0.64 vs. 7.43 ± 1.43" and the others. I assigned control first because "Vmax upstroke was faster" fits only that order, and Fig. 4's bars show the same order. Cell area and circularity are printed control first too; time to peak is printed T3 first ("versus … in control cells").
- **Fig. 3C bars against the text.** By eye the bars sit near 6.3 and 10.3 nN/cell, but the text cites Fig. 3C for 7.5 ± 0.4 and 12.3 ± 0.7. I logged this as a `suspected_error` on F3C_force for the figures reader. No value was read.
- **Fig. 3E asterisk.** Panel E carries "*", but the legend defines only "#P < 0.001 vs. control". I wrote `*P < 0.05 (Methods)` and logged a suspected error.
- **Fig. 2D threshold.** The legend prints "*P < 0.05" after panel C and "*P < 0.05 vs. control" after panel F; panel D has an asterisk and the words "significant increase". I used the legend's "*P < 0.05" for D.
- **Two-group tests named as ANOVA + Student–Newman–Keuls.** The Methods name one test for all comparisons. I recorded it as printed on every two-group item, without the Methods label. Fig. 1F (qPCR) and the RUES2 fold changes show no test, so their stats are `not stated`.
- **Abstract "significant increase in SERCA2a"** rests on Fig. 1F, which prints no test, P or n. I judged support *does not follow*. This is a judgement call; *cannot judge* is defensible.
- **"Almost two-fold higher force"** against printed means of 12.3 and 7.5 nN/cell (about 1.6-fold). I judged strength *overstated*; magnitude wording has no dimension of its own.
- **Micropost stiffness and size.** Field rules put post stiffness as a qualifier of `mechanical_load`, but the paper states no load type for single cells on posts. So stiffness (38.4 nN/μm) and post dimensions went to `other_conditions` of F3, and `mechanical_load` stays `not stated`.
- **T3 as treatment, not maturation_strategy.** T3 is the compared exposure, so it sits in `treatment` (20 ng/ml, one week). `maturation_strategy` is `not stated`, so the same fact is not held in two fields.
- **Pacing in the force assay.** Only the Discussion says "spontaneous contractile force generation". I used `pacing: spontaneous` from that sentence.
- **day_of_measurement** is not printed as a day: T3 starts after 20 days and runs one week. I did not compute day 27; it is `not stated`.
- **Other-study values.** Lundy's 1.65 ± 0.02 → 1.81 ± 0.01 μm and Taylor's 50–100 nN/cell went in as other-study groups (TXT6, TXT7). For Lundy's ± values the spread type is not stated, so they are kind `value`. Lundy's start group has no label in the paper; I worded one. The "0.1 nN to almost 100 nN [31–35]" range spans five studies and was not recorded as a group.
- **Supplemental videos.** The video files are not in the folder (listed as missing). The forces of the two representative cells (8.28 and 12.12 nN) are printed in the .docx captions and recorded from there.
- **Supplemental Figure 1** is an image inside the .docx. Only its caption's P = 0.36 and the main text's "~7% in both groups" were recorded. No n is printed.
- **Typos not tied to values:** the Fig. 2 legend has "Hoechst 44432" (33342 elsewhere), and the journal's Fig. 4 legend has "fura-2 M" where the author manuscript has "fura-2 AM". Both are logged only.
- **Fig. 5B** values are relative to control (control = 1) and are plotted only. Basal, maximal and non-mitochondrial OCR carry "#" and reserve capacity carries "*", read from the figure's marks. The only printed value is the Discussion's "1.5-fold" for maximal OCR.
- **Fig. 5A traces** and the representative force and calcium traces are not items (representative only).

## Vocabulary agent

- **cm_day_at_formation 'Twenty days' (F3) flagged** *not a context*, for want of a better kind: it is the same day as '20 days' in this paper's other units, printed in words. Code's number words stop at twelve, so it does not parse as a quantity, and a quantity field can't take terms. The spec gives no flag kind for "copies that differ only in wording". The reader could write '20 days', or the code could learn more number words.
- **Gene and protein qualifiers** are HGNC symbols (MYH6, MYH7, ATP2A2, CDKN1A, TTN (N2BA)/(N2B), MT-ND1), not the printed names (α-MHC, β-MHC, SERCA2a, p21). The spec doesn't say which, and "same gene" keys compare the text, so this only helps if other papers use symbols too.
- **'titin isoform expression (N2BA and N2B)'** mapped to transcript isoform fraction (N2BA share of N2BA + N2B), because the item states which isoform dominates. *Several measures* was the alternative.
- **'relative mitochondrial volume'** (TEM point counting) mapped to mitochondrial area fraction. Point counting on sections estimates the area fraction the paper calls volume. No synonym was added.
- **pacing 'stimulated' (0.5 Hz)** mapped to the root *electrical pacing*: field or point delivery isn't stated. No synonym, as the word is too generic.
- **platform 'glass slides', 'tissue culture dishes', 'Seahorse plates'** mapped to *2D culture* with `format`. Monolayer is not stated.
- **'HEPES-containing medium' (F3)** got a new root term *HEPES-buffered medium, base not stated*. The paper doesn't say it is the culture medium, though F4's "culturing medium containing 15 mM HEPES" suggests it is. The record's qualifier is named `HEPES`; the term declares `hepes`.
- **'RPMI-B27'** mapped to RPMI 1640 with a split request: B27 to supplements, which lists only L-glutamine.
- **No new measures**, so no standing keys were written. Every measure here already had a key.
- **Possible reader issue**: F4C's unit 'F/F0/s' sits oddly with a ratiometric fura-2 signal (worth a look). Nothing else.

## Formula agent

- **Micropost stiffness (38.4 nN/μm)** is printed as a condition ("based upon the dimensions of the microposts and the material properties of PDMS"), not as an evidence value, so its derivation has no output path and cannot be checked. Observation for code: under a small-deflection circular cantilever (k = 3πEd⁴/64L³) with d = 2.3 μm and L = 6.45 μm, 38.4 nN/μm corresponds to E ≈ 2.5 MPa; the modulus is not stated. A record value for k (or an `output` that can point at an `other_conditions` entry) would make it checkable.
- **Total force per cell** is a sum over a per-cell, unstated number of posts. No fixed-arity library entry covers it, so it is marked `no_formula`, with the per-post Hooke step as its `derived` input. The spec doesn't say how to express a variable-length sum.
- **F1F and TXT3 fold changes**: "normalized using HPRT" and "fold vs. control" are mapped to `ratio_def`. 2^-ΔΔCt is a labelled alternative because the Ct arithmetic is not stated. TXT3 (RUES2) gives no method or control at all, and its "decreased 0.73-fold" is read as a ratio of 0.73.
- **F4B amplitude** is printed in 'F/F0' units with values of 0.20–0.24 (below 1). That rules out a plain F/F0 peak ratio unless the baseline is above the peak, so the units and the values may disagree. Marked `no_formula`, with both readings as alternatives. The F4C/F4D velocities (maximum of a derivative) are not closed-form.
- **Seahorse chain**: basal is a mean of points 1–5, maximal = FCCP − (rot+AA), and reserve = FCCP − basal; all are mapped. The 1.5-fold is "relative T3 treated/untreated control levels" per replicate, and its aggregation is left `not stated`. The normalisation to Hoechst cell number is a further ratio step, not written, because none of its inputs are printed. "Total OCR" in the 20–30 % non-mitochondrial fraction is not defined.
- **Proposal circularity_def** (4πA/P², definition): cited to the ImageJ user guide by URL plus the paper's own statement. I did not have the guide's exact wording, so no quote is given. ISO 9276-6 uses the square-root form, which is noted under `does_not_apply`.
- **Point counting (S1E)**: the total number of points is implied by the 20 × 20 line grid but not printed, so it is left not stated.
