## Record reader

- **Paper mixes materials science and tissue work.** Ink rheology, Young's moduli, resistivity, gauge factor, layer thicknesses and Supplementary Table S1 are reported measurements, so I recorded them as units (F1, S1S2, S3, S4, S8S9, F3ad, ST1), with every cell and culture field set to *not applicable*. Because of that, nothing holds for every unit, and `default_context` is empty. The schema has no field for "material specimen" (dog-bone, cast film), so the specimen goes in the item's `method`.
- **Table S1 is image-only and holds model parameters** (z-positions from stylus profiling, Young's moduli). I recorded it as unit ST1 with one group per layer and `kind: unit` anchors. The groups have empty contexts because no context field separates cantilever layers. Its legend's sinusoid parameters (wavelength, amplitude, sheet thickness for 40/60/80 µm) are model inputs, so I left them out.
- **Asterisk over a single bar** (Fig. 2d over 60 µm; Fig. 4e,f over day 2; Fig. 4g over day 28). The post-hoc partners aren't marked, so Fig. 2d, 4e and 4f are each one omnibus item with `p: "∗, P < 0.05"`. The test comes from Methods (one-way ANOVA with Holm–Sidak). Fig. 4g is a pair (day 28 vs day 14) because the text names it as significant.
- **Two EC50s printed twice with different values** (Fig. 3i legend 1.12 × 10−6 M vs text 1.15 × 10−6 M; Fig. 3j legend 2.74 × 10−9 M vs text 2.69 × 10−9 M). I recorded both values in each case and marked the text value as a suspected error. I didn't pick one.
- **Fig. 4b,c legend lists days 2, 4, 8, 20 and 28**, but the axes show six bars, including day 14. I marked this as a suspected error on both items.
- **Dose-response units** have two groups: no drug (`treatment: none`, `time_point: baseline`) and the drug given in cumulative doses (`time_point: "10 min for each dose"`). Cumulative dosing ties the drug to time, so code will see these pairs as confounded. That is true of the design, so I didn't hide it. Directions come only from `stated_direction`, because no group values or tests are printed. Dose levels aren't printed, so the individual doses aren't separate groups.
- **Force-frequency examples (S16, S20, S24)** show one trace each at 1, 2 and 3 Hz. I wrote one group per frequency and a measurement item with no direction, because "negative force-frequency relationship" covers three levels and the paper prints no pair. Claims C37–C40 rest on these items and leave `asserts_direction` unset.
- **`cell_line: "Cor4U"`.** This is the commercial product name (Axiogenesis) as printed. The underlying iPSC line isn't stated. The vocabulary agent may flag it.
- **`preparation: "laminar tissue"`** for live recordings. "Intact tissue" isn't worded anywhere, so I took the paper's own term from a quote. Fixed samples are `fixed`.
- **Filament spacing** is a qualifier of `substrate` ("soft PDMS filaments"). Units outside Fig. 2 and S14 carry 60 µm, from "Standard substrate of soft PDMS filaments spaced by 60 µm" (S11, S19). For the thicker-tissue units I gave no spacing, because none is stated for them.
- **Incubator measurement temperature** isn't stated, so it is *not stated*. "inside an incubator" goes in `other_conditions`. Matrigel added at seeding for thicker tissues also goes in `other_conditions`, because no field holds a seeding additive. RH237 dye goes in `other_conditions` for optical mapping.
- **NRVM fibronectin coating** is recorded under `replating_coating` (coating of the device wells at seeding). For primary NRVMs this is first plating, not replating.
- **S18 measurement medium**: the legend says "regular 199 media", while Methods says serum-free media is added before drug-dose experiments. I used the legend's words for S18 and Methods for the other drug units.
- **Gauge factor "2.51 ± 0.09"** is printed with an unlabelled ±. I kept it as one string with `spread: not stated` rather than guessing between SD and SEM.
- **The "factor of four" increase from day 2 to day 4** is recorded with printed "four", kind ratio, `between: [d4, d2]`.
- **Fig. 2h velocities and tmax** come from one representative tissue per spacing, printed as figure text. I recorded them as values with n "Representative".
- **Native ventricle ratio 2.1 [18]** is an `other study` group in F2gi with `platform: native ventricle`.
- **Not recorded:** Supplementary Fig. S10 (ABS and PLA wells; example readouts only, no cell type or measure stated), S5 (qualitative 0.1% vs 1% strain behaviour), the images in S11, S19, S21 and S23, and the MATLAB scripts (the conversion factor 3.107528138998242e+004 is a model output). Claim C42 (viability unaffected by PLA/ABS wells) rests on nothing, because no viability data are shown.
- **`nihms819005.pdf`** is the author manuscript of the same paper, with the same values. It goes in `not_used`, and anchors point to the journal version.
- **`pages_dont_commit/`**, a subfolder of earlier page renders in the paper folder, isn't a source file, so I didn't use or list it.

## Figures and data reader

- **F2gi (Fig. 2g), read as a vector figure** (`figures/f2g_velocity_ratio.py`, journal_version.pdf p. 3). The panel has grey mean bars, one s.e.m. cap per bar and open circles. The y axis is calibrated from the four drawn tick strokes (0–3) and their labels. I read all four spacings, s60 included, so the plotted mean can be compared with the printed 2.7.
- **Counts.** The legend says only n ≥ 3. The plot shows 4 (40 µm), 3 (60), 3 (80) and 3 (100) circles. For every spacing, the circles' mean and s.e.m. agree with the bar top and cap to within 0.01. That suggests every point is drawn and none is hidden behind a bar.
- **Printed 2.7 against the plotted 60 µm mean.** The bar reads 2.765 and the points average 2.76. Rounded, that is 2.8, not 2.7. I recorded both as they are and corrected neither.
- The green band and dashed line at ratio 1 are not data. Fig. 2h–i (representative maps and traces) have values printed as text, so I read nothing from them. No released data files.
- **F4dg (Fig. 4d–g, Supplementary Fig. S19): no points.** Fig. 4e–g (journal_version.pdf p. 5) are vector bars (grey, blue, red for day 2, 14, 28) with s.e.m. whisker strokes and no per-tissue markers, so no script was written. The legend gives n = 5, but no points can be counted against it. Panel d and S19 are micrographs. The day-2/day-14 means for OOP (0.11, 0.32) and SPD (0.07, 0.2) and the day-14/day-28 sarcomere lengths (1.7, 1.8 µm) are printed in the text only. No released data hold them.

## Vocabulary agent

- **Device and material measures.** This paper is half device engineering, so I added material and device measures: printed layer thickness, filament width and z-position, ink viscosity, material resistivity and conductivity, gauge factor, sensor resistance. They have no parent, and their keys are loose (platform, substrate). Which layer or ink a value belongs to sits only in group labels (F1, S1S2, S3, S4, ST1), not in any context field. So cross-paper value strata for these measures would mix layers. A `measure.layer` qualifier set per value, or a field for the device component, would fix that.
- **Flag, several measures:** 'storage and loss moduli' (S8_moduli, S9_moduli) names two measures in one item. It should be split into a storage modulus item and a loss modulus item.
- **'relative resistance change'** is mapped to the new sensor electrical resistance with `expressed_as: % change` and `normalised_to: baseline resistance R0` (the paper prints σ~ΔR/R0). The same wording is used for F3ad (gauge testing, no tissue) and S14m (tissue readout).
- **EC50 of verapamil.** The paper calls this an 'apparent EC50' even though verapamil lowers stress. I kept it as EC50, as printed, not IC50. I added an `ec50` qualifier `compound` because the existing `agonist` does not fit verapamil. I used `compound` for isoproterenol too, so the two now sit apart from earlier papers' `agonist`. A re-map should settle on one name.
- **purification 'puromycin-mediated positive selection'** is the reader's wording; the paper says puromycin is added 'to eliminate un-differentiated stem cells'. I mapped it to genetic selection (qualifier agent: puromycin). That puromycin works through a cardiac-promoter resistance gene is the vendor's design and is not stated in the paper. The reader may want to use the printed words.
- **preparation 'laminar tissue'** names a format, not a preparation. I mapped it to live cells, whose scope covers live 2D or patterned cultures, not to intact tissue, which is 3D. I added no synonym.
- **cell_types 'neonatal rat ventricular myocytes'** maps to CL:2000046 with split requests: 'neonatal' goes to donor_age and 'rat' goes to species. Both fields are already filled and agree.
- **'paced'** maps to electrical pacing with no synonym, since in other papers it could mean optical pacing. **'native ventricle'** (a cited benchmark) maps to native heart tissue with region ventricle; its species is not stated.
- **'normalized twitch stress'** (S20, S24) is `expressed_as: normalised`. What it is normalised to is not printed.
- **Possible reader slip:** the supplement prints '1.5 µL vitamin B-12', which is probably µM. It was not recorded as a quantity, so nothing in the record is affected.

## Formula agent

- **Ag:PA conductivity is flagged inconsistent because of how code parses the number, not because of the paper.** 1/(6.60×10−5 Ω·cm) = 15 151 S/cm, interval [15 140, 15 163]. That overlaps the printed 1.52·10⁴ S/cm, interval [15 150, 15 250]. But `numbers.parse_number("1.52·10⁴")` returns 1.52: a middle dot followed by a superscript exponent isn't parsed, while "6.60x10-5" is. `"6.60·10-5"` (the S4b legend's form) is also read as 6.60. This is an observation for code. The CB:TPU check (1/1.19 = 0.8403 against 0.841) is consistent.
- **Units Ω·cm and S/cm aren't known to code** ("used as printed"). The reciprocal still works numerically because 1 S = 1/Ω.
- **Stress conversion σ = C·ΔR/R0** (Fig. 3f prints "σ tissue = C × ΔR/R0"). I mapped it to `ratio_def` (C = σ/(ΔR/R0)), because the library has no proportionality entry other than the statistical `constant_scaling_mean`. C comes from a multilayer, sinusoid-grooved modified Stoney model (Supplementary Eqs. 1–26, integrals over the groove profile). That isn't closed-form algebra the library can hold, so I proposed no model and wrote no assumption report for it. Its stated assumptions (cylindrical bending, the two strips sharing a curvature, tissue bending resistance ignored, ν = 0.5, uniform stress across the width) therefore go unchecked.
- **The printed C = 3.107528138998242e+004 (kPa)** sits in the Supplementary MATLAB script ("for default plot … standard gauge resistance of 125k"). I used it as the printed input for F3gh and S14n s60. Whether every figure used this default factor isn't stated. Wire thickness was "corrected using the resistance of a given cantilever wire loop", so C may vary per cantilever (written as an alternative). The per-spacing factors (40 and 80 µm; 100 µm uses the 80 µm factor) aren't printed.
- **Nothing in the derivations can be rebuilt except the conductivities.** No ΔR/R0, stress, velocity or stress–strain values are read or released (S3, S4, Fig. 3d, Fig. 4b bars and S14 are unread). So Young's moduli, gauge factor, anisotropy ratio, twitch stress and the day-4/day-2 fold are *cannot check*.
- **The F2g ratio at 60 µm** is written as a mean of per-tissue ratios. The readings (bar 2.765, points' mean 2.76) already disagree with the printed 2.7 at printed precision. I didn't add a derivation from the readings, because they are readings of the ratio itself, not its inputs.
- **EC50s (7 items) and resistivity are fits.** The dose–response model isn't stated, and the resistivity is the slope of R·A against L. These are marked `no_formula` with formula "not stated". OOP, SPD and conduction velocities are by reference ([19], [21]).
- **Not written as derivations.** ST1 top = bottom + layer thickness isn't stated as a formula. Note that it puts the TPU base at 0–1.25 µm, while F1 prints 3 µm (the legend explains "partial integration" of the wire). The ST1 moduli copy S3's means, which is a copy, not a formula. S20 and S24 "normalized twitch stress" normalise to something that isn't printed.
