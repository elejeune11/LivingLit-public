## Record reader

- **Asterisk pairs read from figures.** Most tests are post-hoc pairs drawn only as brackets. I read the bracket ends at high zoom. Fig. 5B G1 is the hardest: two brackets (* and ***) both seem to span Torin1 → Torin1 + pifithrin. I recorded * as `p`, the *** under `p_values` with a unit anchor, and a suspected error. The S/G2/M brackets in Fig. 5B (*** for Torin1 vs Torin1 + pifithrin, bars ~20 vs ~22) are also doubtful.
- **Marks the legend does not key.** Several legends key only some marks: Fig. 2A has ** but keys only * and ****; Fig. 3B has **** but keys * and ***; Fig. 4A has *** without a key; Fig. 6D has *** but keys **. For an unkeyed mark I wrote `P<0.05 (Methods)` (the paper-wide threshold) plus a suspected error. For a keyed mark I wrote the legend's string, even when it is unusual ("****P<0.001", "*P<0.01", "**P<0.0001"). I preferred this to `significant, threshold not stated` because the Methods do give a paper-wide threshold.
- **Unmarked bars in post-hoc families.** Genes or phases with no mark are written as `groups:` measurements, not as pairs with an implied n.s. The exceptions are places where the text states a direction or "nonsignificant" (CDKN2a, RBL2, HCN4, F1C); those are pairs with `stated_direction`.
- **Representative blots.** Many claims rest on representative Western blots that have no densitometry (F1B, F2B, F5A, F5C, F6C, S6B, S6C). I wrote these as pair or measurement items with the text's `stated_direction`. I chose the pair (Torin1 200 nM vs DMSO) where the text says "dose-dependent". That pair is my choice, not one the paper prints.
- **Group sets the text never states in one sentence.** For S6C "Control + FA" and S7 "Control + 1 µM iso", the DMSO + additive combination appears only in rotated lane or axis labels, which pdftotext cannot extract. I anchored these two treatment values on the group's ContextObj with `kind: unit`, not in `context_anchors`. Is this an acceptable use of `kind: unit` for context?
- **Groups that differ between legend and panel.** Fig. 1E lists 10/50/200 nM in the legend but plots only 200 nM. Fig. 3F lists 10/50/200 nM but plots 50 and 200. Suppl. Fig. 3 plots D9-14, which the legend does not list. I wrote the groups as plotted and added suspected errors.
- **number_of_lines.** The supplement says the figures come from "a single cell line". I wrote `single` (the paper's word, not a number). Units whose legend names no line get `cell_line: not stated`. F2F combines BJRiPS and Gibco, so it gets `number_of_lines: not stated`. I did not count the lines myself.
- **MTF pacing.** No sentence holds "electrical pacing" for the MTFs. I used the device name "MyoPacer Cell Stimulator", with frequency 1-4 Hz and 20V as qualifiers. Voltage and calcium imaging use "electrically stimulated".
- **Treatment duration.** The standard "7 days starting ≈2 days after onset of beating" is not a qualifier. Only S4 (4 days) and S3 (windows) carry duration or window qualifiers. The schema has no field for "days after onset of beating".
- **Native-tissue groups (S2).** Fetal and adult heart RNA are `native tissue` groups with species and donor_age only. The comparisons against them have no test ("not included in statistical analysis"). I wrote them as pairs with the text's stated direction. The text says Torin1 TNNT2 is "markedly below" fetal heart, but the Torin1 bar looks higher than the fetal bar. That is judged by eye, so it is logged as a suspected error, not a reading.
- **Fig. 7 volcano plots.** The text calls IL8/SPP1 "significant". In Fig. 7G no adjusted-P threshold line appears above the points, while the Methods use Benjamini–Yekutieli FDR control. I judged the claim's support as overstated. The volcano positions are not read.
- **Left out.** Fig. 1A and Suppl. 4A schematics, including "ascorbic acid" in the differentiation medium. It is only image text with no sentence, so it is not in `cm_medium` or `supplements`. Also left out: the Fig. 6A mechanism schematic, PCA panels as values, and the absolute forces in the Fig. 2E representative trace (plotted only).
- **Item `definition` vs `method`.** CTD25/CTD75 are defined in the paper as "calcium transient" durations although they come from FluoVolt voltage imaging. I recorded the definitions as printed. The mismatch may be a wording slip in the paper.
- **nihms-1549573.pdf** is the author manuscript of the same paper. I listed it under `not_used` and anchored to the journal version. The gate searches both files for quotes.

## Figures and data reader (F2C)

- **Fig. 2C read (raster, journal_version.pdf p. 6), script `figures/F2C.py`.** Bars with individual points, n=3 per condition. No table or released data gives these values (the paper releases no data files; the supplement has none for this panel).
- **Markers touch lines.** Every marker sits on a bar-top line or an error bar, so no marker is a separate blob in the raw raster. The script removes lines thinner than a marker by a morphological opening (15 px disc at 600 dpi; lines ~7 px, markers ~18 px); control (circles) and 10 nM (squares) then give exactly three separate blobs each for disc sizes 13–17 px. Points counted: control 3, 10 nM 3 = printed n. Points agree with the bars: control mean of points 1725 vs bar 1724; 10 nM 2732 vs 2742, SEM from points 239 vs read error 225. Intervals add a term for line remnants left on a marker by the opening.
- **200 nM not countable.** The three triangles lie under the error bar, both caps and the bar top (one is almost entirely hidden); they do not separate at any disc size that leaves the others intact. Bar only (mean 3895, upper error 3955), `countable: false`, no points. So F2C_t200 has no points and the Tukey family (ctrl, t10, t200) cannot be checked from points.
- **Control error bar** lies inside the bar-top line and is not read (mean only).
- **Shared control.** One label maps to one item, so the control points are returned twice ("Control (vs 10 nM)" → F2C_t10/ctrl, "Control (vs 200 nM)" → F2C_t200/ctrl); same readings, not independent data.

## Vocabulary agent

- **Gibco iPS → CVCL_RM92 (CBiPSC6.2).** The paper names the "commercially-available Gibco episomal iPSC" line without a catalogue number; the local Cellosaurus lookup resolves "Gibco Human Episomal iPSC" and "A18945" exactly to CBiPSC6.2. Adopted with "Gibco iPS" as a synonym. A person may want to confirm.
- **BJRiPS → CVCL_X737 (BJ RiPS-A).** The Methods name the clone BJRiPS-A; figure legends say BJRiPS. Both wordings were added as synonyms.
- **Own term in two fields.** `supplements:fetal_bovine_serum` already exists, but an own id cannot be adopted into a second field (only ontology ids can), so FBS given to some groups got a second own term, `treatment:fetal_bovine_serum`. The spec does not say how an own term should serve two fields.
- **Compounds ChEBI lacks:** pifithrin-α (ChEBI has only the hydrobromide salt, CHEBI:229924) and nutlin-3a (ChEBI has only racemic Nutlin-3, CHEBI:46742) became own terms with those ids as xrefs. The Gibco Chemically Defined Lipid Concentrate is an own term (a proprietary mixture).
- **Flags (3):** `number_of_lines: single` (a count written as a word that code doesn't parse; the reader may write "1"); `time_point: final day of treatment` (a time in words, neither a number nor a state); `measure: clustering and principal component analysis of all genes` (several measures, Fig. S8A and S8B).
- **Split request (1):** `cm_medium: RPMI/B27`. B27 belongs in supplements (`supplements:b27`), and the default supplements value names only insulin.
- **Same medium, two wordings:** `RPMI1640 (no phenol red)` (F4DJ) and `RPMI without phenol red` (S7) are probably one bath medium. Both map to `measurement_medium:culture_medium` with the wording as the `medium` qualifier. These were not flagged because no flag kind fits.
- **Downstroke velocity in msec** (F4G): mapped to a new `action_potential_downstroke_velocity` term (voltage_rate), as the paper names it. The printed unit is a time, which the reader already labelled.
- **CTD25 and CTD75 of the voltage signal** map to action potential duration with `level` CTD25 or CTD75. The paper's own definition says "of the calcium transient" for a FluoVolt voltage signal, so the wording looks copied from the calcium method. T75-25 got a new `action_potential_repolarisation_time` term.
- **Profiles:** OCR and ECAR curves across Seahorse injections got new `*_time_course` terms (following `force_trace`) rather than mapping to the rate itself.
- **Pathway scores** (nSolver) got a new `pathway_score` term under gene set expression. It is not mapped to `principal_component_score`, although nSolver derives the score from a first principal component, because the paper does not say so.
- **Platform vessels** (12-well plate, 96 well plate, chamber slides, Seahorse microplate) map to `platform:culture_2d` with the vessel as `format`, because the paper does not say monolayer.
- **Gelatin** (MTF substrate) maps to a new root `substrate:gelatin_hydrogel`, not under `hydrogel`, whose definition says cells are embedded. "gelatin" alone was not added as a synonym because it would also match gelatin coatings.
- **Western blot "protein" items** without densitometry map to `protein_level` (phospho-forms as the phosphorylated protein against a loading control, not phospho/total). The `normalised_to` and `expressed_as` qualifiers are left to the values for qPCR (TBP then control) and NanoString genes, which are normalised differently.
- **Re-map against vocabulary v52 (2026-11-12).** The v44 edits from this paper are already logged; no new edits were needed. One stored mapping was removed: `measure: respiratory reserve capacity` is now matched by code, because a later paper (v50, 2014_Yang) added it as a synonym of `measure:spare_respiratory_capacity`. No later term was a better fit for any other value. In particular, `Seahorse assay media` stays on the broader `assay_medium`, because the paper does not say whether the medium was the XF DMEM or XF RPMI product.

## Formula agent

- **Few printed numbers.** Almost every derived value is plotted only, so 139 of 143 derivations are *cannot check*. The only rebuilt ones are the Ki67−/pH3− pie percentages (complement_fraction; all four are consistent). I wrote one derivation per qPCR, flow-percentage and NanoString item, and most of these have every input not stated. Is that the intended granularity, or should one derivation per method do?
- **Complement as a stated formula.** For Suppl. Fig. 1 I wrote `neg = 1 - pos` with the pie-chart legend ("Ki67+ or Ki67-" of one total) as the anchor. The paper never writes the subtraction; the two exclusive categories drawn as one pie imply it.
- **Seahorse.** The stated subtractions map to sum_of_parts (max = FCCP − non-mito; reserve = FCCP − baseline; glycolysis, capacity, reserve). OCR is normalized to cell number and then to each well's own baseline, so the cell-number step cancels and the normalized baseline is 1 (100 %) by construction. Glycolytic reserve also equals capacity − glycolysis; I gave that as an alternative. The supplement writes "rotenone/actinomycin A" where the kit list gives antimycin A, probably a typo. Nothing here is plotted with readings.
- **Not a stated derivation, left out:** Western densitometry in Figs. 5D, 6D and 6E ("Densitometry of … bands"; no normalisation stated, though S4 says "normalized to control"); geometric MFI (F2C, F2I, F4B, S3: per-sample reductions with no library entry); FluoVolt times "normalized" to baseline fluorescence (a scale that does not change times); and F4C beating rate (no method).
- **Image-only normalisations.** "Normalized to TBP then control" (main qPCR figures) and the mtDNA ratio "normalized to control" appear only in raster axis labels, so those derivations are `no_formula` anchored on the TBP sentence. S6A and S4 print the label as text, so they use ratio_def.
- **MTF:** the modified Stoney equation is cited (ref 33) and not printed, so it is no_formula. The Supplemental Methods compute systolic, diastolic and twitch *stresses*, but Fig. 2F reports *force*. The printed "nearly 2-fold" is the output, but no force is printed, so it cannot be rebuilt.
- **Not recorded:** F1F gate percentages sum to 99–101 % (G0+G1+S/G2/M), but the paper does not say the gates are exhaustive (Fig. 1G adds sub-G1), so I wrote no sum. The S1 chi-squared P (counts recoverable as % × total) needs a 2×2 Pearson χ² entry the library lacks, and its output is a P, not a value, so I did not propose one.
