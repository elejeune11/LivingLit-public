## Record reader

- **Electric stimulation as a culture condition.** The `pacing` field covers measurement or training. I put the culture-time stimulation (2 Hz, 5 V/cm, 5 ms, second week) in `maturation_strategy` as "electric stimulation", and static stress against no stress in `mechanical_load`. `pacing` is used for stimulation during measurement only. With this, NS vs SS differs in one field, SE vs SS in one, and SE vs NS in two.
- **"Not significant" statements with no printed P.** Several pairs say only "not significantly different", "did not reach significance", "not to significance" or "did not significantly increase" (F2B NS vs collagen, F3 SE vs SS, F5B SE vs SS, F5C SE vs NS, S6 SERCA2 SE vs SS, S4 isoproterenol force, TXT4). I wrote `p: "not significant"` so that the parser reads it as P ≥ 0.05, and put the exact wording in `note`. TXT2 ("did not find any difference", with no word about significance) got `p: "not stated"`.
- **P labels inside figure images.** Fig. 2A–C, 3A, 4D, 5B and Supplement Fig. 6 print "p<0.01", "p<0.008", "p<0.0167" and so on as image text. I recorded them as extra `p_values` anchored `kind: unit`. NS vs SE in Fig. 4D (p<0.001) appears only as a figure label, so that whole item is anchored `kind: unit`. The labels seem to be Bonferroni thresholds (0.05/3 = 0.0167, 0.05/6 ≈ 0.008), not exact P.
- **Bonferroni threshold against text P.** The paper says pairwise P were Bonferroni-corrected, and the figures use 0.0167. Yet the text calls SS vs SE stiffness (P=0.03) and collagen alignment (P=0.03, 0.0289, 0.0293) increases. I judged those claims' support *overstated*. It is unclear whether the printed P are raw or corrected.
- **Several P for one test.** F4D SS vs SE: text P=0.0164, legend P<0.01. F2C: text P=0.07 / 0.01, legend P=0.0728 / 0.0121. F6A: text P=0.1 / 0.87, legend P=0.09 / 0.8. In every case the text P is in `p` and the others are in `p_values`. F4D SS vs SE also carries a suspected error.
- **Pooled value.** Conduction velocity, 2.76±0.61 cm/s, is an average over all constructs, with "no significant difference between groups". I attached it with `over: [NS, SS, SE]`. But `over` means a value that holds for each group, and this is a pooled mean. The schema has no "pooled" attachment.
- **Fold changes and percent decreases.** "≈7-fold", "10-fold", "2.5-fold" and "≈50%" are `ratio`/`percent` values with `between`. Force-frequency decreases (43.19±0.01%) are `between: [<cond>_3, <cond>_2]`, with the SEM as a separate `sem` value, also `between`. The ± of 0.01% and 0.03% is flagged as a suspected error (implausibly small).
- **Discussion range not recorded as a value.** "1.5- to 2.5-fold increase" for calcium-handling proteins covers SERCA2 and RYR2 at once, and I could not tell which group pair it refers to. It is kept only as a claim quote.
- **Cell-free collagen group (F2B).** It inherits `cell_line`, `purity` and the differentiation fields from `default_context`, although it has no cells. I overrode `cell_types: none` in the group. Overriding `cell_line` would have forced it out of the default for the whole paper.
- **Benchmark groups from the Discussion.** Rat heart alignment (≈9.08), rat neonatal stiffness, hESC-CM size (480±32 µm²), adult strip force (>10), the literature force ranges, and newborn/infant strips are `other study` groups. Each sits in the unit it is compared with (F2A, F2B, F3, F4D, TXT3), and each has one citation key. Multi-reference citations ("11,24,25,48", "46,47") are one citation entry each. Species (rat) is in `note`, because there is no species field.
- **Units of alignment.** The text prints alignment values without a unit. For cell alignment I used the Fig. 2A axis label "1/angle dispersion, %". For collagen alignment, which has no axis label, I wrote "not stated". Cell volume fraction is printed in % in the text, but the Fig. 2C axis is a fraction (0–0.8).
- **n.** The Methods give mechanics n as "N=6 for NS, n=7 for SS, and n=9 for ES". The force-frequency legend gives n=5/11/4. SS n=11 exceeds the 7 constructs used for mechanics; this is noted on F6A_curve but not flagged as an error. What n counts (constructs, images, cells) is not stated for histology or cell size. Supplement Figs. 4–5 rest on single representative constructs.
- **Naming.** "ES" is used for SE in the Methods n sentence and in the Fig. 3 legend. "Chiron 9902" is presumably CHIR99021; I kept it as printed with a suspected_error on the context value.
- **Context left as "not stated".** Wnt activation and inhibition days and the number of lines are not stated, and I did not compute them from the protocol steps. `disease_model` and `genotype` are "not stated", because the paper never says the line is healthy.
- **Units without items.** Fig. 1B–F, Fig. 6B (representative calcium transients), Supplement Figs. 1–2 and the TEM images give no values or tests, so they are not units. Supplement Fig. 5A has no printed calcium levels. It is one group with the bath calcium "10 to 4000 µM" in context; the figure reader supplies the x values.

## Vocabulary agent

- **Words in quantity fields.** `confluency: confluence` and `donor_age: newborn` / `infant` cannot be mapped: quantity fields take no terms, and only declared states are accepted (donor_age declares fetal, neonatal, young, adult). I flagged all three *not a context*; the honest kind would be "undeclared state", which the flag list lacks. "Newborn" is probably "neonatal", and "infant" fits no declared state, but equating them would be my inference. This needs an orchestrator decision (declare the states in field content), not a re-read.
- **Electric stimulation.** I added "electric stimulation" as a synonym of the broad *electrical stimulation*, because the words do not name a regime. This paper's value is mapped explicitly to *constant electrical stimulation* (2 Hz for one week; the record carries the frequency).
- **Static stress = isometric load.** The paper defines SS as "maintaining constructs at a fixed static length", so "static stress" became a synonym of *isometric load*. "No stress" (one end cut free) needed a new root, *unloaded*.
- **Twitch force vs twitch stress.** Fig. 4C/4D and the Discussion benchmarks are in mN/mm² and map to *twitch stress*. Fig. 6A and Supplement Figs. 4–5 are in mN or normalised to 2 Hz, and map to *twitch force*. The cell-based (α-actinin) normalisation is in `normalised_to`, but the existing *twitch stress* value key does not require `normalised_to`. So a stress normalised to cell area could be compared with one normalised to construct area. Revisit at the checkpoint.
- **Contractility** (the slope of twitch force against strain, mN/mm²) became a new child of *Frank-Starling response*: *slope of twitch stress against strain*, with dimension stress. Its parent is dimensionless; the spec does not say whether a child may differ in dimension.
- **Alignment.** Cell alignment and collagen alignment are both "1/angle dispersion". I added *inverse angle dispersion* with the qualifier `structure`. The record leaves collagen alignment's unit "not stated", but the figure reader found the axis label.
- **Area fractions.** "Cell volume fraction" is defined as an area percentage. "Bona fide muscle cross-sectional area" is printed in %, so it is a share of the section and not an area. Both map to a new *tissue area fraction* with the qualifier `component`. The record reader might note that the measure's name says area while its values are %.
- **Standardized Western blots** (Supplement Fig. 6) map to *protein level*. The log-transform and autoscaling are written into `normalised_to`, so value keys keep them apart from the plain GAPDH ratios. `expressed_as` has no value for a standardized score.
- **"Chiron 9902"** is mapped to CHIR 99021 by a synonym, as the earlier misprint "CHIR99201" was, and the record keeps its suspected_error. **"Native tissue"** is not printed (the paper says "native myocardium"), so it is mapped without a synonym. **NRVM** maps to CL ventricular cardiac muscle cell, with `no_ontology_term`: species and age are lost, and no context field holds species.
- **B27 with or without insulin** became the qualifier `insulin` on B27 (added), rather than insulin as a second supplement.

## Formula agent

- **Slopes have no entry.** Passive stiffness (F2B) and contractility (F4D) are slopes of fitted stress–strain curves, and the paper does not say how the fit was done. The library has no regression-slope entry, so they are `no_formula` with labelled alternatives. A sum-based least-squares definition would map them, but code could not rebuild it from readings: inputs are single values, not series. Observation for code: a least-squares line through the Fig. 4C means plus the slack point (0, 0) gives SS 0.635 (printed 0.63), SE 1.23 (printed 1.34), NS 0.051 (printed 0.055). The secant from slack to 125 % gives 0.72 / 1.46 / 0.054.
- **Circular area as a model.** "Assuming circular cross-sectional geometry" is a modelling choice, so I proposed `circular_cross_section_area` (model; assumes circular cross-section, uniform diameter). Its textbook citation has no quote or url; the gate accepted it on the paper's anchor. Check that this is acceptable. The area is taken at the nonstrained length and used at every step up to 125 %, so the stresses are nominal. No assumption covers this, and incompressibility would shrink the true area by about 1/1.25.
- **"≈" outputs are judged at printed precision.** ≈20 (SS/NS stiffness) rebuilds 23.5 and comes out *inconsistent*. ≈7 (NS/collagen) rebuilds 5.95 and comes out *close*. ≈50 % (cell size) rebuilds 54.5 % and comes out *inconsistent*. Code reads "≈" as an exact value. Outputs printed as ">10", "at least 10" or "about 2" were not used; I used the plain legend values.
- **F6A % decreases do not match the plotted means.** Rebuilt from readings, with 1 at 2 Hz by normalisation (`normalized_to_own_control`): NS 45.5 % (printed 43.19), SS 13.8 % (16.8), SE 3.9 % (4.4). If the paper averaged per-construct decreases, the mean of per-sample values could differ, but per-construct values are not available. Aggregation is set to *not stated*.
- **TXT3 cell-based normalisation.** This is construct twitch stress at 125 % divided by the α-actinin area share, so `ratio_def` and not `stress_def` (whose does_not_apply excludes per-cell areas). Stress and share come from different samples, so the aggregation is *of means*. NS rebuilds 0.067 against a printed 0.08, consistent only within reading.
- **One derivation serving several items.** The per-sample definitions (cell volume fraction, cell size, alignment, GAPDH normalisation) are written once, on the first pairwise item, with a note naming the others. The spec does not say whether the pairwise copies need their own derivations.
- **Alignment.** "1/angle dispersion, %" has no readable unit. I mapped it to a proposed derived `reciprocal_def` (from `ratio_def` with a = 1). For collagen alignment, the definition is written for cell edges and applied to "cell/collagen alignment".
- **Not derived here.** cTnT % and viability % have no stated formula, so they get bounds only. S6 standardisation is stated only by citation (ref 42): `no_formula`, by reference.

## Vocabulary agent (key checkpoint)

Re-map checkpoint after 10 papers (03-vocabulary, Earlier papers; 05-across-sources, Standing keys), after the independent check of release v5-rebuild-10. 4 standing_key edits revise the keys of the measures this paper first added.

- Value keys now require treatment, genotype and disease_model for every measure whose value a drug, variant or disease can change. Left unchanged: method, device, design and yield measures (analysis time, required sample size, micropattern yield, usable constructs, post spring constant, applied load, strip bending force, between-sample variability, identified protein count, PCA variance, medium composition, signal-to-noise, embryoid body diameter, cardiomyocyte yield, beating onset).
- Direction keys of force and beat-rate measures now require pacing (paced against spontaneous), so a spontaneous and a paced isoprenaline response do not meet.
- Guess: kinetic times (twitch duration, relaxation and calcium decay times) are also rate dependent, but their direction keys were not given pacing; the request named rate and force only.
- Concern for a person: genotype and disease_model are 'not stated' for most healthy lines, and an unstated must-match field blocks a value comparison. A dry run of the revised keys leaves 2 cross-paper value agree/differ verdicts of 104 (44 with treatment alone). The relaxation report will name genotype; whether an unstated genotype of a healthy line should count as wild type is a design decision.
- Re-mapped measure 'tensile stiffness' (Fig. 2B) from specimen: tissue to specimen: engineered construct. One stated text covers both the cell-free collagen constructs (group collagen, cell_types none) and the hiPSC-CM constructs (NS, SS, SE), and a mapping is per text, not per group, so it cannot say tissue for some groups and material for others. The elastic_modulus value key now requires cell_types, which separates cell-free from cellular constructs. Cost: Ruan's cellular constructs no longer meet Bliley's 'tissue' moduli on measure.specimen (they would also differ on cell_types: cardiomyocytes vs cardiomyocytes + cardiac fibroblasts). The record's group collagen keeps preparation 'intact tissue' from the unit context; the record reader may want a group-level preparation.
