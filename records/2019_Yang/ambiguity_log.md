## Record reader

- **mmc2.pdf** is the main article and mmc1.pdf combined in one file. I read it and listed it under `not_used` as a duplicate. All anchors point at main.pdf or mmc1.pdf.
- **Fatty acids: `treatment` or `maturation_strategy`.** The fatty acid cocktail is both the compared exposure and the paper's maturation strategy. If I wrote it in both fields, the two would be aliased in every unit, so I wrote it only in `treatment`, with each complex's concentration as a qualifier. I set `maturation_strategy: not stated` paper-wide.
- **Two doses and two carnitine concentrations.** The uptake experiments (S3a, TXT1, TXT2) used the full dose (105/81/45 µM complexes + 250 µM carnitine, Results). Every later experiment used half the dose (52.5/40.5/22.5 µM + 120 µM carnitine, Methods). I recorded these as different contexts, not as a conflict. The control's 50 µM fatty acid-free albumin is its `treatment` (vehicle).
- **differentiation_factors** ("activin A + BMP4 + CHIR 99021 + Xav 939") is stated in two sentences, and no single ≤30-word quote holds all four. I gave the value its own `anchor` (the CHIR/Xav sentence) and added a note. The Hofsteen sentence, which names activin A and BMP4, anchors the by-reference fields. The schema has no way to give one value two anchors.
- **By reference.** I marked `wnt_activation_days`, `wnt_inhibition_days`, `insulin_day` and `confluency` as by reference to Hofsteen et al. 2016 ("a protocol based on our previously reported directed differentiation method"). For RUES2 and WTC-11 these fields are *not stated*: the paper says only "differentiation protocols similar to those for IMR90", which I kept as an other_condition.
- **RUES2 units (S4a, S4b).** The supplement says "Methods are similar … please refer to … the main text". From this I gave S4a and S4b the IMR90 measurement context (platform, substrate, temperature, medium) and kept the sentence as an other_condition. This could be argued to carry context the paper does not state exactly.
- **number_of_lines** is *not stated* per unit. The paper states "three different hPSC lines" only for the study as a whole, and the study also includes a HEK293 assay and rat pilot data.
- **time_point and the treatment duration.** The time after the start of treatment ("2 weeks", "2 days", "15 min") goes in `time_point`, not in `day_of_measurement`, because no culture day is printed and computing 20 + 14 days is forbidden. For the micropost unit the Methods say "One week after fatty acid treatment"; it is unclear whether this means one week into treatment or one week after the 2-week course ended. I kept the phrase as printed.
- **Fig. 7 western blots** print no test, n or quantification. I wrote one pair per protein and time point (15 min vs CNTL, 30 min vs CNTL), each with the text's `stated_direction`. For ACC, Akt, ERK and p38 the text says only "after fatty acid treatment", so I applied the direction to both time points. The F7 control's treatment is `none` ("control cardiomyocytes"). The schema has no form for "groups compared visually, no test".
- **Asterisk without a printed P** (S1: 0.01 ng/ml LPS vs medium; Table S2: five rows): I recorded p as `not stated`. Where the text says "no significant" or "does not affect", I wrote p `not significant` (S1 palmitate-albumin) or left a note (ST2). I was unsure whether `not significant` is a P string the code accepts.
- **Table S2 vs Fig. 4.** The dV/dtmax and Cm rows of Table S2 are values of the Fig. 4 items (suffix `_table`). The other five rows are items of unit ST2. The table gives no spread label, so I took SEM from the Methods.
- **F/F0 is F340/F380** (ratiometric), not a baseline-normalised F/F0, so I did not set `expressed_as` on the Fig. 2 values.
- **Fig. 5:** the title and Methods call the measure *reserve* capacity (FCCP minus baseline), while the text and axis call it *maximal* capacity, so I logged a suspected error. The only printed value is "~38%" (between groups). The n (6) counts differentiation runs.
- **RNA-seq genes:** one item per gene the text names, each with a stated direction. P is `significant, threshold not stated` only where the text says "significantly". The n of RNA-seq is not stated. The GO enrichment items have no direction. CPT-1A (Results) vs CPT-1B (Discussion, qPCR, and Table S1's gloss "CPT1B (… 1A)") is a suspected error on F6_CPT1A.
- **qPCR (Fig. 6D, S5B):** the text states a direction only for SLC2A4/GluT4 and HK2. For CD36, CPT1B and PDK4 I set no stated_direction, because the text links them to RNA-seq, not to the qPCR panel. F6D prints no n.
- **Data not shown** (rat pilot TXT3; python cocktail in hPSC-CMs TXT4): I recorded these as units with stated directions, and judged `presence: does not follow` on their claims. The rat units have `species: rat` and `donor_age: neonatal`.
- **S2 live/dead timing:** the legend and Supplemental Results say two weeks, and the Supplemental Methods say two days. I recorded a suspected error on the `time_point` context value.
- **Replication:** single-cell t-tests (n = 91 cells from 4 runs, and so on) are judged `replication: overstated` on the claims that rest on them.
- **Fig. 4 legend n = 24/25** covers panel C (Cm), whose n is 14/15 in the text and Table S2. I logged this as a suspected error on F4_capacitance.
- **Not recorded:** the representative traces (Fig. 2A, 3A, 4A, 5A), the GAPDH loading control, the Table S1 primer sequences, and the Introduction's medium-lipid figures, which are attributed claims only. Fig. 5A OCR time courses are representative traces, not a tested measure.

## Vocabulary agent

- **Flag (several measures):** `MYH6, MYH7 and NPPA mRNA` (TXT4_genes) names three genes in one measure. TXT3 splits the same genes into three items, so this one should be split too, or the record should say why it is one measure. I considered mapping it to *expression of a gene set*, but I flagged it instead.
- **F5 measure:** the paper calls the value "maximal mitochondrial respiratory capacity after FCCP" (text, y axis) but defines it as FCCP minus baseline OCR (title, Methods), which is reserve capacity. The reader already labelled this a suspected error. I mapped it to the common parent *oxygen consumption rate*, not to *maximal OCR* or *spare respiratory capacity*.
- **Treatment sets:** I mapped the fatty acid-albumin complexes to {palmitate, oleic acid, linoleic acid, fatty acid-free albumin}. The control is fatty acid-free albumin alone, so the computed contrast adds the fatty acids. ChEBI has no bovine or fatty acid-free albumin, so `treatment:fatty_acid_free_albumin` is an own term. The Sigma oleic and linoleic acid-albumin complexes do not say that their albumin is fatty acid-free.
- **Acid or anion ids:** I followed the existing palmitate → hexadecanoate (anion) pattern for myristate → tetradecanoate and palmitoleate (anion). For linoleic acid I adopted the acid, to match the already-adopted oleic acid. The vocabulary is now mixed acid/anion, and the spec does not say which form to adopt.
- **Carnitine:** I mapped the stated "carnitine" to (R)-carnitine because the Methods say "L-Carnitine" (same paper). I did not add a synonym, because "carnitine" is broader than (R)-carnitine.
- **mTeSR:** I added *mTeSR (version not stated)* as a sibling of mTeSR1, following the GCaMP6 (variant not stated) precedent, rather than assuming mTeSR1.
- **Platforms:** I mapped 96-well plates, 10 cm2 plates, Seahorse plates, glass slides and glass coverslips to *2D culture* with a `format` qualifier. The paper does not say whether they are monolayers.
- **Measurement media:** "HEPES-containing medium" (force on microposts) is not said to be the culture medium; I mapped it to *culture medium as bath* with additive "15 mM HEPES". "bath solution" (patch clamp) is a salt solution with its own recipe; I mapped it to *extracellular solution* without adding a synonym. "unbuffered DMEM" (Sigma D5030) is not the Agilent XF medium, so I used the broader *assay or imaging medium*.
- **Existing term fit:** *fatty acid uptake* (code-matched) is defined as uptake of a *labelled* fatty acid read in the cells. This paper measures depletion of NEFA from the medium. Its key requires `method`, so values stay apart, but the definition is narrower than its use here. *micropost array*'s definition says fibronectin-stamped tips; here the tips are stamped with laminin.
- **Gene qualifiers:** I wrote HGNC symbols so that "same gene" matches: CPT-1A → CPT1A, GluT4 → SLC2A4. The rat pilot (TXT3) genes are written as printed (MYH6, MYH7, NPPA), although they are rat orthologues.
- **cm_medium "RPMI-B27-insulin":** I mapped it to RPMI 1640 and sent a split request for "B27-insulin" to `supplements`. The record's `supplements` value names only carnitine.
- **Record notes (not fixed):** in the S1 transgene the printed "human HATLR4" is likely hTLR4. HEK293 sits in `cell_line`, whose scope says hiPSC lines; there is no field for non-hPSC reporter cells.
- **Re-map at vocabulary v68 (2026-11-13):** later papers added terms that code now matches for three measurement media, so I removed those stored mappings: "HEPES-containing medium" → *HEPES-buffered medium*, "unbuffered DMEM" → *unbuffered DMEM*, and "bath solution" → *extracellular solution* (that one is a synonym now). I kept one stored mapping for "HEPES-containing medium", on the matched term, only to carry the qualifier hepes "15 mM" taken from the Methods sentence. These replace my earlier mappings to the broader *culture medium* and *assay medium*. No other mapping changed. The F5 measure stays on *oxygen consumption rate*: v68 has both *maximal OCR* and *spare respiratory capacity*, and the paper's label and its definition point to different ones.

## Formula agent

- **"nearly 50%"** (S3a reduction) rebuilds to 45.6 % from 31 and 57 µM; code judged it consistent. "Nearly" is a hedge, and I did not know how code reads it. I wrote the paper's words as printed.
- **Uptake per one million cells.** The uptake (medium NEFA before minus after; `sum_of_parts`) is printed per one million cells. How the depletion was scaled to cell number is not stated, so the inputs are `not_stated` and the scaling is a note. One formula cannot hold both steps.
- **Total micropost force** is "adding together the forces measured at each post beneath" a cell. The number of posts per cell varies and is not stated, and no library entry sums a variable number of parts, so the total is `no_formula`. The per-post step is `hooke_spring` with the printed k = 38.4 nN/µm. I set `aggregation: mean of per-sample` because n counts cells.
- **Post stiffness.** The paper says k "was based on the dimensions of the microposts and the material properties of PDMS" and gives no formula, so I mapped `cantilever_tip_stiffness` with formula `not stated`. E is not printed. The printed k is not an evidence value, so it cannot be the output, and the step cannot be rebuilt. The posts are 6.45 µm tall and 2.3 µm across (L/d ≈ 2.8). That is hardly a slender beam: I reported it as a quote, because no value path exists for a `limit`. I proposed `circular_second_moment_diameter` (I = πd⁴/64, derived from `circular_second_moment` with d = 2R) so that the printed diameter can be used.
- **S4b (RUES2) force** uses the main-text micropost method, because the supplement refers to it. The same posts and the same k are assumed.
- **Fig. 5 chain:** reserve = FCCP − baseline OCR per group, then the FA/control ratio per run, then the change = ratio − 1 against the printed "~38%". No group OCR values are printed and Fig. 5 has no points, so nothing can be rebuilt. The OCR normalisation to cell number sits in notes, not as its own step. I wrote the per-run ratio summary as `mean of per-sample`; the paper says only that the ratios were "used to compare and summarize".
- **qPCR (F6d, S5b):** "normalized using HPRT" does not say ΔCt or 2^-ΔΔCt, so these are `no_formula`, with 2^-ΔΔCt as a labelled alternative. No values are printed.
- **Calcium transients:** F/F0 = F340/F380 is `ratio_def`. Amplitude, upstroke and decay velocity are stated only by citation (Korte 2011), so they are `no_formula`.
- **Percent changes** (46 %, 59 %, 57 %, 24 %) are relative changes of the printed group means. For dV/dtmax and Cm I used the text values, not the Table S2 values.
- **Not written:** the "about one-third" uptake (57 of 231 µM) and "half (115.5 µM)" of the dose are not evidence values in the record. The circularity index formula is not stated. Spontaneous rate in bpm has no stated derivation.
