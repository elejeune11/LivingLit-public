## Vocabulary agent

- **"afterload" → auxotonic, with no synonym.** All four K groups contract against flexible PDMS posts, so the load is elastic (auxotonic), K4 ("nearly isometric", 9.2 μN/μm) included. "afterload" was not made a synonym of *auxotonic*: a rigid post is also an afterload, so the word is broader than the term. Post stiffness stays a group qualifier (`post_stiffness`); the term's own qualifier is named `stiffness`, so the two names do not line up.
- **Measurement mode is a measure qualifier.** This paper measures twitch force and time to 50 % decay both auxotonically (on the posts, Fig. 2) and isometrically (on a force transducer, Fig. 3 and S3). I added the qualifier `contraction_mode` to twitch force, relaxation time and time to peak, plus `length` (Lmax) to twitch force. The standing keys of these measures do not require `measure.contraction_mode` yet, so auxotonic and isometric force can still fall into one stratum (they differ only in `preparation`, both *intact tissue*). ## Formula agent
- **Outputs that are only readings:** twitch stress (σ = F/A), fractional shortening and the βMHC/αMHC ratio have no printed value, only Fig. 2B, Fig. 6C and Fig. S7 readings. A derivation's `output` must be a record value, so code rebuilds these but does not judge them. By eye: stress from the printed force means and the one pooled area (0.64 mm²) gives K1 0.089, K2 0.205, K3 0.364, K4 0.409 mN/mm², against bar readings of 0.087, 0.240, 0.404 and 0.380. That fits the paper's note that widths varied between groups. The ratio of the S7 means is 25.7 (K4) and 10.8 (K1), against readings of 30.2 and 10.3; a mean of per-tissue ratios can differ from that. The spec should allow a reading as an output.
- **Stress aggregation:** the paper divides each tissue's force by its own area, but only the mean area over all groups is printed. I left `aggregation` unset because no printed mean is judged, and put the per-sample form in as an alternative.
- **Fractional shortening:** the named operation is (L0 − L)/L0, but the paper does not say how L was measured. With one post rigid, the shortening is probably the flexible post's deflection, so FS = F/(k·L0) could be chained from printed values. The paper does not say this, so I recorded it only as an alternative.
- **% against Day 1 (observation for code):** "NPPA went up by 40% and ACTA1 by 80%" comes out as 44% and 84% from the Fig. 7A K4 bars (fold vs Day 1, minus 1). Code finds both inconsistent at printed precision. The paper probably means "about".
- **Fold improvements** (1.5×, 4.7×) I read as ratios of group means of maximum isometric force. Code finds both consistent.
- **Force resolution:** 76 μN/pixel = 9.2 μN/μm × 8.3 μm. Code finds this consistent. The paper's "3 to 4 pixels" at K4 implies 228–304 μN. That brackets the printed 262 μN, but it is a range, so I wrote no derivation for it.
- **Tissue stiffness:** this is the slope over 0–40 % strain, divided by area. My proposed derived entry rests on the linear-elastic model, whose assumptions are *small strain* (and 40 % is not small) and linearity (not stated). Because the reported range is only a quote, code can only say "reported", not "fails". The printed 0.6–0.9 kPa is a range across groups, not one output. The UL tissues were never measured in situ, yet the paper says the same in situ area was used for them; it does not say which area that is.
- **Not derived here:** resting tension "calculated from Kpost and resting length" (Discussion) has no evidence item. The "0.08 mm ≈ 2.5 % strain" step implies a slack length of 3.2 mm, against about 7.7 mm resting length between posts. That may be a real difference (the tissue was off the posts by then) or a slip, and it is not an item either. I treated the Ca²⁺ τ (exponential fit) and the Frank–Starling slope (regression) as fitted reductions, not closed-form steps.
- **One derivation per gene, not per pairwise item:** for ΔΔCt (and per measure for velocity, power and work) I wrote one derivation each, with a note that it covers every item of that measure. F6B is plotted "relative to HPRT" without a Day-1 calibrator, so its exponent may be ΔCt rather than ΔΔCt. The Fig. 7B–C z-scores have no formula printed, so I marked them no_formula and proposed no z-score entry.
- **Hooke assumptions:** for small deflection I report the post length (22 mm) and the K4 pixel movement as quotes. The braces shorten the effective post length by an amount that is not printed, so no numeric limit was possible. Linearity of the post spring is not stated.
