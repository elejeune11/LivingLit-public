## Record reader

- **mmc2.pdf**: it is main.pdf followed by mmc1.pdf in one file. I read it and listed it under `not_used` as a duplicate; anchors point at main.pdf and mmc1.pdf.
- **Test for main-figure panels**: no main-figure legend names a test. The Methods list one-way and two-way ANOVA (paired and unpaired, ordinary or mixed-effects) with Bonferroni-Holm correction, but don't assign them to panels. I wrote `test: not stated`. Supplementary legends do name tests; I used them per panel: ANOVA with Bonferroni correction for the multi-group panels, Welch's t-test for the two-group panels S5L/M/P/Q (that legend lists only the *** and **** thresholds), and a one-tailed t-test for S3P.
- **Significance marks were read from rendered figures** (main figures are raster images). The per-line supplementary panels (S3H, S3K, S4A–D, S4H, S5B–K) have dense brackets. I read each bracket's ends at 300 dpi, but a misassigned bracket is possible. Panels with no mark between two groups were not written as pairs. Where a panel had no marks at all, I wrote a group measurement.
- **ns without a key**: Figs 4–6 and S3–S5 print "ns" but their legends don't define it. I wrote `p: ns`. Figs 2 and 7 define it, so there I wrote "ns, p > 0.05".
- **Published datasets (Fig. 1, S1F, Table S1, Fig. 4D)**: I made the adult, fetal, child and embryo hearts `native tissue` groups and the published hiPSC-CM datasets an `other study` group, citing Table S1. All of them are data from other studies; the schema has no kind for "native tissue from another study".
- **Fig. 1 DEG and loci counts**: these are counts between groups, so I wrote them as `compares` items with `between` values and `p: not stated`. The "prenatal" reference for the H3K27ac loci covers fetal and embryo; I used fetal and added a note.
- **Fetal age**: Table S1 says 8w – 28w; the Supplemental Methods say 9–28 weeks. I flagged the table value as a suspected error.
- **cm_day_at_formation**: I used the replating or seeding day where stated (IF day 20-23; Seahorse and MEA day 25 or day 55; TEM day 34). For day-60 IF groups I wrote `not stated`, because the replating sentence describes only the day-30 cells.
- **maturation_strategy**: I wrote `not stated`. The SP treatment is the paper's maturation intervention, but it sits in `treatment` (one fact, one field), and the paper describes its basal culture as having no maturation cues.
- **S3J time windows**: SP groups carry the window as a `treatment.days` qualifier. For the DMSO controls the paper doesn't say over which days DMSO was given, so the window is only an `other_conditions` label there. As a result, the code sees the three DMSO groups as identical.
- **Calcium per line (Fig. S5H–K)**: the figure key says DMSO/SP "(30d)", while Fig. 6 and the Methods say day 40. I used day 40 and added a unit note.
- **Patch clamp day**: the text says "1–5 days after our standard 30-day protocol"; the Fig. 7 legend says day 31. I used day 31.
- **INa (Fig. 7E)**: the text calls it significant, but no mark is printed. I wrote `P < 0.05 (Methods)`. Because peak INa is printed more negative, the claim's direction is `down`.
- **Table S2**: the p-value column prints bare numbers. I wrote `p = <value>` and kept the q-value as a value. The table prints no direction of change.
- **Discussion surface area "4,210 ± 180 cm2"**: recorded as printed with a suspected unit error (Fig. 3C is in μm²). The spread type isn't named.
- **Minus signs** extract as a control character in both extractors, so I shortened the quotes for NES, resting membrane potential, INa and the −80 mV citation to avoid the sign. Values keep "−" as printed.
- **Left out (analysis outputs, not measurements)**: PCA plots (Fig. 1B, S1A–D; only Fig. 4D is kept, as a stated direction), hierarchical clustering heatmaps (1D, 1F, 4E, 4G apart from the AURKB statement), GO/KEGG dot plots (S1E, S2A, 4F), ChIP tracks except the MAPK14 statement, the averaged Ca/AP/FP traces (6B, 7A, 7H), the OCR vs ECAR scatter (5F), transcript whisker plots without printed statistics (6H, S4F apart from the stated trend, S5O apart from the q-value, S6C TP53), the S5N fit curve, and p-p38 "data not shown".
- **Representative flow plot percentages** (Figs. 2B, 2E, S3B–C, S4G): recorded as measurement items of single representative plots. Main-figure numbers are image-only (`kind: unit`).
- **Structure wanted**: a way to mark an item as "the same data shown twice" (Fig. 2H and S3P look like the same western quantification but name different tests), and a per-group qualifier for a treatment window on a vehicle control.

## Vocabulary agent

- **MYL2-mEGFP → CVCL_UK03 (WTC-mEGFP-MYL2-cl27).** The paper does not name the clone. It cites Roberts et al. 2019 (Allen Institute), and the pinned Cellosaurus has only this one MYL2-mEGFP line. Adopted, with that inference written in the lookup note.
- **PLZ and 19004 are not in Cellosaurus.** Both added with `adopt_own`. Cellosaurus has GM19004, a Coriell lymphoblastoid line that is unrelated.
- **'normalized contraction force' → traction stress.** The supplement says the TFM force of contraction is "normalized by the surface area of the cell". That makes it a force per area on a 2D gel, so it went to `traction_stress`, not to `twitch_force` with a normalisation (S5B 'contraction force' stays under twitch force). This is a judgement.
- **'micropatterned single-cell' in `preparation`.** Most of this value is platform content. I mapped it to *dissociated cells*, since 'single cells' is already a synonym there, and sent 'micropatterned' to `platform` as a split request. A reader might prefer *live cells*.
- **Platform values naming vessels** ('glass coverslips', 'Thermonox plastic coverslips', '24-well plates', 'XF96 96-well microplates'). Each went to *2D culture* with a format qualifier, plus a split request to `substrate`, where those units say *not stated*.
- **Flags.**
  - S1F, both GSEA items: *several measures*. Each names three KEGG pathways.
  - Table S1 'number of samples': *not a context*. It is dataset sample counts (an n), not a measurement.
- **Gene groups as gene-set expression.** 'DUSP1 and DUSP6', 'ECT2 and GAS2L3' and the three Fig. 1 gene lists were mapped to *expression of a gene set*, not flagged. The paper states one direction for each group, not one per gene.
- **Possible record errors (record not changed).**
  - 'cell surface area' values are printed in cm2 (4,210 ± 180 cm2). The paper itself prints cm2, but µm2 is almost certainly meant.
  - S5N records the slope factor k = 9.4 with unit mV and kind *mean*, beside V1/2.
- **Left unclear.**
  - % spare respiratory capacity: the paper does not say whether this is max/basal or (max−basal)/basal, so `expressed_as` was left to the unit.
  - Western 'relative cTnI/ssTnI expression' is called a "stoichiometric relationship" without saying what it is divided by, so `normalised_to` was left out.
  - Calcium 'time to peak' was mapped to *calcium transient rise time* with level *time to peak*.

## Formula agent

- **Seahorse partition is a model, so I proposed one.** The library had no entry for "ATP-linked = basal − oligomycin rate; leak = oligomycin rate − rotenone/antimycin rate". I proposed `mito_stress_test_partition` (model, Brand & Nicholls 2011) with three assumptions. The paper's only statement bearing on them is the preliminary titration of the modulators. "Oligomycin does not change proton leak" is not stated. Basal OCR, maximal OCR and the oligomycin rate are never plotted, so the 24 ATP-linked and leak derivations cannot be rebuilt.
- **% spare respiratory capacity: two conventions.** Written as (max − basal)/basal, which is `relative_change`. The other convention, max/basal × 100 (the Seahorse report generator's "%"), is a labelled alternative.
- **Venn percentages (Fig. 4C) state no denominator.** I took it to be every gene in the diagram (405 + 308 + 2,094). The alternative is 713 + 2,402, which counts the shared genes twice. Anchor: *not stated*.
- **"decreased … by 10%" (mononucleation) and "increased by ~9 and ~1%".** Read as percentage points against Fig. 3F readings. A relative change is the alternative.
- **Normalised contraction force.** F/A per cell. F comes from Fig. S5B readings (the same cells in the same order and counts as Fig. 5C); the cell area is never printed, so the derivation cannot be rebuilt. The micropattern island (~20 × 140 μm) is offered as a labelled alternative area. That is my addition, not the paper's.
- **Geometric means.** Contraction force (over 3 contractions) and sarcomere length (over 4 sarcomeres) are geometric means. There is no library entry for one, and the parts are never printed, so I made no proposal.
- **MLC-2v+/MLC-2a+ ratios (S3J, S3O).** Built from the 2v and 2a readings with aggregation *not stated*. The points' pairing across panels is not shown, so a "mean of per-sample" built from reading order may pair the wrong points. In S3J (window 25–30) the ratio panel has 11 points while the 2v and 2a panels have 13.
- **Not recorded as formulas.** qRT-PCR "relative to TBP·POLR2A" (no ΔCt/ΔΔCt stated), western ssTnI/cTnI "stoichiometric relationship" (no denominator stated), T-tubule density (μm²/μm³; quantity not stated) and INa density (pA/pF; capacitance normalisation implied only by the unit). All are `no_formula` with alternatives. Ca²⁺ upstroke/downstroke velocity, time to peak, CV and FPD are software outputs (in-house MATLAB, Cardio 2D) with no stated formula, so they got no derivation. The S5N V½ and k are Boltzmann fits, with no library entry and no curve read.
- **Observation for code.** Table S1 percents and totals and the Fig. 4C sums all have printed parts. These are the only fully stated chains in the paper.
