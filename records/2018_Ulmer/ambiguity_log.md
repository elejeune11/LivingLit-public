## Formula agent

- **No OCR/ECAR ATP calculation in this paper.** Oxidative ATP (Fig. 6G) is computed from 14C-CO2 (metabolized substrate = N/M*K, times 33 / 15 / 127,1 ATP per glucose / lactate / oleic acid); glycolytic ATP from the lactate increase (2 ATP per 2 lactate). The Seahorse trace (Fig. S5C) feeds no calculation.
- **Input radioactivity M is not stated in Bq** (only 0.0037 MBq/ml; the flux assay's volume is not given), so the per-substrate ATP, the 291 / 671 nmol totals and the fatty acid / glucose / lactate shares are written but *cannot check*. Taking M and K as concentrations (volume cancels) and the Fig. 6D–F readings gives oxidative ATP 1,672 nmol (2D) and 3,835 nmol (3D): one common factor of about 5.7 above the printed 291 / 671 (an unstated constant), and shares scaled to the printed totals of FA 37 / glucose 12 / lactate 2 % (2D) and 61 / 18 / 6 % (3D) against printed 40 / 9 / 2 and 65 / 14 / 6. Not written as a derivation (it would rest on my reading of M).
- **'127,1' (decimal comma)** is printed as such; `parse_number` reads it as 1271. Harmless here (the chain cannot rebuild), but it would matter if M were stated.
- **The '+230% / +388% / +214%' (Fig. 6D–F)** are written as relative increases (as printed); code finds all three inconsistent. Read as 3D in percent of 2D they fit, but with glucose and fatty acid swapped: readings give 3D/2D = 2.25 (oleic acid, panel D), 3.85 (lactate, E), 2.14 (glucose, F), so the text's order glucose / lactate / fatty acid carries the panels' D / E / F values. The 'constant factor 0.5' flag code adds to the glucose item is a coincidence of that swap, not a constant-factor error.
- **Glucose consumption and lactate production are worded with the opposite sign**: "initial cGluc … subtracted from cGluc after" is final − initial, and "subtracting cLac after incubation from initial cLac" is initial − final; both would be negative for consumption and production.
