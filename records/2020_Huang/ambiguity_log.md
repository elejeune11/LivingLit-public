## Record reader

- **TDI as `maturation_strategy` or `treatment`.** The paper calls TDI (T3 + IGF-1 + dexamethasone) "chemical conditioning" to mature cells. It fits both fields. I put it in `maturation_strategy` (value "TDI", with each dose as a qualifier, controls "none"). That leaves `treatment` for exposures during a measurement: isoproterenol (F5LM) and blebbistatin (the optical-mapping units). If the vocabulary wants TDI as a treatment set, every unit's groups change together.
- **Mixed figures split by platform.** Fig. 1D, 2I, 3A, 4C and the TEM panels each show monolayers (or single cells) and CMTs, tested separately. I made one unit per platform (`F1D_CM` / `F1D_CMT`, …), so the groups differ only in TDI and all the platform fields sit in the unit. Nothing in the paper compares the two platforms directly. The one exception is "intercalated discs observable in CMTs but not monolayers", which I recorded only as a CMT observation and logged as a cross-reference.
- **Fig. 4F–K significance marks.** Pacing frequencies are not printed except 0.5 Hz (the range start) and 1 Hz (in the text). I placed the asterisks on frequencies by their position over the axis ticks, at 300 dpi. I recorded only the pairs at 0.5 Hz and 1 Hz, and each such item says so in its `note`. Further marks are not recorded. Panel F has marks near 0.7, 1.15 and 1.3 Hz; G near 0.65, 1.2 and 1.3; H near 0.75, 1.15 and 1.35; I near 0.7 and 1.2; J near 1.1, 1.35 and 1.65; K near 0.7 and 1.05. CMT CV (K) has no mark at 0.5 Hz. The text's CV increase is recorded at 1 Hz as a stated direction. A figures reader could add groups for these frequencies.
- **Heatmap (Fig. 3A, S7, S8).** I wrote one pair item per gene per platform. P comes from the asterisks, with S7/S8 as second printings in `p_values`. Genes without a mark have `p: not stated`. Absence of a mark presumably means n.s., but this is not printed. The log2 fold changes are colours only; a figures reader could read them against the colour bar.
- **Text "(p < 0.01)" for ATP2A2 and SLC8A1 "mRNA and protein (Fig. 3A, D)".** I attached it to the protein items (Fig. 3D, where both are **). SLC8A1 mRNA has no mark in either platform, which I note on claim C17.
- **Isoproterenol percentages.** The text gives "increases ... of 360 ± 30%" and "184 ± 6%", but the plots (axis "%", starting at 100) plateau near those numbers. I wrote them as `% change`, as the text words them, with a suspected error on each value. The baseline dose 10−10 M (text) disagrees with the Methods range starting at 10−9 M. That is a suspected error on the items. The "no increase in contractility" for controls names no dose; I recorded it at 10−5 vs 10−10 M. EC50 is kept as an unattached value of the TDI dose–response item. It may count as a model output under the spec; drop it if so.
- **Calcium transients of "monolayers"** (text, Fig. 4L) are dissociated, replated single cells in Table S3 (N = 7 cells). The Day 31 cells' substrate, coating and density are not stated. The Day 21 values (glass slides, Geltrex, 1–2 × 10^4 cells/cm2) were not carried over.
- **Measurement calcium.** 1.8 mM is stated for the Indo-1 Tyrode's and in the Fig. 5L legend. Optical mapping also uses "Tyrode's solution", but I left its calcium as not stated rather than carry the 2.7 composition over.
- **insulin_day.** The B27+ switch at Day 7–9 implies insulin (the paper names B27− "minus insulin"), but no sentence says when insulin is added. I left it not stated.
- **dissociation_reagent.** Cells were dissociated with TrypLE at Day 7–9, and CMs were "trypsinized" at Day 19–21 for CMT generation. I wrote "trypsin" for CMT units and "TrypLE" for monolayers. One field cannot hold both steps.
- **preparation** for live measurements (force, optical mapping, calcium) is not stated. The paper never says "intact", so I wrote no value.
- **Composition conflict.** Methods give 75:10:15 (CM:HUVEC:FB, with 15% fibroblasts and 10% HUVECs). Results give 75:15:10. I recorded 75:10:15 with a suspected error, and a cross-reference to Section 2.3.
- **Supplementary Fig. S2, CD90.** The percentages printed in the gates (96.4–99.7% CD90+) contradict "< 10% CD90+". They probably refer to the fibroblast positive control. Recorded with suspected errors.
- **Supplementary Fig. S3 days** (treatment day 0/4/6) are printed only on the axis, so they are anchored to the unit. Pairs without asterisks (day 0, and day 4 for aspect ratio) are recorded as measurements, not tests.
- **Fig. 5K legend** gives "10 μm isoproterenol" (μm for μM). No item rests on Fig. 5K, so this has no place in the record.
- **Fig. 4C** legend gives N = 4, but fewer control CMT points appear visible. The figures reader should check.
- **Not recorded:** activation maps (Fig. 4A–B, isotropic propagation, no comparison); representative traces (Fig. 4D–E, 4L, 5E, 5K, S11); within-group time courses in Fig. 5B–C (static force rising to Day 18–19, then falling after 25–30 days); Table S1 primers and Table S2 antibodies (methods lists).
- **cell_line** is an unnamed in-house PBMC-derived line (Sendai reprogramming), so I wrote not stated. **genotype** and **disease_model** are also not stated: the paper never calls the line healthy or a control line.

## Vocabulary agent

- **IWR-1 has no ChEBI id.** The pinned ChEBI has only IWR-1-endo (CHEBI:62882) and IWR-1-exo (CHEBI:62883), with no parent class; the paper does not name the isomer, so it is the own term `differentiation_factors:iwr_1` (no xref, to avoid guessing the isomer).
- **"3D cardiac microtissues" → microfabricated tissue gauge.** Mapped from the paper's μTUG device (Methods 2.2), not from the words, which are generic across labs; no synonym added. `axial mechanical load` went to *load regime not stated* (synonym added), not *auxotonic*, although the flexible pillars make the load elastic: filling it from the platform would be inferring.
- **TDI** stays on *maturation medium* with a new qualifier `components` (T3 + dexamethasone + IGF-1), following the existing scope note that hormone-only media stay on the parent. A later paper with T3 + Dex alone would match it at term level; a `hormonal maturation medium` child may be worth adding at the re-map checkpoint.
- **"single-cell culture"** is 2D culture with `cell_contact: single cells`; "plastic culture dishes" is *culture plate* with `format: culture dishes` (the term's definition says multi-well plate).
- **Calcium in nM (Table S3, Indo-1):** "calcium transient amplitude" is matched by code to a term whose definition is dye fluorescence relative to baseline; I stored it with `expressed_as: absolute` so it does not pool with ΔF/F0. "diastolic Ca2+" went to *resting calcium indicator signal* and "peak Ca2+" to a new *peak calcium indicator signal*, both dimensionless with `indicator: Indo-1`, although these values are calibrated concentrations. The calcium measures may need a concentration dimension or a calibrated/raw qualifier.
- **"peak dynamic force"** is printed in mN/mm2, so it is mapped to *twitch stress*, not force. "static stress" and "dynamic stress" are now synonyms of *diastolic stress* and *twitch stress* (Fig. 5A defines static and dynamic force as resting and beat-to-beat pillar deflection).
- **Gene and protein levels:** `expressed_as: fold`. mRNA is normalised to 18S rRNA and untreated controls; protein is normalised to the Coomassie-stained membrane and untreated controls (bars with control at 1). F3A plots log2 fold change. That is still `fold`; the scale is in the values.
- **Flag:** "functional performance" (TXT3_fbhi) is *several measures*: the sentence names no one measure.
- **Possible record issues (not fixed):** S10_MYL7 and S10_MYL2 are named "TNNT2+ MYL7+ fraction" / "TNNT2+ MYL2+ fraction", but their values include all four quadrants (TNNT2+MYL7−, double-negative, …), which are different populations. "fraction of CMTs with organized spontaneous contractions" was mapped to *presence of beating* with a `mode` qualifier, not a new term. "earliest day of electrical capture" counts from differentiation in TXT1 and from formation in TXT2, so the mapping leaves out `reference`.
- **New measures (5, each with a standing key):** onset of electrical capture, time to cessation of beating, peak calcium indicator signal, fraction of events gated as cells, detachment from anchors.

## Formula agent

- **Stress without an area.** Force is stated (F = kδ, δ the mean deflection of the two pillars, k = 0.25 μN/μm), but every force result is printed as stress (Fig. 5B–C, peak dynamic force in mN/mm2). The paper never states the area or how it was measured. The stress steps map to `stress_def` with A `not_stated`. Nothing can be rebuilt, and the stress formula itself is not anchored (`anchor: not stated`).
- **Hooke assumptions.** "linear spring" is reported only as "effective spring constant ... for small deflections". No deflection is printed, so "small deflection" is *not stated*.
- **Indo-1 values in nM.** Methods state only the 405/485 ratio. The conversion to [Ca2+]i (calibration constants) is not stated, so diastolic and peak have `no_formula`. The amplitude follows from "baseline to peak": amp = peak − dia (sum_of_parts). Code rebuilds all six groups of Table S3, all consistent at printed precision.
- **CMT CV** written as ν = Δx/(t2 − t1) to map to `conduction_velocity_def`. Δt is printed as one symbol. Monolayer CV is a Gaussian-fit centre: no formula.
- **Unstated references.** "30% narrower": the reference width is unstated (control assumed; alternative relative to TDI). "Increase of 360 ± 30%" and "184 ± 6%": either a relative change or % of baseline (record's suspected error); both are written as labelled alternatives. "Times longer" (APD) is a ratio of means, as the sentence says.
- **qPCR, Western blots.** Per-set log2 fold change from log-transformed, 18S-normalised data, mapped to `ratio_from_log2_values`. The S7/S8 readings are the per-set log2 ratios, i.e. outputs. A derivation output must be a printed value, so the readings cannot be attached and these 46 derivations have no output. Western "relative protein level" normalisation to control is shown (control bars at 1), not stated.
- **Not written:** the flow-cytometry quadrants (S10, S2) should sum to 100 %, but the paper states no such derivation. Only `fraction_bounds` was applied to each percentage (73 bounds, all consistent).
